# Procedural & Geometry Nodes

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]]

Geometry generated by a graph rather than placed by hand. The payoff is that a change
is a parameter edit rather than a remodel; the cost is that debugging a graph is harder than
debugging a mesh.
