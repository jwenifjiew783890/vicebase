# Lighting

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]]

Lighting decides whether a render reads as real more than modelling or materials do.
A good model badly lit looks bad; a simple model well lit often looks excellent.
