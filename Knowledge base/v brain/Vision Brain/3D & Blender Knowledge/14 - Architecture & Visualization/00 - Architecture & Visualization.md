# Architecture & Visualization

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]]

Buildings and spaces. Architectural visualisation has its own conventions, and the
governing difference from other 3D work is that **it must be true** - the image represents
something that will be built, so accuracy is not artistic licence.
