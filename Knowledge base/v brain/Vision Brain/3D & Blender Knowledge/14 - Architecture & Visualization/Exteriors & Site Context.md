# Exteriors & Site Context

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/14 - Architecture & Visualization/00 - Architecture & Visualization|Architecture & Visualization]]

## What it is

The building in its setting - ground, landscape, neighbours, sky, and the life around it.

## Context is what makes it believable

A building rendered on an empty plane reads as a model of a building. The same building with
ground, planting, neighbouring structures and people reads as a place.

Context also communicates **scale**, which a bare object cannot.

## Ground and site

- Model the actual **topography** if the site slopes - a building sitting on flat ground when the
  site is graded is simply wrong
- **Where the building meets the ground** is the most scrutinised junction in the image: kerbs,
  thresholds, paving, planting beds, drainage
- Paving with real joint patterns and slight irregularity
- The transition from paving to planting to landscape

## Vegetation

Usually the largest cost in an exterior:

- Scatter with logic - see
  [[3D & Blender Knowledge/13 - Environment & Scene Design/Scattering & Set Dressing|Scattering & Set Dressing]]
- Match species and maturity to the location and season
- Trees are large, so silhouette matters more than leaf detail
- Vary size, rotation and species; identical trees are immediately obvious

## Neighbours and background

Surrounding buildings can be simple - massing blocks with plausible materials, lower detail with
distance. They matter enormously for believability and cost little.

Distant background can be a matte or an HDRI, provided it matches the lighting.

## People and vehicles

Divisive but effective. People give scale and life instantly. Used badly - too many, badly cut
out, wrongly lit, obviously stock - they destroy an image faster than any other element.

If used: few, appropriate to the setting, correctly scaled, lit consistently with the scene, often
slightly blurred or in motion.

## Atmosphere

- Slight aerial perspective on distant elements - reduced contrast, shifted toward sky colour
- Subtle volumetric haze where the sun angle allows it
- These cost render time and add a great deal of depth

## Common mistakes

- Building on an empty plane
- Ignoring real topography
- The building/ground junction left unresolved
- Identical scattered trees
- Too many, or badly integrated, people
- Background lighting inconsistent with the scene
