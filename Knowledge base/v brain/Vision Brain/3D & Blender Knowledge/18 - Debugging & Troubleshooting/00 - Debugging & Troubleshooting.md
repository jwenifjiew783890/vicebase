# Debugging & Troubleshooting

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]]

One procedure per failure class. Each follows the same shape - **likely causes,
diagnosis, evidence, safest fix, verification, prevention** - because a symptom list without a
procedure is not useful under pressure.

Start with [[3D & Blender Knowledge/04 - Debugging Method (3D)\|Debugging Method]] for the layer
order. These notes assume you have identified the layer.
