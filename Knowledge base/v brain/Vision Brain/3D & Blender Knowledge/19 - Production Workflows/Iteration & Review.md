# Iteration & Review

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/19 - Production Workflows/00 - Production Workflows|Production Workflows]]

## What it is

Improving work deliberately rather than adjusting it aimlessly.

## See it fresh

The core problem in review is that you stop seeing your own work. The reliable tricks:

- **Flip it horizontally.** Composition and proportion errors become obvious instantly.
- **View it small**, as a thumbnail. Structural problems survive at small size; detail does not.
- **Greyscale.** Judges tonal structure without colour interfering.
- **Leave it overnight.** The cheapest and most effective, when the deadline allows.
- **View it somewhere else** - a different screen, a phone, printed.

## Review against the requirement

Judge against **what the work is for**, not against how much effort went in. Ask:

- Does it meet the brief?
- Does the subject read?
- Is the scale believable?
- Would someone unfamiliar understand what they are looking at?
- What is the single weakest element?

**Fix the weakest element.** Effort spent improving what is already good is invisible; effort
spent on the worst part is immediately visible.

## Taking feedback

- Distinguish **observation** from **prescription**. "It looks flat" is useful information; "add
  another light" is one possible response to it. Solve the observation, not the prescription.
- Ask what problem a request is solving if it seems wrong.
- Record decisions, so the same discussion does not recur.

## Knowing when to stop

Diminishing returns arrive earlier than they feel like they do. Stop when:

- the requirement is met
- remaining changes are not visible at delivery size
- you are adjusting values back and forth rather than forwards

**Fiddling is not iterating.** Iteration has a hypothesis - "the composition is flat, adding a
foreground element will fix it". Fiddling has none.

## Recording what was learned

When something cost real time - a failure mode, a technique that worked, a setting that mattered -
write it down. That is what turns a project into experience rather than just a delivery. In this
vault, that means a note with its provenance recorded.

## Common mistakes

- Reviewing only at 100% zoom, missing structural problems
- Polishing the best part
- Acting on prescriptions rather than the underlying observation
- Never leaving the work
- Fiddling and calling it iteration
- Learning something expensive and not recording it
