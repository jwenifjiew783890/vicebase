# VFX & Compositing

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] > [[3D & Blender Knowledge/00 - 3D & Blender Knowledge|3D & Blender Knowledge]]

Post-render work and live-action integration. Blender ships a full node compositor, a
motion tracker with camera and object solving, a mask editor for rotoscoping, and a video
sequencer - a capability surface that is easy to overlook because most Blender material stops at
rendering.

The practical claim of this section: **a large fraction of what looks like a render problem is
cheaper to fix in the compositor than by re-rendering.**
