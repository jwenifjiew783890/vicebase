# Engineering Experience

Practitioner judgement rather than specification: the heuristics, incident lessons and trade-off instincts that experienced engineers use.

Part of [[Coding Knowledge/00 - Coding Knowledge|Coding & Engineering Knowledge]].

> [!important] Evidence grade for this whole section
> This is **practitioner judgement**, not documented fact. It is what experienced engineers
> tend to do and why, and it is defeasible - a specific situation can justify departing from any
> of it. Do not present a heuristic from this section as though it were a rule from a
> specification. See [[Coding Knowledge/99 - Sources & Provenance|Sources & Provenance]].

The value of this section is that it changes *where an engineer looks first* and *what they
suspect*. That is most of what experience actually is.
