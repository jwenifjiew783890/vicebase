<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

###### 1:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ</div>

> The Most Beneficent, the Most Merciful

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَٰلِكِ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> The Only Owner (and the Only Ruling Judge) of the Day of Recompense (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 1:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِيَّاكَ نَعۡبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسۡتَعِينُ</div>

> You (Alone) we worship, and you (Alone) we ask for help (for each and everything)

###### 1:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ</div>

> Guide us to the Straight Way

###### 1:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غَيۡرِ ٱلۡمَغۡضُوبِ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ</div>

> The Way of those on whom You have bestowed Your Grace, not (the way) of those who earned Your Anger, nor of those who went astray
