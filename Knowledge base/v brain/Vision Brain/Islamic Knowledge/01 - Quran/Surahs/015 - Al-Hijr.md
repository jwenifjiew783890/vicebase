# 15. Al-Hijr — The Rock

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْحِجْرِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 99 · **Juz':** 14

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 15:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">الٓرۚ تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ وَقُرۡءَانࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> Alif-Lam-Ra. [These letters are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings]. These are the Verses of the Book, and a plain Quran

###### 15:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رُّبَمَا يَوَدُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَوۡ كَانُواْ مُسۡلِمِينَ</div>

> Perhaps (often) will those who disbelieve wish that they were Muslims [those who have submitted themselves to Allah's Will in Islam Islamic Monotheism, this will be on the Day of Resurrection when they will see the disbelievers going to Hell and the Muslims going to Paradise]

###### 15:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذَرۡهُمۡ يَأۡكُلُواْ وَيَتَمَتَّعُواْ وَيُلۡهِهِمُ ٱلۡأَمَلُۖ فَسَوۡفَ يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Leave them to eat and enjoy, and let them be preoccupied with (false) hope. They will come to know

###### 15:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَرۡيَةٍ إِلَّا وَلَهَا كِتَابࣱ مَّعۡلُومࣱ‏</div>

> And never did We destroy a township but there was a known decree for it

###### 15:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّا تَسۡبِقُ مِنۡ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلَهَا وَمَا يَسۡتَـٔۡخِرُونَ</div>

> No nation can anticipate its term, nor delay it

###### 15:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِي نُزِّلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلذِّكۡرُ إِنَّكَ لَمَجۡنُونࣱ‏</div>

> And they say: "O you (Muhammad SAW) to whom the Dhikr (the Quran) has been sent down! Verily, you are a mad man

###### 15:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّوۡمَا تَأۡتِينَا بِٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ</div>

> Why do you not bring angels to us if you are of the truthful ones

###### 15:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا نُنَزِّلُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَمَا كَانُوٓاْ إِذࣰ ا مُّنظَرِينَ</div>

> We send not the angels down except with the truth (i.e. for torment, etc.), and in that case, they (the disbelievers) would have no respite

###### 15:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ</div>

> Verily We: It is We Who have sent down the Dhikr (i.e. the Quran) and surely, We will guard it (from corruption)

###### 15:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِكَ فِي شِيَعِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> Indeed, We sent Messengers before you (O Muhammad SAW) amongst the sects (communities) of old

###### 15:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ</div>

> And never came a Messenger to them but they did mock him

###### 15:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَ نَسۡلُكُهُۥ فِي قُلُوبِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> Thus do We let it (polytheism and disbelief) enter into the hearts of the Mujrimun [criminals, polytheists, pagans, etc. (because of their mockery at the Messengers]

###### 15:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِهِۦ وَقَدۡ خَلَتۡ سُنَّةُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> They would not believe in it (the Quran), and already the example of (Allah's punishment of) the ancients (who disbelieved) has gone forth

###### 15:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡ فَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم بَابࣰ ا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَظَلُّواْ فِيهِ يَعۡرُجُونَ</div>

> And even if We opened to them a gate from the heaven and they were to continue ascending thereto

###### 15:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا سُكِّرَتۡ أَبۡصَٰرُنَا بَلۡ نَحۡنُ قَوۡمࣱ مَّسۡحُورُونَ</div>

> They would surely say: "Our eyes have been (as if) dazzled. Nay, we are a people bewitched

###### 15:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ جَعَلۡنَا فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ بُرُوجࣰ ا وَزَيَّنَّٰهَا لِلنَّٰظِرِينَ</div>

> And indeed, We have put the big stars in the heaven and We beautified it for the beholders

###### 15:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحَفِظۡنَٰهَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡطَٰنࣲ رَّجِيمٍ</div>

> And We have guarded it (near heaven) from every outcast Shaitan (devil)

###### 15:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَرَقَ ٱلسَّمۡعَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُۥ شِهَابࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> Except him (devil) that gains hearing by stealing, he is pursued by a clear flaming fire

###### 15:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ مَّوۡزُونࣲ‏</div>

> And the earth We spread out, and placed therein firm mountains, and caused to grow therein all kinds of things in due proportion

###### 15:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا لَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَعَٰيِشَ وَمَن لَّسۡتُمۡ لَهُۥ بِرَٰزِقِينَ</div>

> And We have provided therein means of living, for you and for those whom you provide not [moving (living) creatures, cattle, beasts, and other animals

###### 15:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن مِّن شَيۡءٍ إِلَّا عِندَنَا خَزَآئِنُهُۥ وَمَا نُنَزِّلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا بِقَدَرࣲ مَّعۡلُومࣲ‏</div>

> And there is not a thing, but with Us are the stores thereof. And We send it not down except in a known measure

###### 15:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَرۡسَلۡنَا ٱلرِّيَٰحَ لَوَٰقِحَ فَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءࣰ فَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُمُوهُ وَمَآ أَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ بِخَٰزِنِينَ</div>

> And We send the winds fertilizing (to fill heavily the clouds with water), then caused the water (rain) to descend from the sky, and We gave it to you to drink, and it is not you who are the owners of its stores [i.e. to give water to whom you like or to withhold it from whom you like]

###### 15:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ نُحۡيِۦ وَنُمِيتُ وَنَحۡنُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثُونَ</div>

> And certainly We! We it is Who give life, and cause death, and We are the Inheritors

###### 15:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا ٱلۡمُسۡتَقۡدِمِينَ مِنكُمۡ وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا ٱلۡمُسۡتَـٔۡخِرِينَ</div>

> And indeed, We know the first generations of you who had passed away, and indeed, We know the present generations of you (mankind), and also those who will come afterwards

###### 15:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ يَحۡشُرُهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُۥ حَكِيمٌ عَلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> And verily, your Lord will gather them together. Truly, He is All-Wise, All-Knowing

###### 15:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن صَلۡصَٰلࣲ مِّنۡ حَمَإࣲ مَّسۡنُونࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed, We created man from sounding clay of altered black smooth mud

###### 15:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡجَآنَّ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن قَبۡلُ مِن نَّارِ ٱلسَّمُومِ</div>

> And the jinn, We created aforetime from the smokeless flame of fire

###### 15:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي خَٰلِقُۢ بَشَرࣰ ا مِّن صَلۡصَٰلࣲ مِّنۡ حَمَإࣲ مَّسۡنُونࣲ‏</div>

> And (remember) when your Lord said to the angels: "I am going to create a man (Adam) from sounding clay of altered black smooth mud

###### 15:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا سَوَّيۡتُهُۥ وَنَفَخۡتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُواْ لَهُۥ سَٰجِدِينَ</div>

> So, when I have fashioned him completely and breathed into him (Adam) the soul which I created for him, then fall (you) down prostrating yourselves unto him

###### 15:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَجَدَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ كُلُّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعُونَ</div>

> So, the angels prostrated themselves, all of them together

###### 15:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ أَبَىٰٓ أَن يَكُونَ مَعَ ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ</div>

> Except Iblis (Satan), - he refused to be among the prostrators

###### 15:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ يَٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا لَكَ أَلَّا تَكُونَ مَعَ ٱلسَّٰجِدِينَ</div>

> (Allah) said: "O Iblis (Satan)! What is your reason for not being among the prostrators

###### 15:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لَمۡ أَكُن لِّأَسۡجُدَ لِبَشَرٍ خَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن صَلۡصَٰلࣲ مِّنۡ حَمَإࣲ مَّسۡنُونࣲ‏</div>

> Iblis (Satan)] said: "I am not the one to prostrate myself to a human being, whom You created from sounding clay of altered black smooth mud

###### 15:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ مِنۡهَا فَإِنَّكَ رَجِيمࣱ‏</div>

> (Allah) said: "Then, get out from here, for verily, you are Rajim (an outcast or a cursed one)." [Tafsir At-Tabari]

###### 15:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكَ ٱللَّعۡنَةَ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> And verily, the curse shall be upon you till the Day of Recompense (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 15:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ فَأَنظِرۡنِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ</div>

> Iblis (Satan)] said: "O my Lord! Give me then respite till the Day they (the dead) will be resurrected

###### 15:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَإِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنظَرِينَ</div>

> Allah said: "Then, verily, you are of those reprieved

###### 15:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡوَقۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡلُومِ</div>

> Till the Day of the time appointed

###### 15:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ بِمَآ أَغۡوَيۡتَنِي لَأُزَيِّنَنَّ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ</div>

> Iblis (Satan)] said: "O my Lord! Because you misled me, I shall indeed adorn the path of error for them (mankind) on the earth, and I shall mislead them all

###### 15:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ</div>

> Except Your chosen, (guided) slaves among them

###### 15:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٌ عَلَيَّ مُسۡتَقِيمٌ</div>

> (Allah) said: "This is the Way which will lead straight to Me

###### 15:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ عِبَادِي لَيۡسَ لَكَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سُلۡطَٰنٌ إِلَّا مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَكَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَاوِينَ</div>

> Certainly, you shall have no authority over My slaves, except those who follow you of the Ghawin (Mushrikun and those who go astray, criminals, polytheists, and evil-doers, etc)

###### 15:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ لَمَوۡعِدُهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ</div>

> And surely, Hell is the promised place for them all

###### 15:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَهَا سَبۡعَةُ أَبۡوَٰبࣲ لِّكُلِّ بَابࣲ مِّنۡهُمۡ جُزۡءࣱ مَّقۡسُومٌ</div>

> It (Hell) has seven gates, for each of those gates is a (special) class (of sinners) assigned

###### 15:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ وَعُيُونٍ</div>

> Truly! The Muttaqun (pious and righteous persons - see V. 2:2) will be amidst Gardens and water-springs (Paradise)

###### 15:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمٍ ءَامِنِينَ</div>

> (It will be said to them): 'Enter therein (Paradise), in peace and security

###### 15:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلٍّ إِخۡوَٰنًا عَلَىٰ سُرُرࣲ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ</div>

> And We shall remove from their breasts any sense of injury (that they may have), (So they will be like) brothers facing each other on thrones

###### 15:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا يَمَسُّهُمۡ فِيهَا نَصَبࣱ وَمَا هُم مِّنۡهَا بِمُخۡرَجِينَ</div>

> No sense of fatigue shall touch them, nor shall they (ever) be asked to leave it

###### 15:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞نَبِّئۡ عِبَادِيٓ أَنِّيٓ أَنَا ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ</div>

> Declare (O Muhammad SAW) unto My slaves, that truly, I am the Oft-Forgiving, the Most-Merciful

###### 15:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّ عَذَابِي هُوَ ٱلۡعَذَابُ ٱلۡأَلِيمُ</div>

> And that My Torment is indeed the most painful torment

###### 15:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَبِّئۡهُمۡ عَن ضَيۡفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ</div>

> And tell them about the guests (the angels) of Ibrahim (Abraham)

###### 15:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ فَقَالُواْ سَلَٰمࣰ ا قَالَ إِنَّا مِنكُمۡ وَجِلُونَ</div>

> When they entered unto him, and said: Salaman (peace)! [Ibrahim (Abraham)] said: "Indeed! We are afraid of you

###### 15:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ لَا تَوۡجَلۡ إِنَّا نُبَشِّرُكَ بِغُلَٰمٍ عَلِيمࣲ‏</div>

> They (the angels) said: "Do not be afraid! We give you glad tidings of a boy (son) possessing much knowledge and wisdom

###### 15:54

> (All) except the family of Lout (Lot). Them all we are surely going to save (from destruction)

> Except his wife, of whom We have decreed that she shall be of those who remain behind (i.e. she will be destroyed)

> Then, when the Messengers (the angels) came unto the family of Lout (Lot)

> He said: "Verily! You are people unknown to me

###### 15:63
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ بَلۡ جِئۡنَٰكَ بِمَا كَانُواْ فِيهِ يَمۡتَرُونَ</div>

> They said: "Nay, we have come to you with that (torment) which they have been doubting

###### 15:64
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَتَيۡنَٰكَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِنَّا لَصَٰدِقُونَ</div>

> And we have brought to you the truth (the news of the destruction of your nation) and certainly, we tell the truth

###### 15:65

> And We made known this decree to him, that the root of those (sinners) was to be cut off in the early morning

###### 15:68

> Lout (Lot)] said: "Verily! these are my guests, so shame me not

###### 15:69

> And fear Allah and disgrace me not

> So As-Saihah (torment - awful cry, etc.) overtook them at the time of sunrise

> Surely! In this are signs, for those who see (or understand or learn the lessons from the Signs of Allah)

###### 15:76

> Surely! Therein is indeed a sign for the believers

> And We gave them Our Signs, but they were averse to them

###### 15:82

> And worship your Lord until there comes unto you the certainty (i.e. death)
