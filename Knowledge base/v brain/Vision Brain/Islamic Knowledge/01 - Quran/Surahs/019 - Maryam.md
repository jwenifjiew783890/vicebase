# 19. Maryam — Mary

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ مَرْيَمَ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 98 · **Juz':** 16 · **Sajda at:** 58

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.

###### 19:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كٓهيعٓصٓ</div>

> Kaf- Ha-Ya-'Ain-Sad. [These letters are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings

###### 19:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذِكۡرُ رَحۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبۡدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّآ</div>

> (This is) a mention of the mercy of your Lord to His slave Zakariya (Zachariah)

###### 19:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ نِدَآءً خَفِيࣰّ ا</div>

> When he called out his Lord (Allah) a call in secret

###### 19:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي وَهَنَ ٱلۡعَظۡمُ مِنِّي وَٱشۡتَعَلَ ٱلرَّأۡسُ شَيۡبࣰ ا وَلَمۡ أَكُنۢ بِدُعَآئِكَ رَبِّ شَقِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Saying: "My Lord! Indeed my bones have grown feeble, and grey hair has spread on my head, And I have never been unblest in my invocation to You, O my Lord

###### 19:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنِّي خِفۡتُ ٱلۡمَوَٰلِيَ مِن وَرَآءِي وَكَانَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتِي عَاقِرࣰ ا فَهَبۡ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ وَلِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And Verily! I fear my relatives after me, since my wife is barren. So give me from Yourself an heir

###### 19:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَرِثُنِي وَيَرِثُ مِنۡ ءَالِ يَعۡقُوبَۖ وَٱجۡعَلۡهُ رَبِّ رَضِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Who shall inherit me, and inherit (also) the posterity of Ya'qub (Jacob) (inheritance of the religious knowledge and Prophethood, not the wealth, etc.). And make him, my Lord, one with whom You are Well-pleased

###### 19:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰزَكَرِيَّآ إِنَّا نُبَشِّرُكَ بِغُلَٰمٍ ٱسۡمُهُۥ يَحۡيَىٰ لَمۡ نَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ مِن قَبۡلُ سَمِيࣰّ ا</div>

> (Allah said) "O Zakariya (Zachariah)! Verily, We give you the glad tidings of a son, His name will be Yahya (John). We have given that name to none before (him)

###### 19:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي غُلَٰمࣱ وَكَانَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتِي عَاقِرࣰ ا وَقَدۡ بَلَغۡتُ مِنَ ٱلۡكِبَرِ عِتِيࣰّ ا</div>

> He said: "My Lord! How can I have a son, when my wife is barren, and I have reached the extreme old age

###### 19:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ كَذَٰلِكَ قَالَ رَبُّكَ هُوَ عَلَيَّ هَيِّنࣱ وَقَدۡ خَلَقۡتُكَ مِن قَبۡلُ وَلَمۡ تَكُ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا</div>

> He said: "So (it will be). Your Lord says; It is easy for Me. Certainly I have created you before, when you had been nothing

###### 19:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ ٱجۡعَل لِّيٓ ءَايَةࣰۖ قَالَ ءَايَتُكَ أَلَّا تُكَلِّمَ ٱلنَّاسَ ثَلَٰثَ لَيَالࣲ سَوِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Zakariya (Zachariah)] said: "My Lord! Appoint for me a sign." He said: "Your sign is that you shall not speak unto mankind for three nights, though having no bodily defect

###### 19:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَخَرَجَ عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلۡمِحۡرَابِ فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ أَن سَبِّحُواْ بُكۡرَةࣰ وَعَشِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Then he came out to his people from Al-Mihrab (a praying place or a private room, etc.), he told them by signs to glorify Allah's Praises in the morning and in the afternoon

###### 19:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰيَحۡيَىٰ خُذِ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِقُوَّةࣲۖ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡحُكۡمَ صَبِيࣰّ ا</div>

> (It was said to his son): "O Yahya (John)! Hold fast the Scripture [the Taurat (Torah)]." And We gave him wisdom while yet a child

###### 19:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحَنَانࣰ ا مِّن لَّدُنَّا وَزَكَوٰةࣰۖ وَكَانَ تَقِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And (made him) sympathetic to men as a mercy (or a grant) from Us, and pure from sins [i.e. Yahya (John)] and he was righteous

###### 19:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبَرَّۢا بِوَٰلِدَيۡهِ وَلَمۡ يَكُن جَبَّارًا عَصِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And dutiful towards his parents, and he was neither an arrogant nor disobedient (to Allah or to his parents)

###### 19:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَسَلَٰمٌ عَلَيۡهِ يَوۡمَ وُلِدَ وَيَوۡمَ يَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ يُبۡعَثُ حَيࣰّ ا</div>

> So Peace on him the day he was born the day that he dies and the day that he will be raised up to life (again)

###### 19:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مَرۡيَمَ إِذِ ٱنتَبَذَتۡ مِنۡ أَهۡلِهَا مَكَانࣰ ا شَرۡقِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And mention in the Book (the Quran, O Muhammad SAW, the story of) Maryam (Mary), when she withdrew in seclusion from her family to a place facing east

###### 19:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱتَّخَذَتۡ مِن دُونِهِمۡ حِجَابࣰ ا فَأَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهَا رُوحَنَا فَتَمَثَّلَ لَهَا بَشَرࣰ ا سَوِيࣰّ ا</div>

> She placed a screen (to screen herself) from them; then We sent to her Our Ruh [angel Jibrael (Gabriel)], and he appeared before her in the form of a man in all respects

###### 19:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَتۡ إِنِّيٓ أَعُوذُ بِٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِنكَ إِن كُنتَ تَقِيࣰّ ا</div>

> She said: "Verily! I seek refuge with the Most Beneficent (Allah) from you, if you do fear Allah

###### 19:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ رَسُولُ رَبِّكِ لِأَهَبَ لَكِ غُلَٰمࣰ ا زَكِيࣰّ ا</div>

> (The angel) said: "I am only a Messenger from your Lord, (to announce) to you the gift of a righteous son

###### 19:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَتۡ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي غُلَٰمࣱ وَلَمۡ يَمۡسَسۡنِي بَشَرࣱ وَلَمۡ أَكُ بَغِيࣰّ ا</div>

> She said: "How can I have a son, when no man has touched me, nor am I unchaste

###### 19:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِ هُوَ عَلَيَّ هَيِّنࣱۖ وَلِنَجۡعَلَهُۥٓ ءَايَةࣰ لِّلنَّاسِ وَرَحۡمَةࣰ مِّنَّاۚ وَكَانَ أَمۡرࣰ ا مَّقۡضِيࣰّ ا</div>

> He said: "So (it will be), your Lord said: 'That is easy for Me (Allah): And (We wish) to appoint him as a sign to mankind and a mercy from Us (Allah), and it is a matter (already) decreed, (by Allah). 

###### 19:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞فَحَمَلَتۡهُ فَٱنتَبَذَتۡ بِهِۦ مَكَانࣰ ا قَصِيࣰّ ا</div>

> So she conceived him, and she withdrew with him to a far place (i.e. Bethlehem valley about 4-6 miles from Jerusalem)

###### 19:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَجَآءَهَا ٱلۡمَخَاضُ إِلَىٰ جِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ قَالَتۡ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي مِتُّ قَبۡلَ هَٰذَا وَكُنتُ نَسۡيࣰ ا مَّنسِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And the pains of childbirth drove her to the trunk of a date-palm. She said: "Would that I had died before this, and had been forgotten and out of sight

###### 19:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَنَادَىٰهَا مِن تَحۡتِهَآ أَلَّا تَحۡزَنِي قَدۡ جَعَلَ رَبُّكِ تَحۡتَكِ سَرِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Then [the babe 'Iesa (Jesus) or Jibrael (Gabriel)] cried unto her from below her, saying: "Grieve not! Your Lord has provided a water stream under you

###### 19:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُزِّيٓ إِلَيۡكِ بِجِذۡعِ ٱلنَّخۡلَةِ تُسَٰقِطۡ عَلَيۡكِ رُطَبࣰ ا جَنِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And shake the trunk of date-palm towards you, it will let fall fresh ripe-dates upon you

###### 19:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكُلِي وَٱشۡرَبِي وَقَرِّي عَيۡنࣰ اۖ فَإِمَّا تَرَيِنَّ مِنَ ٱلۡبَشَرِ أَحَدࣰ ا فَقُولِيٓ إِنِّي نَذَرۡتُ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ صَوۡمࣰ ا فَلَنۡ أُكَلِّمَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ إِنسِيࣰّ ا</div>

> So eat and drink and be glad, and if you see any human being, say: 'Verily! I have vowed a fast unto the Most Beneficent (Allah) so I shall not speak to any human being this day. 

###### 19:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَتَتۡ بِهِۦ قَوۡمَهَا تَحۡمِلُهُۥۖ قَالُواْ يَٰمَرۡيَمُ لَقَدۡ جِئۡتِ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا فَرِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Then she brought him (the baby) to her people, carrying him. They said: "O Mary! Indeed you have brought a thing Fariya (an unheard mighty thing)

###### 19:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأُخۡتَ هَٰرُونَ مَا كَانَ أَبُوكِ ٱمۡرَأَ سَوۡءࣲ وَمَا كَانَتۡ أُمُّكِ بَغِيࣰّ ا</div>

> O sister (i.e. the like) of Harun (Aaron) [not the brother of Musa (Moses), but he was another pious man at the time of Maryam (Mary)]! Your father was not a man who used to commit adultery, nor your mother was an unchaste woman

###### 19:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَشَارَتۡ إِلَيۡهِۖ قَالُواْ كَيۡفَ نُكَلِّمُ مَن كَانَ فِي ٱلۡمَهۡدِ صَبِيࣰّ ا</div>

> Then she pointed to him. They said: "How can we talk to one who is a child in the cradle

###### 19:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ إِنِّي عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِيَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِي نَبِيࣰّ ا</div>

> He ['Iesa (Jesus)] said: Verily! I am a slave of Allah, He has given me the Scripture and made me a Prophet

###### 19:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلَنِي مُبَارَكًا أَيۡنَ مَا كُنتُ وَأَوۡصَٰنِي بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱلزَّكَوٰةِ مَا دُمۡتُ حَيࣰّ ا</div>

> And He has made me blessed wheresoever I be, and has enjoined on me Salat (prayer), and Zakat, as long as I live

###### 19:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبَرَّۢا بِوَٰلِدَتِي وَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡنِي جَبَّارࣰ ا شَقِيࣰّ ا</div>

> And dutiful to my mother, and made me not arrogant, unblest

###### 19:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَيَّ يَوۡمَ وُلِدتُّ وَيَوۡمَ أَمُوتُ وَيَوۡمَ أُبۡعَثُ حَيࣰّ ا</div>

> And Salam (peace) be upon me the day I was born, and the day I die, and the day I shall be raised alive

###### 19:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذَٰلِكَ عِيسَى ٱبۡنُ مَرۡيَمَۖ قَوۡلَ ٱلۡحَقِّ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ يَمۡتَرُونَ</div>

> Such is 'Iesa (Jesus), son of Maryam (Mary). (it is) a statement of truth, about which they doubt (or dispute)

###### 19:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا كَانَ لِلَّهِ أَن يَتَّخِذَ مِن وَلَدࣲۖ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥٓۚ إِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمۡرࣰ ا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ</div>

> It befits not (the Majesty of) Allah that He should beget a son [this refers to the slander of Christians against Allah, by saying that 'Iesa (Jesus) is the son of Allah]. Glorified (and Exalted be He above all that they associate with Him). When He decrees a thing, He only says to it, "Be!" and it is

###### 19:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطࣱ مُّسۡتَقِيمࣱ‏</div>

> Iesa (Jesus) said]: "And verily Allah is my Lord and your Lord. So worship Him (Alone). That is the Straight Path. (Allah's Religion of Islamic Monotheism which He did ordain for all of His Prophets)." [Tafsir At-Tabari]

###### 19:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱخۡتَلَفَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِهِمۡۖ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن مَّشۡهَدِ يَوۡمٍ عَظِيمٍ</div>

> Then the sects differed [i.e. the Christians about 'Iesa (Jesus)], so woe unto the disbelievers [those who gave false witness by saying that 'Iesa (Jesus) is the son of Allah] from the meeting of a great Day (i.e. the Day of Resurrection, when they will be thrown in the blazing Fire)

###### 19:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَسۡمِعۡ بِهِمۡ وَأَبۡصِرۡ يَوۡمَ يَأۡتُونَنَا لَٰكِنِ ٱلظَّٰلِمُونَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> How clearly will they (polytheists and disbelievers in the Oneness of Allah) see and hear, the Day when they will appear before Us! But the Zalimun (polytheists and wrong-doers) today are in plain error

###### 19:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنذِرۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡحَسۡرَةِ إِذۡ قُضِيَ ٱلۡأَمۡرُ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةࣲ وَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> And warn them (O Muhammad SAW) of the Day of grief and regrets, when the case has been decided, while (now) they are in a state of carelessness, and they believe not

###### 19:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَرِثُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَنۡ عَلَيۡهَا وَإِلَيۡنَا يُرۡجَعُونَ</div>

> Verily! We will inherit the earth and whatsoever is thereon. And to Us they all shall be returned

###### 19:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرۡ فِي ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صِدِّيقࣰ ا نَّبِيًّا</div>

> And mention in the Book (the Quran) Ibrahim (Abraham). Verily! He was a man of truth, a Prophet

###### 19:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ قَالَ لِأَبِيهِ يَٰٓأَبَتِ لِمَ تَعۡبُدُ مَا لَا يَسۡمَعُ وَلَا يُبۡصِرُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي عَنكَ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا</div>

> When he said to his father: "O my father! Why do you worship that which hears not, sees not and cannot avail you in anything

###### 19:43
