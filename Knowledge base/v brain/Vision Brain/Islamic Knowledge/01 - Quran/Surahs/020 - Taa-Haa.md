# 20. Taa-Haa — Taa-Haa

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ طٰهٰ</div>

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.

###### 20:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">طه</div>

> Ta-Ha. [These letters are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings

###### 20:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِتَشۡقَىٰٓ</div>

> We have not sent down the Quran unto you (O Muhammad SAW) to cause you distress

###### 20:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا تَذۡكِرَةࣰ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰ</div>

> But only as a Reminder to those who fear (Allah)

###### 20:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَنزِيلࣰ ا مِّمَّنۡ خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلۡعُلَى</div>

> A revelation from Him (Allah) Who has created the earth and high heavens

###### 20:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ عَلَى ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱسۡتَوَىٰ</div>

> The Most Beneficent (Allah) Istawa (rose over) the (Mighty) Throne (in a manner that suits His Majesty)

###### 20:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَمَا تَحۡتَ ٱلثَّرَىٰ</div>

> To Him belongs all that is in the heavens and all that is on the earth, and all that is between them, and all that is under the soil

###### 20:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن تَجۡهَرۡ بِٱلۡقَوۡلِ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلسِّرَّ وَأَخۡفَى</div>

> And if you (O Muhammad SAW) speak (the invocation) aloud, then verily, He knows the secret and that which is yet more hidden

###### 20:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۖ لَهُ ٱلۡأَسۡمَآءُ ٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ</div>

> Allah! La ilahla illa Huwa (none has the right to be worshipped but He)! To Him belong the Best Names

###### 20:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ</div>

> And has there come to you the story of Musa (Moses)

###### 20:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ رَءَا نَارࣰ ا فَقَالَ لِأَهۡلِهِ ٱمۡكُثُوٓاْ إِنِّيٓ ءَانَسۡتُ نَارࣰ ا لَّعَلِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم مِّنۡهَا بِقَبَسٍ أَوۡ أَجِدُ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ هُدࣰ ى</div>

> When he saw a fire, he said to his family: "Wait! Verily, I have seen a fire, perhaps I can bring you some burning brand therefrom, or find some guidance at the fire

###### 20:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِيَ يَٰمُوسَىٰٓ</div>

> And when he came to it (the fire), he was called by name: "O Musa (Moses)

###### 20:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنِّيٓ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكَ فَٱخۡلَعۡ نَعۡلَيۡكَ إِنَّكَ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوࣰ ى</div>

> Verily! I am your Lord! So take off your shoes, you are in the sacred valley, Tuwa

###### 20:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَا ٱخۡتَرۡتُكَ فَٱسۡتَمِعۡ لِمَا يُوحَىٰٓ</div>

> And I have chosen you. So listen to that which is inspired to you

###### 20:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّنِيٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنَا۠ فَٱعۡبُدۡنِي وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِذِكۡرِيٓ</div>

> Verily! I am Allah! La ilaha illa Ana (none has the right to be worshipped but I), so worship Me, and perform As-Salat (Iqamat-as-Salat) for My Remembrance

###### 20:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ ءَاتِيَةٌ أَكَادُ أُخۡفِيهَا لِتُجۡزَىٰ كُلُّ نَفۡسِۭ بِمَا تَسۡعَىٰ</div>

> Verily, the Hour is coming and My Will is to keep it hidden that every person may be rewarded for that which he strives

###### 20:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَا يَصُدَّنَّكَ عَنۡهَا مَن لَّا يُؤۡمِنُ بِهَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ فَتَرۡدَىٰ</div>

> Therefore, let not the one who believes not therein (i.e. in the Day of Resurrection, Reckoning, Paradise and Hell, etc.), but follows his own lusts, divert you therefrom, lest you perish

###### 20:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا تِلۡكَ بِيَمِينِكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ</div>

> And what is that in your right hand, O Musa (Moses)

###### 20:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ هِيَ عَصَايَ أَتَوَكَّؤُاْ عَلَيۡهَا وَأَهُشُّ بِهَا عَلَىٰ غَنَمِي وَلِيَ فِيهَا مَـَٔارِبُ أُخۡرَىٰ</div>

> He said: "This is my stick, whereon I lean, and wherewith I beat down branches for my sheep, and wherein I find other uses

###### 20:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ أَلۡقِهَا يَٰمُوسَىٰ</div>

> (Allah) said: "Cast it down, O Musa (Moses)

###### 20:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَلۡقَىٰهَا فَإِذَا هِيَ حَيَّةࣱ تَسۡعَىٰ</div>

> He cast it down, and behold! It was a snake, moving quickly

###### 20:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ خُذۡهَا وَلَا تَخَفۡۖ سَنُعِيدُهَا سِيرَتَهَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> Allah said:"Grasp it, and fear not, We shall return it to its former state

###### 20:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱضۡمُمۡ يَدَكَ إِلَىٰ جَنَاحِكَ تَخۡرُجۡ بَيۡضَآءَ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ سُوٓءٍ ءَايَةً أُخۡرَىٰ</div>

> And press your (right) hand to your (left) side, it will come forth white (and shining), without any disease as another sign

###### 20:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِنُرِيَكَ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِنَا ٱلۡكُبۡرَى</div>

> That We may show you (some) of Our Greater Signs

###### 20:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱذۡهَبۡ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ</div>

> Go to Fir'aun (Pharaoh)! Verily, he has transgressed (all bounds in disbelief and disobedience, and has behaved as an arrogant, and as a tyrant)

###### 20:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشۡرَحۡ لِي صَدۡرِي</div>

> Musa (Moses)] said: "O my Lord! Open for me my chest (grant me self-confidence, contentment, and boldness)

###### 20:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَسِّرۡ لِيٓ أَمۡرِي</div>

> And ease my task for me

###### 20:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱحۡلُلۡ عُقۡدَةࣰ مِّن لِّسَانِي</div>

> And make loose the knot (the defect) from my tongue, (i.e. remove the incorrectness from my speech) [That occurred as a result of a brand of fire which Musa (Moses) put in his mouth when he was an infant]. [Tafsir At-Tabari, Vol. 16, Page]

###### 20:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَفۡقَهُواْ قَوۡلِي</div>

> That they understand my speech

###### 20:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱجۡعَل لِّي وَزِيرࣰ ا مِّنۡ أَهۡلِي</div>

> And appoint for me a helper from my family

###### 20:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰرُونَ أَخِي</div>

> Harun (Aaron), my brother

###### 20:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱشۡدُدۡ بِهِۦٓ أَزۡرِي</div>

> Increase my strength with him

###### 20:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَشۡرِكۡهُ فِيٓ أَمۡرِي</div>

> And let him share my task (of conveying Allah's Message and Prophethood)

###### 20:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَيۡ نُسَبِّحَكَ كَثِيرࣰ ا</div>

> That we may glorify You much

###### 20:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَذۡكُرَكَ كَثِيرًا</div>

> And remember You much

###### 20:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّكَ كُنتَ بِنَا بَصِيرࣰ ا</div>

> Verily! You are of us Ever a Well-Seer

###### 20:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ قَدۡ أُوتِيتَ سُؤۡلَكَ يَٰمُوسَىٰ</div>

> Allah said: "You are granted your request, O Musa (Moses)

###### 20:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ مَنَنَّا عَلَيۡكَ مَرَّةً أُخۡرَىٰٓ</div>

> And indeed We conferred a favour on you another time (before)

###### 20:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ أَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّكَ مَا يُوحَىٰٓ</div>

> When We inspired your mother with that which We inspired

###### 20:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنِ ٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلتَّابُوتِ فَٱقۡذِفِيهِ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ فَلۡيُلۡقِهِ ٱلۡيَمُّ بِٱلسَّاحِلِ يَأۡخُذۡهُ عَدُوࣱّ لِّي وَعَدُوࣱّ لَّهُۥۚ وَأَلۡقَيۡتُ عَلَيۡكَ مَحَبَّةࣰ مِّنِّي وَلِتُصۡنَعَ عَلَىٰ عَيۡنِيٓ</div>

> Saying: 'Put him (the child) into the Tabut (a box or a case or a chest) and put it into the river (Nile), then the river shall cast it up on the bank, and there, an enemy of Mine and an enemy of his shall take him.' And I endued you with love from Me, in order that you may be brought up under My Eye

###### 20:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ تَمۡشِيٓ أُخۡتُكَ فَتَقُولُ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَن يَكۡفُلُهُۥۖ فَرَجَعۡنَٰكَ إِلَىٰٓ أُمِّكَ كَيۡ تَقَرَّ عَيۡنُهَا وَلَا تَحۡزَنَۚ وَقَتَلۡتَ نَفۡسࣰ ا فَنَجَّيۡنَٰكَ مِنَ ٱلۡغَمِّ وَفَتَنَّٰكَ فُتُونࣰ اۚ فَلَبِثۡتَ سِنِينَ فِيٓ أَهۡلِ مَدۡيَنَ ثُمَّ جِئۡتَ عَلَىٰ قَدَرࣲ يَٰمُوسَىٰ</div>

> When your sister went and said: 'Shall I show you one who will nurse him?' So We restored you to your mother, that she might cool her eyes and she should not grieve. Then you did kill a man, but We saved you from great distress and tried you with a heavy trial. Then you stayed a number of years with the people of Madyan (Midian). Then you came here according to the fixed term which I ordained (for you), O Musa (Moses)

###### 20:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱصۡطَنَعۡتُكَ لِنَفۡسِي</div>

> And I have Istana'tuka, for Myself

###### 20:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱذۡهَبۡ أَنتَ وَأَخُوكَ بِـَٔايَٰتِي وَلَا تَنِيَا فِي ذِكۡرِي</div>

> Go you and your brother with My Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.), and do not, you both, slacken and become weak in My Remembrance

###### 20:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱذۡهَبَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ</div>

> Go, both of you, to Fir'aun (Pharaoh), verily, he has transgressed (all bounds in disbelief and disobedience and behaved as an arrogant and as a tyrant)

###### 20:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقُولَا لَهُۥ قَوۡلࣰ ا لَّيِّنࣰ ا لَّعَلَّهُۥ يَتَذَكَّرُ أَوۡ يَخۡشَىٰ</div>

> And speak to him mildly, perhaps he may accept admonition or fear Allah

###### 20:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَا رَبَّنَآ إِنَّنَا نَخَافُ أَن يَفۡرُطَ عَلَيۡنَآ أَوۡ أَن يَطۡغَىٰ</div>

> They said: "Our Lord! Verily! We fear lest he should hasten to punish us or lest he should transgress (all bounds against us)

###### 20:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لَا تَخَافَآۖ إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَآ أَسۡمَعُ وَأَرَىٰ</div>

> He (Allah) said: "Fear not, verily! I am with you both, hearing and seeing

###### 20:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأۡتِيَاهُ فَقُولَآ إِنَّا رَسُولَا رَبِّكَ فَأَرۡسِلۡ مَعَنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ وَلَا تُعَذِّبۡهُمۡۖ قَدۡ جِئۡنَٰكَ بِـَٔايَةࣲ مِّن رَّبِّكَۖ وَٱلسَّلَٰمُ عَلَىٰ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ</div>

> So go you both to him, and say: 'Verily, we are Messengers of your Lord, so let the Children of Israel go with us, and torment them not; indeed, we have come with a sign from your Lord! And peace will be upon him who follows the guidance

###### 20:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا قَدۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيۡنَآ أَنَّ ٱلۡعَذَابَ عَلَىٰ مَن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ</div>

> Truly, it has been revealed to us that the torment will be for him who denies [believes not in the Oneness of Allah, and in His Messengers, etc.], and turns away.'(from the truth and obedience of Allah)

###### 20:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَمَن رَّبُّكُمَا يَٰمُوسَىٰ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh) said: "Who then, O Musa (Moses), is the Lord of you two

###### 20:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبُّنَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَعۡطَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلۡقَهُۥ ثُمَّ هَدَىٰ</div>

> Musa (Moses)] said: "Our Lord is He Who gave to each thing its form and nature, then guided it aright

###### 20:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَمَا بَالُ ٱلۡقُرُونِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh)] said: "What about the generations of old

###### 20:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ عِلۡمُهَا عِندَ رَبِّي فِي كِتَٰبࣲۖ لَّا يَضِلُّ رَبِّي وَلَا يَنسَى</div>

> Musa (Moses)] said: "The knowledge thereof is with my Lord, in a Record. My Lord is neither unaware nor He forgets

###### 20:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَهۡدࣰ ا وَسَلَكَ لَكُمۡ فِيهَا سُبُلࣰ ا وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءࣰ فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجࣰ ا مِّن نَّبَاتࣲ شَتَّىٰ</div>

> Who has made earth for you like a bed (spread out); and has opened roads (ways and paths etc.) for you therein; and has sent down water (rain) from the sky. And We have brought forth with it various kinds of vegetation

###### 20:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كُلُواْ وَٱرۡعَوۡاْ أَنۡعَٰمَكُمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتࣲ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلنُّهَىٰ</div>

> Eat and pasture your cattle, (therein); verily, in this are proofs and signs for men of understanding

###### 20:55

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ بَصُرۡتُ بِمَا لَمۡ يَبۡصُرُواْ بِهِۦ فَقَبَضۡتُ قَبۡضَةࣰ مِّنۡ أَثَرِ ٱلرَّسُولِ فَنَبَذۡتُهَا وَكَذَٰلِكَ سَوَّلَتۡ لِي نَفۡسِي</div>

> (Samiri) said: "I saw what they saw not, so I took a handful (of dust) from the hoof print of the messenger [Jibrael's (Gabriel) horse] and threw it [into the fire in which were put the ornaments of the Fir'aun's (Pharaoh) people, or into the calf]. Thus my inner-self suggested to me

> Musa (Moses) said: "Then go away! And verily, your (punishment) in this life will be that you will say: "Touch me not (i.e. you will live alone exiled away from mankind); and verily (for a future torment), you have a promise that will not fail. And look at your ilah (god), to which you have been devoted. We will certainly burn it, and scatter its particles in the sea

###### 20:98
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَآ إِلَٰهُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَسِعَ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عِلۡمࣰ ا</div>

> Your Ilah (God) is only Allah, the One (La ilaha illa Huwa) (none has the right to be worshipped but He). He has full knowledge of all things

###### 20:99
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَ نَقُصُّ عَلَيۡكَ مِنۡ أَنۢبَآءِ مَا قَدۡ سَبَقَۚ وَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَٰكَ مِن لَّدُنَّا ذِكۡرࣰ ا</div>

> Thus We relate to you (O Muhammad SAW) some information of what happened before. And indeed We have given you from Us a Reminder (this Quran)

###### 20:100
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّنۡ أَعۡرَضَ عَنۡهُ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَحۡمِلُ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ وِزۡرًا</div>

> Whoever turns away from it (this Quran i.e. does not believe in it, nor acts on its orders), verily, they will bear a heavy burden (of sins) on the Day of Resurrection

###### 20:101
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهِۖ وَسَآءَ لَهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ حِمۡلࣰ ا</div>

> They will abide in that (state in the Fire of Hell), and evil indeed will it be that load for them on the Day of Resurrection

###### 20:102
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِي ٱلصُّورِۚ وَنَحۡشُرُ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ زُرۡقࣰ ا</div>

> The Day when the Trumpet will be blown (the second blowing): that Day, We shall gather the Mujrimun (criminals, polytheists, sinners, disbelievers in the Oneness of Allah, etc.) Zurqa: (blue or blind eyed with black faces)

###### 20:103
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَتَخَٰفَتُونَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ إِن لَّبِثۡتُمۡ إِلَّا عَشۡرࣰ ا</div>

> In whispers will they speak to each other (saying): "You stayed not longer than ten (days)

###### 20:104
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَّحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَقُولُونَ إِذۡ يَقُولُ أَمۡثَلُهُمۡ طَرِيقَةً إِن لَّبِثۡتُمۡ إِلَّا يَوۡمࣰ ا</div>

> We know very well what they will say, when the best among them in knowledge and wisdom will say: "You stayed no longer than a day

###### 20:105
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَسۡـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلۡجِبَالِ فَقُلۡ يَنسِفُهَا رَبِّي نَسۡفࣰ ا</div>

> And they ask you concerning the mountains, say; "My Lord will blast them and scatter them as particles of dust

###### 20:106
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَيَذَرُهَا قَاعࣰ ا صَفۡصَفࣰ ا</div>

> Then He shall leave it as a level smooth plain

###### 20:107
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا تَرَىٰ فِيهَا عِوَجࣰ ا وَلَآ أَمۡتࣰ ا</div>

> You will see therein nothing crooked or curved

###### 20:108
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَئِذࣲ يَتَّبِعُونَ ٱلدَّاعِيَ لَا عِوَجَ لَهُۥۖ وَخَشَعَتِ ٱلۡأَصۡوَاتُ لِلرَّحۡمَٰنِ فَلَا تَسۡمَعُ إِلَّا هَمۡسࣰ ا</div>

> On that Day mankind will follow strictly (the voice of) Allah's caller, no crookedness (that is without going to the right or left of that voice) will they show him (Allah's caller). And all voices will be humbled for the Most Beneficent (Allah), and nothing shall you hear but the low voice of their footsteps

###### 20:109
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لَّا تَنفَعُ ٱلشَّفَٰعَةُ إِلَّا مَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَرَضِيَ لَهُۥ قَوۡلࣰ ا</div>

> On that day no intercession shall avail, except the one for whom the Most Beneficent (Allah) has given permission and whose word is acceptable to Him

###### 20:110
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَعۡلَمُ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَهُمۡ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِهِۦ عِلۡمࣰ ا</div>

> He (Allah) knows what happens to them (His creatures) in this world, and what will happen to them (in the Hereafter), and they will never compass anything of His Knowledge

###### 20:111
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَعَنَتِ ٱلۡوُجُوهُ لِلۡحَيِّ ٱلۡقَيُّومِۖ وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَنۡ حَمَلَ ظُلۡمࣰ ا</div>

> And (all) faces shall be humbled before (Allah), the Ever Living, the One Who sustains and protects all that exists. And he who carried (a burden of) wrongdoing (i.e. he who disbelieved in Allah, ascribed partners to Him, and did deeds of His disobedience), became indeed a complete failure (on that Day)

###### 20:112
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَهُوَ مُؤۡمِنࣱ فَلَا يَخَافُ ظُلۡمࣰ ا وَلَا هَضۡمࣰ ا</div>

> And he who works deeds of righteousness, while he is a believer (in Islamic Monotheism) then he will have no fear of injustice, nor of any curtailment (of his reward)

###### 20:113
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَذَٰلِكَ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَانًا عَرَبِيࣰّ ا وَصَرَّفۡنَا فِيهِ مِنَ ٱلۡوَعِيدِ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَّقُونَ أَوۡ يُحۡدِثُ لَهُمۡ ذِكۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And thus We have sent it down as a Quran in Arabic, and have explained therein in detail the warnings, in order that they may fear Allah, or that it may cause them to have a lesson from it (or to have the honour for believing and acting on its teachings)

###### 20:114

> Then High above all be Allah, the True King. And be not in haste (O Muhammad SAW) with the Quran before its revelation is completed to you, and say: "My Lord! Increase me in knowledge

###### 20:115
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ عَهِدۡنَآ إِلَىٰٓ ءَادَمَ مِن قَبۡلُ فَنَسِيَ وَلَمۡ نَجِدۡ لَهُۥ عَزۡمࣰ ا</div>

> And indeed We made a covenant with Adam before, but he forgot, and We found on his part no firm will-power

###### 20:116
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذۡ قُلۡنَا لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ ٱسۡجُدُواْ لِأٓدَمَ فَسَجَدُوٓاْ إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ أَبَىٰ</div>

###### 20:117
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقُلۡنَا يَٰٓـَٔادَمُ إِنَّ هَٰذَا عَدُوࣱّ لَّكَ وَلِزَوۡجِكَ فَلَا يُخۡرِجَنَّكُمَا مِنَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ فَتَشۡقَىٰٓ</div>

> Then We said: "O Adam! Verily, this is an enemy to you and to your wife. So let him not get you both out of Paradise, so that you be distressed in misery

###### 20:118
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ لَكَ أَلَّا تَجُوعَ فِيهَا وَلَا تَعۡرَىٰ</div>

> Verily, you have (a promise from Us) that you will never be hungry therein nor naked

###### 20:119
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّكَ لَا تَظۡمَؤُاْ فِيهَا وَلَا تَضۡحَىٰ</div>

> And you (will) suffer not from thirst therein nor from the sun's heat

###### 20:120
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَسۡوَسَ إِلَيۡهِ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ قَالَ يَٰٓـَٔادَمُ هَلۡ أَدُلُّكَ عَلَىٰ شَجَرَةِ ٱلۡخُلۡدِ وَمُلۡكࣲ لَّا يَبۡلَىٰ</div>

> Then Shaitan (Satan) whispered to him, saying: "O Adam! Shall I lead you to the Tree of Eternity and to a kingdom that will never waste away

###### 20:121
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَكَلَا مِنۡهَا فَبَدَتۡ لَهُمَا سَوۡءَٰتُهُمَا وَطَفِقَا يَخۡصِفَانِ عَلَيۡهِمَا مِن وَرَقِ ٱلۡجَنَّةِۚ وَعَصَىٰٓ ءَادَمُ رَبَّهُۥ فَغَوَىٰ</div>

###### 20:122
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ ٱجۡتَبَٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡهِ وَهَدَىٰ</div>

> Then his Lord chose him, and turned to him with forgiveness, and gave him guidance

###### 20:123
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ ٱهۡبِطَا مِنۡهَا جَمِيعَۢاۖ بَعۡضُكُمۡ لِبَعۡضٍ عَدُوࣱّۖ فَإِمَّا يَأۡتِيَنَّكُم مِّنِّي هُدࣰ ى فَمَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ هُدَايَ فَلَا يَضِلُّ وَلَا يَشۡقَىٰ</div>

> (Allah) said:"Get you down (from the Paradise to the earth), both of you, together, some of you are an enemy to some others. Then if there comes to you guidance from Me, then whoever follows My Guidance shall neither go astray, nor fall into distress and misery

###### 20:124
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَنۡ أَعۡرَضَ عَن ذِكۡرِي فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ مَعِيشَةࣰ ضَنكࣰ ا وَنَحۡشُرُهُۥ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ أَعۡمَىٰ</div>

> But whosoever turns away from My Reminder (i.e. neither believes in this Quran nor acts on its orders, etc.) verily, for him is a life of hardship, and We shall raise him up blind on the Day of Resurrection

###### 20:125
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ لِمَ حَشَرۡتَنِيٓ أَعۡمَىٰ وَقَدۡ كُنتُ بَصِيرࣰ ا</div>

> He will say:"O my Lord! Why have you raised me up blind, while I had sight (before)

###### 20:126
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ كَذَٰلِكَ أَتَتۡكَ ءَايَٰتُنَا فَنَسِيتَهَاۖ وَكَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ تُنسَىٰ</div>

> (Allah) will say: "Like this, Our Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) came unto you, but you disregarded them (i.e. you left them, did not think deeply in them, and you turned away from them), and so this Day, you will be neglected (in the Hell-fire, away from Allah's Mercy)

###### 20:127
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي مَنۡ أَسۡرَفَ وَلَمۡ يُؤۡمِنۢ بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِۦۚ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَشَدُّ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ</div>

> And thus do We requite him who transgresses beyond bounds [i.e. commits the great sins and disobeys his Lord (Allah) and believes not in His Messengers, and His revealed Books, like this Quran, etc.], and believes not in the Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) of his Lord, and the torment of the Hereafter is far more severe and more lasting

###### 20:128
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَلَمۡ يَهۡدِ لَهُمۡ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ يَمۡشُونَ فِي مَسَٰكِنِهِمۡۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتࣲ لِّأُوْلِي ٱلنُّهَىٰ</div>

> Is it not a guidance for them (to know) how many generations We have destroyed before them, in whose dwellings they walk? Verily, in this are signs indeed for men of understanding

###### 20:129
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡلَا كَلِمَةࣱ سَبَقَتۡ مِن رَّبِّكَ لَكَانَ لِزَامࣰ ا وَأَجَلࣱ مُّسَمࣰّ ى</div>

> And had it not been for a Word that went forth before from your Lord, and a term determined, (their punishment) must necessarily have come (in this world)

###### 20:130
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ قَبۡلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَقَبۡلَ غُرُوبِهَاۖ وَمِنۡ ءَانَآيِٕ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡ وَأَطۡرَافَ ٱلنَّهَارِ لَعَلَّكَ تَرۡضَىٰ</div>

> So bear patiently (O Muhammad SAW) what they say, and glorify the praises of your Lord before the rising of the sun, and before its setting, and during some of the hours of the night, and at the sides of the day (an indication for the five compulsory congregational prayers), that you may become pleased with the reward which Allah shall give you

###### 20:131
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيۡنَيۡكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعۡنَا بِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجࣰ ا مِّنۡهُمۡ زَهۡرَةَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا لِنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ وَرِزۡقُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰ</div>

> And strain not your eyes in longing for the things We have given for enjoyment to various groups of them (polytheists and disbelievers in the Oneness of Allah), the splendour of the life of this world that We may test them thereby. But the provision (good reward in the Hereafter) of your Lord is better and more lasting

###### 20:132
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأۡمُرۡ أَهۡلَكَ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ عَلَيۡهَاۖ لَا نَسۡـَٔلُكَ رِزۡقࣰ اۖ نَّحۡنُ نَرۡزُقُكَۗ وَٱلۡعَٰقِبَةُ لِلتَّقۡوَىٰ</div>

> And enjoin As-Salat (the prayer) on your family, and be patient in offering them [i.e. the Salat (prayers)]. We ask not of you a provision (i.e. to give Us something: money, etc.); We provide for you. And the good end (i.e. Paradise) is for the Muttaqun (pious - see V)

###### 20:133
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ لَوۡلَا يَأۡتِينَا بِـَٔايَةࣲ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦٓۚ أَوَلَمۡ تَأۡتِهِم بَيِّنَةُ مَا فِي ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> They say: "Why does he not bring us a sign (proof) from his Lord?" Has there not come to them the proof of that which is (written) in the former papers [Scriptures, i.e. the Taurat (Torah), and the Injeel (Gospel), etc. about the coming of the Prophet Muhammad SAW

###### 20:134
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡ أَنَّآ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُم بِعَذَابࣲ مِّن قَبۡلِهِۦ لَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا لَوۡلَآ أَرۡسَلۡتَ إِلَيۡنَا رَسُولࣰ ا فَنَتَّبِعَ ءَايَٰتِكَ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن نَّذِلَّ وَنَخۡزَىٰ</div>

> And if We had destroyed them with a torment before this (i.e. Messenger Muhammad SAW and the Quran), they would surely have said: "Our Lord!
