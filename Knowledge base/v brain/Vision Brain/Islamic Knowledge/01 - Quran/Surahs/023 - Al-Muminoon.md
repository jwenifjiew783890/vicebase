# 23. Al-Muminoon — The Believers

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 118 · **Juz':** 18

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 23:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> Successful indeed are the believers

###### 23:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي صَلَاتِهِمۡ خَٰشِعُونَ</div>

> Those who offer their Salat (prayers) with all solemnity and full submissiveness

###### 23:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَنِ ٱللَّغۡوِ مُعۡرِضُونَ</div>

> And those who turn away from Al-Laghw (dirty, false, evil vain talk, falsehood, and all that Allah has forbidden)

###### 23:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِلزَّكَوٰةِ فَٰعِلُونَ</div>

> And those who pay the Zakat

###### 23:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ</div>

> And those who guard their chastity (i.e. private parts, from illegal sexual acts)

###### 23:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ غَيۡرُ مَلُومِينَ</div>

> Except from their wives or (the captives and slaves) that their right hands possess, for then, they are free from blame

###### 23:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡعَادُونَ</div>

> But whoever seeks beyond that, then those are the transgressors

###### 23:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ</div>

> Those who are faithfully true to their Amanat (all the duties which Allah has ordained, honesty, moral responsibility and trusts etc.) and to their covenants

###### 23:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَوَٰتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ</div>

> And those who strictly guard their (five compulsory congregational) Salawat (prayers) (at their fixed stated hours)

###### 23:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡوَٰرِثُونَ</div>

> These are indeed the inheritors

###### 23:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ يَرِثُونَ ٱلۡفِرۡدَوۡسَ هُمۡ فِيهَا خَٰلِدُونَ</div>

> Who shall inherit the Firdaus (Paradise). They shall dwell therein forever

###### 23:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن سُلَٰلَةࣲ مِّن طِينࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed We created man (Adam) out of an extract of clay (water and earth)

###### 23:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ نُطۡفَةࣰ فِي قَرَارࣲ مَّكِينࣲ‏</div>

> Thereafter We made him (the offspring of Adam) as a Nutfah (mixed drops of the male and female sexual discharge) (and lodged it) in a safe lodging (womb of the woman)

###### 23:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلنُّطۡفَةَ عَلَقَةࣰ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡعَلَقَةَ مُضۡغَةࣰ فَخَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡمُضۡغَةَ عِظَٰمࣰ ا فَكَسَوۡنَا ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ لَحۡمࣰ ا ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَٰهُ خَلۡقًا ءَاخَرَۚ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحۡسَنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقِينَ</div>

> Then We made the Nutfah into a clot (a piece of thick coagulated blood), then We made the clot into a little lump of flesh, then We made out of that little lump of flesh bones, then We clothed the bones with flesh, and then We brought it forth as another creation. So blessed be Allah, the Best of creators

###### 23:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنَّكُم بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ لَمَيِّتُونَ</div>

> After that, surely, you will die

###### 23:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ تُبۡعَثُونَ</div>

> Then (again), surely, you will be resurrected on the Day of Resurrection

###### 23:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعَ طَرَآئِقَ وَمَا كُنَّا عَنِ ٱلۡخَلۡقِ غَٰفِلِينَ</div>

> And indeed We have created above you seven heavens (one over the other), and We are never unaware of the creation

###### 23:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءَۢ بِقَدَرࣲ فَأَسۡكَنَّٰهُ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِۖ وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰ ذَهَابِۭ بِهِۦ لَقَٰدِرُونَ</div>

> And We sent down from the sky water (rain) in (due) measure, and We gave it lodging in the earth, and verily, We are Able to take it away

###### 23:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَنشَأۡنَا لَكُم بِهِۦ جَنَّٰتࣲ مِّن نَّخِيلࣲ وَأَعۡنَٰبࣲ لَّكُمۡ فِيهَا فَوَٰكِهُ كَثِيرَةࣱ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ</div>

> Then We brought forth for you therewith gardens of date-palms and grapes, wherein is much fruit for you, and whereof you eat

###### 23:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَشَجَرَةࣰ تَخۡرُجُ مِن طُورِ سَيۡنَآءَ تَنۢبُتُ بِٱلدُّهۡنِ وَصِبۡغࣲ لِّلۡأٓكِلِينَ</div>

> And a tree (olive) that springs forth from Mount Sinai, that grows oil, and (it is a) relish for the eaters

###### 23:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَنۡعَٰمِ لَعِبۡرَةࣰۖ نُّسۡقِيكُم مِّمَّا فِي بُطُونِهَا وَلَكُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ كَثِيرَةࣱ وَمِنۡهَا تَأۡكُلُونَ</div>

> And Verily! In the cattle there is indeed a lesson for you. We give you to drink (milk) of that which is in their bellies. And there are, in them, numerous (other) benefits for you, and of them you eat

###### 23:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَعَلَيۡهَا وَعَلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ تُحۡمَلُونَ</div>

> And on them, and on ships you are carried

###### 23:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ فَقَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۚ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ</div>

> And indeed We sent Nuh (Noah) to his people, and he said: "O my people! Worship Allah! You have no other Ilah (God) but Him (Islamic Monotheism). Will you not then be afraid (of Him i.e. of His Punishment because of worshipping others besides Him)

###### 23:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَؤُاْ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِن قَوۡمِهِۦ مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يُرِيدُ أَن يَتَفَضَّلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ وَلَوۡ شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ لَأَنزَلَ مَلَٰٓئِكَةࣰ مَّا سَمِعۡنَا بِهَٰذَا فِيٓ ءَابَآئِنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> But the chiefs of those who disbelieved among his people said: "He is no more than a human being like you, he seeks to make himself superior to you. Had Allah willed, He surely could have sent down angels; never did we hear such a thing among our fathers of old

###### 23:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا رَجُلُۢ بِهِۦ جِنَّةࣱ فَتَرَبَّصُواْ بِهِۦ حَتَّىٰ حِينࣲ‏</div>

> He is only a man in whom is madness, so wait for him a while

###### 23:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرۡنِي بِمَا كَذَّبُونِ</div>

> Nuh (Noah)] said: "O my Lord! Help me because they deny me

###### 23:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِ أَنِ ٱصۡنَعِ ٱلۡفُلۡكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَا وَوَحۡيِنَا فَإِذَا جَآءَ أَمۡرُنَا وَفَارَ ٱلتَّنُّورُ فَٱسۡلُكۡ فِيهَا مِن كُلࣲّ زَوۡجَيۡنِ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ وَأَهۡلَكَ إِلَّا مَن سَبَقَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَلَا تُخَٰطِبۡنِي فِي ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوٓاْ إِنَّهُم مُّغۡرَقُونَ</div>

> So We inspired him (saying): "Construct the ship under Our Eyes and under Our Revelation (guidance). Then, when Our Command comes, and the oven gushes forth water, take on board of each kind two (male and female), and your family, except those thereof against whom the Word has already gone forth. And address Me not in favour of those who have done wrong. Verily, they are to be drowned

###### 23:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا ٱسۡتَوَيۡتَ أَنتَ وَمَن مَّعَكَ عَلَى ٱلۡفُلۡكِ فَقُلِ ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي نَجَّىٰنَا مِنَ ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ</div>

> And when you have embarked on the ship, you and whoever is with you, then say: "All the praises and thanks be to Allah, Who has saved us from the people who are Zalimun (i.e. oppressors, wrong-doers, polytheists, those who join others in worship with Allah, etc)

###### 23:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقُل رَّبِّ أَنزِلۡنِي مُنزَلࣰ ا مُّبَارَكࣰ ا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلۡمُنزِلِينَ</div>

> And say: "My Lord! Cause me to land at a blessed landing-place, for You are the Best of those who bring to land

###### 23:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَٰتࣲ وَإِن كُنَّا لَمُبۡتَلِينَ</div>

> Verily, in this [what We did as regards drowning of the people of Nuh (Noah)], there are indeed Ayat (proofs, evidences, lessons, signs, etc. for men to understand), for sure We are ever putting (men) to the test

###### 23:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ قَرۡنًا ءَاخَرِينَ</div>

> Then, after them, We created another generation

###### 23:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَرۡسَلۡنَا فِيهِمۡ رَسُولࣰ ا مِّنۡهُمۡ أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنۡ إِلَٰهٍ غَيۡرُهُۥٓۚ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ</div>

> And We sent to them a Messenger from among themselves (saying): "Worship Allah! You have no other Ilah (God) but Him. Will you not then be afraid (of Him i.e. of His Punishment because of worshipping others besides Him)

###### 23:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِن قَوۡمِهِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَكَذَّبُواْ بِلِقَآءِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَأَتۡرَفۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنۡيَا مَا هَٰذَآ إِلَّا بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُكُمۡ يَأۡكُلُ مِمَّا تَأۡكُلُونَ مِنۡهُ وَيَشۡرَبُ مِمَّا تَشۡرَبُونَ</div>

> And the chiefs of his people, who disbelieved and denied the Meeting in the Hereafter, and to whom We had given the luxuries and comforts of this life, said: "He is no more than a human being like you, he eats of that which you eat, and drinks of what you drink

###### 23:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَئِنۡ أَطَعۡتُم بَشَرࣰ ا مِّثۡلَكُمۡ إِنَّكُمۡ إِذࣰ ا لَّخَٰسِرُونَ</div>

> If you were to obey a human being like yourselves, then verily! You indeed would be losers

###### 23:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَعِدُكُمۡ أَنَّكُمۡ إِذَا مِتُّمۡ وَكُنتُمۡ تُرَابࣰ ا وَعِظَٰمًا أَنَّكُم مُّخۡرَجُونَ</div>

> Does he promise you that when you have died and have become dust and bones, you shall come out alive (resurrected)

###### 23:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞هَيۡهَاتَ هَيۡهَاتَ لِمَا تُوعَدُونَ</div>

> Far, very far is that which you are promised

###### 23:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا حَيَاتُنَا ٱلدُّنۡيَا نَمُوتُ وَنَحۡيَا وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَبۡعُوثِينَ</div>

> There is nothing but our life of this world! We die and we live! And we are not going to be resurrected

###### 23:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا رَجُلٌ ٱفۡتَرَىٰ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبࣰ ا وَمَا نَحۡنُ لَهُۥ بِمُؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> He is only a man who has invented a lie against Allah, but we are not going to believe in him

###### 23:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ ٱنصُرۡنِي بِمَا كَذَّبُونِ</div>

> He said: "O my Lord! Help me because they deny me

###### 23:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ عَمَّا قَلِيلࣲ لَّيُصۡبِحُنَّ نَٰدِمِينَ</div>

> (Allah) said: "In a little while, they are sure to be regretful

###### 23:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّيۡحَةُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ غُثَآءࣰۚ فَبُعۡدࣰ ا لِّلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ</div>

> So As-Saihah (torment - awful cry, etc.) overtook them with justice, and We made them as rubbish of dead plants. So away with the people who are Zalimun (polytheists, wrong-doers, disbelievers in the Oneness of Allah, disobedient to His Messengers, etc)

###### 23:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَنشَأۡنَا مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ قُرُونًا ءَاخَرِينَ</div>

> Then, after them, We created other generations

###### 23:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا تَسۡبِقُ مِنۡ أُمَّةٍ أَجَلَهَا وَمَا يَسۡتَـٔۡخِرُونَ</div>

> No nation can anticipate their term, nor can they delay it

###### 23:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَرۡسَلۡنَا رُسُلَنَا تَتۡرَاۖ كُلَّ مَا جَآءَ أُمَّةࣰ رَّسُولُهَا كَذَّبُوهُۖ فَأَتۡبَعۡنَا بَعۡضَهُم بَعۡضࣰ ا وَجَعَلۡنَٰهُمۡ أَحَادِيثَۚ فَبُعۡدࣰ ا لِّقَوۡمࣲ لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> Then We sent Our Messengers in succession, every time there came to a nation their Messenger, they denied him, so We made them follow one another (to destruction), and We made them as Ahadith (the true stories for mankind to learn a lesson from them). So away with a people who believe not

###### 23:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَرۡسَلۡنَا مُوسَىٰ وَأَخَاهُ هَٰرُونَ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا وَسُلۡطَٰنࣲ مُّبِينٍ</div>

> Then We sent Musa (Moses) and his brother Harun (Aaron), with Our Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) and manifest authority

###### 23:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَمَلَإِيْهِۦ فَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ وَكَانُواْ قَوۡمًا عَالِينَ</div>

> To Fir'aun (Pharaoh) and his chiefs, but they behaved insolently and they were people self-exalting (by disobeying their Lord, and exalting themselves over and above the Messenger of Allah)

###### 23:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالُوٓاْ أَنُؤۡمِنُ لِبَشَرَيۡنِ مِثۡلِنَا وَقَوۡمُهُمَا لَنَا عَٰبِدُونَ</div>

> They said: "Shall we believe in two men like ourselves, and their people are obedient to us with humility (and we use them to serve us as we like)

###### 23:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَكَانُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهۡلَكِينَ</div>

> So they denied them both [Musa (Moses) and Harun (Aaron)] and became of those who were destroyed

###### 23:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ ءَاتَيۡنَا مُوسَى ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَهۡتَدُونَ</div>

> And indeed We gave Musa (Moses) the Scripture, that they may be guided

###### 23:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱبۡنَ مَرۡيَمَ وَأُمَّهُۥٓ ءَايَةࣰ وَءَاوَيۡنَٰهُمَآ إِلَىٰ رَبۡوَةࣲ ذَاتِ قَرَارࣲ وَمَعِينࣲ‏</div>

> And We made the son of Maryam (Mary) and his mother as a sign, And We gave them refuge on high ground, a place of rest, security and flowing streams

###### 23:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلرُّسُلُ كُلُواْ مِنَ ٱلطَّيِّبَٰتِ وَٱعۡمَلُواْ صَٰلِحًاۖ إِنِّي بِمَا تَعۡمَلُونَ عَلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> O (you) Messengers! Eat of the Taiyibat [all kinds of Halal (legal) foods which Allah has made legal (meat of slaughtered eatable animals, milk products, fats, vegetables, fruits, etc.], and do righteous deeds. Verily! I am Well-Acquainted with what you do

###### 23:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمۡ أُمَّةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمۡ فَٱتَّقُونِ</div>

> And verily! This your religion (of Islamic Monotheism) is one religion, and I am your Lord, so keep your duty to Me

###### 23:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَتَقَطَّعُوٓاْ أَمۡرَهُم بَيۡنَهُمۡ زُبُرࣰ اۖ كُلُّ حِزۡبِۭ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ فَرِحُونَ</div>

> But they (men) have broken their religion among them into sects, each group rejoicing in its belief

###### 23:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَرۡهُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَتِهِمۡ حَتَّىٰ حِينٍ</div>

> So leave them in their error for a time

###### 23:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَحۡسَبُونَ أَنَّمَا نُمِدُّهُم بِهِۦ مِن مَّالࣲ وَبَنِينَ</div>

> Do they think that We enlarge them in wealth and children

###### 23:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نُسَارِعُ لَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِۚ بَل لَّا يَشۡعُرُونَ</div>

> We hasten unto them with good things (in this worldly life so that they will have no share of good things in the Hereafter)? Nay, but they perceive not

###### 23:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنۡ خَشۡيَةِ رَبِّهِم مُّشۡفِقُونَ</div>

> Verily! Those who live in awe for fear of their Lord

###### 23:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِـَٔايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> And those who believe in the Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) of their Lord

###### 23:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِرَبِّهِمۡ لَا يُشۡرِكُونَ</div>

> And those who join not anyone (in worship) as partners with their Lord

###### 23:60
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤۡتُونَ مَآ ءَاتَواْ وَّقُلُوبُهُمۡ وَجِلَةٌ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ رَٰجِعُونَ</div>

> And those who give that (their charity) which they give (and also do other good deeds) with their hearts full of fear (whether their alms and charities, etc., have been accepted or not), because they are sure to return to their Lord (for reckoning)

###### 23:61
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ يُسَٰرِعُونَ فِي ٱلۡخَيۡرَٰتِ وَهُمۡ لَهَا سَٰبِقُونَ</div>

> It is these who race for the good deeds, and they are foremost in them [e.g. offering the compulsory Salat (prayers) in their (early) stated, fixed times and so on

###### 23:62
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا نُكَلِّفُ نَفۡسًا إِلَّا وُسۡعَهَاۚ وَلَدَيۡنَا كِتَٰبࣱ يَنطِقُ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَهُمۡ لَا يُظۡلَمُونَ</div>

> And We tax not any person except according to his capacity, and with Us is a Record which speaks the truth, and they will not be wronged

###### 23:63
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ قُلُوبُهُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَةࣲ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا وَلَهُمۡ أَعۡمَٰلࣱ مِّن دُونِ ذَٰلِكَ هُمۡ لَهَا عَٰمِلُونَ</div>

> Nay, but their hearts are covered (blind) from understanding this (the Quran), and they have other (evil) deeds, besides, which they are doing

###### 23:64
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَآ أَخَذۡنَا مُتۡرَفِيهِم بِٱلۡعَذَابِ إِذَا هُمۡ يَجۡـَٔرُونَ</div>

> Until, when We grasp those of them who lead a luxurious life with punishment, behold! They make humble invocation with a loud voice

###### 23:65
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا تَجۡـَٔرُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَۖ إِنَّكُم مِّنَّا لَا تُنصَرُونَ</div>

> Invoke not loudly this day! Certainly, you shall not be helped by Us

###### 23:66
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَدۡ كَانَتۡ ءَايَٰتِي تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَكُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡقَٰبِكُمۡ تَنكِصُونَ</div>

> Indeed My Verses used to be recited to you, but you used to turn back on your heels (denying them, and with hatred to listen to them)

###### 23:67
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُسۡتَكۡبِرِينَ بِهِۦ سَٰمِرࣰ ا تَهۡجُرُونَ</div>

> In pride (they Quraish pagans and polytheists of Makkah used to feel proud that they are the dwellers of Makkah sanctuary Haram), talking evil about it (the Quran) by night

###### 23:68
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَلَمۡ يَدَّبَّرُواْ ٱلۡقَوۡلَ أَمۡ جَآءَهُم مَّا لَمۡ يَأۡتِ ءَابَآءَهُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> Have they not pondered over the Word (of Allah, i.e. what is sent down to the Prophet SAW), or has there come to them what had not come to their fathers of old

###### 23:69
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَمۡ يَعۡرِفُواْ رَسُولَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَهُۥ مُنكِرُونَ</div>

> Or is it that they did not recognize their Messenger (Muhammad SAW) so they deny him

###### 23:70
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ بِهِۦ جِنَّةُۢۚ بَلۡ جَآءَهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَأَكۡثَرُهُمۡ لِلۡحَقِّ كَٰرِهُونَ</div>

> Or say they: "There is madness in him?" Nay, but he brought them the truth [i.e. "(A) Tauhid: Worshipping Allah Alone in all aspects (B) The Quran (C) The religion of Islam,"] but most of them (the disbelievers) are averse to the truth

###### 23:71
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلۡحَقُّ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡ لَفَسَدَتِ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّۚ بَلۡ أَتَيۡنَٰهُم بِذِكۡرِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ عَن ذِكۡرِهِم مُّعۡرِضُونَ</div>

> And if the truth had been in accordance with their desires, verily, the heavens and the earth, and whosoever is therein would have been corrupted! Nay, We have brought them their reminder, but they turn away from their reminder

###### 23:72
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ تَسۡـَٔلُهُمۡ خَرۡجࣰ ا فَخَرَاجُ رَبِّكَ خَيۡرࣱۖ وَهُوَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰزِقِينَ</div>

> Or is it that you (O Muhammad SAW) ask them for some wages? But the recompense of your Lord is better, and He is the Best of those who give sustenance

###### 23:73
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّكَ لَتَدۡعُوهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطࣲ مُّسۡتَقِيمࣲ‏</div>

> And certainly, you (O Muhammad SAW) call them to a Straight Path (true religion Islamic Monotheism)

###### 23:74
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ عَنِ ٱلصِّرَٰطِ لَنَٰكِبُونَ</div>

> And verily, those who believe not in the Hereafter are indeed deviating far astray from the Path (true religion Islamic Monotheism)

###### 23:75
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَلَوۡ رَحِمۡنَٰهُمۡ وَكَشَفۡنَا مَا بِهِم مِّن ضُرࣲّ لَّلَجُّواْ فِي طُغۡيَٰنِهِمۡ يَعۡمَهُونَ</div>

> And though We had mercy on them and removed the distress which is on them, still they would obstinately persist in their transgression, wandering blindly

###### 23:76
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ أَخَذۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡعَذَابِ فَمَا ٱسۡتَكَانُواْ لِرَبِّهِمۡ وَمَا يَتَضَرَّعُونَ</div>

> And indeed We seized them with punishment, but they humbled not themselves to their Lord, nor did they invoke (Allah) with submission to Him

###### 23:77
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا فَتَحۡنَا عَلَيۡهِم بَابࣰ ا ذَا عَذَابࣲ شَدِيدٍ إِذَا هُمۡ فِيهِ مُبۡلِسُونَ</div>

> Until, when We open for them the gate of severe punishment, then lo! They will be plunged into destruction with deep regrets, sorrows and in despair

###### 23:78
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلࣰ ا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ</div>

> It is He, Who has created for you (the sense of) hearing (ears), sight (eyes), and hearts (understanding). Little thanks you give

###### 23:79
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ</div>

> And it is He Who has created you on the earth, and to Him you shall be gathered back

###### 23:80
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُ وَلَهُ ٱخۡتِلَٰفُ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَٱلنَّهَارِۚ أَفَلَا تَعۡقِلُونَ</div>

> And it is He Who gives life and causes death, and His is the alternation of night and day. Will you not then understand

###### 23:81
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ قَالُواْ مِثۡلَ مَا قَالَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ</div>

> Nay, but they say the like of what the men of old said

###### 23:82
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابࣰ ا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ</div>

> They said: "When we are dead and have become dust and bones, shall we be resurrected indeed

###### 23:83
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَقَدۡ وُعِدۡنَا نَحۡنُ وَءَابَآؤُنَا هَٰذَا مِن قَبۡلُ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّآ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> Verily, this we have been promised, we and our fathers before (us)! This is only the tales of the ancients

###### 23:84
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُل لِّمَنِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَن فِيهَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Say: "Whose is the earth and whosoever is therein? If you know

###### 23:85
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلۡ أَفَلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ</div>

> They will say: "It is Allah's!" Say: "Will you not then remember

###### 23:86
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ مَن رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ ٱلسَّبۡعِ وَرَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ</div>

> Say: "Who is (the) Lord of the seven heavens, and (the) Lord of the Great Throne

###### 23:87
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلۡ أَفَلَا تَتَّقُونَ</div>

> They will say: "Allah." Say: "Will you not then fear Allah (believe in His Oneness, obey Him, believe in the Resurrection and Recompense for each and every good or bad deed)

###### 23:88
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ مَنۢ بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ وَهُوَ يُجِيرُ وَلَا يُجَارُ عَلَيۡهِ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Say "In Whose Hand is the sovereignty of everything (i.e. treasures of each and everything)? And He protects (all), while against Whom there is no protector, (i.e. if Allah saves anyone none can punish or harm him, and if Allah punishes or harms anyone none can save him), if you know." [Tafsir Al-Qurtubi, Vol. 12, Page]

###### 23:89
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَيَقُولُونَ لِلَّهِۚ قُلۡ فَأَنَّىٰ تُسۡحَرُونَ</div>

> They will say: "(All that belongs) to Allah." Say: "How then are you deceived and turn away from the truth

###### 23:90
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ أَتَيۡنَٰهُم بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَكَٰذِبُونَ</div>

> Nay, but We have brought them the truth (Islamic Monotheism), and verily, they (disbelievers) are liars

###### 23:91
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا ٱتَّخَذَ ٱللَّهُ مِن وَلَدࣲ وَمَا كَانَ مَعَهُۥ مِنۡ إِلَٰهٍۚ إِذࣰ ا لَّذَهَبَ كُلُّ إِلَٰهِۭ بِمَا خَلَقَ وَلَعَلَا بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضࣲۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ</div>

> No son (or offspring or children) did Allah beget, nor is there any ilah (god) along with Him; (if there had been many gods), behold, each god would have taken away what he had created, and some would have tried to overcome others! Glorified be Allah above all that they attribute to Him

###### 23:92
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَتَعَٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ</div>

> All-Knower of the unseen and the seen! Exalted be He over all that they associate as partners to Him

###### 23:93
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُل رَّبِّ إِمَّا تُرِيَنِّي مَا يُوعَدُونَ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): " My Lord! If You would show me that with which they are threatened (torment)

###### 23:94
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَبِّ فَلَا تَجۡعَلۡنِي فِي ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ</div>

> My Lord! Then (save me from Your Punishment), and put me not amongst the people who are the Zalimun (polytheists and wrong-doing)

###### 23:95
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّرِيَكَ مَا نَعِدُهُمۡ لَقَٰدِرُونَ</div>

> And indeed We are Able to show you (O Muhammad SAW) that with which We have threatened them

###### 23:96
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱدۡفَعۡ بِٱلَّتِي هِيَ أَحۡسَنُ ٱلسَّيِّئَةَۚ نَحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَصِفُونَ</div>

> Repel evil with that which is better. We are Best-Acquainted with the things they utter

###### 23:97
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقُل رَّبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنۡ هَمَزَٰتِ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ</div>

> And say: "My Lord! I seek refuge with You from the whisperings (suggestions) of the Shayatin (devils)

###### 23:98
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ رَبِّ أَن يَحۡضُرُونِ</div>

> And I seek refuge with You, My Lord! lest they may attend (or come near) me

###### 23:99
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا جَآءَ أَحَدَهُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ٱرۡجِعُونِ</div>

> Until, when death comes to one of them (those who join partners with Allah), he says: "My Lord! Send me back

###### 23:100
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَعَلِّيٓ أَعۡمَلُ صَٰلِحࣰ ا فِيمَا تَرَكۡتُۚ كَلَّآۚ إِنَّهَا كَلِمَةٌ هُوَ قَآئِلُهَاۖ وَمِن وَرَآئِهِم بَرۡزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ</div>

> So that I may do good in that which I have left behind!" No! It is but a word that he speaks, and behind them is Barzakh (a barrier) until the Day when they will be resurrected

###### 23:101
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَلَآ أَنسَابَ بَيۡنَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ وَلَا يَتَسَآءَلُونَ</div>

> Then, when the Trumpet is blown, there will be no kinship among them that Day, nor will they ask of one another

###### 23:102
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡمُفۡلِحُونَ</div>

> Then, those whose scales (of good deeds) are heavy, - these, they are the successful

###### 23:103
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ خَسِرُوٓاْ أَنفُسَهُمۡ فِي جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدُونَ</div>

> And those whose scales (of good deeds) are light, they are those who lose their ownselves, in Hell will they abide

###### 23:104
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَلۡفَحُ وُجُوهَهُمُ ٱلنَّارُ وَهُمۡ فِيهَا كَٰلِحُونَ</div>

> The Fire will burn their faces, and therein they will grin, with displaced lips (disfigured)

###### 23:105
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ تَكُنۡ ءَايَٰتِي تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡكُمۡ فَكُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> Were not My Verses (this Quran) recited to you, and then you used to deny them

###### 23:106
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا غَلَبَتۡ عَلَيۡنَا شِقۡوَتُنَا وَكُنَّا قَوۡمࣰ ا ضَآلِّينَ</div>

> They will say: "Our Lord! Our wretchedness overcame us, and we were (an) erring people

###### 23:107
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَبَّنَآ أَخۡرِجۡنَا مِنۡهَا فَإِنۡ عُدۡنَا فَإِنَّا ظَٰلِمُونَ</div>

> Our Lord! Bring us out of this; if ever we return (to evil), then indeed we shall be Zalimun: (polytheists, oppressors, unjust, and wrong-doers, etc)

###### 23:108
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ ٱخۡسَـُٔواْ فِيهَا وَلَا تُكَلِّمُونِ</div>

> He (Allah) will say: "Remain you in it with ignominy! And speak you not to Me

###### 23:109
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فَرِيقࣱ مِّنۡ عِبَادِي يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَآ ءَامَنَّا فَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَا وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ</div>

> Verily! There was a party of My slaves, who used to say: "Our Lord! We believe, so forgive us, and have mercy on us, for You are the Best of all who show mercy

###### 23:110
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱتَّخَذۡتُمُوهُمۡ سِخۡرِيًّا حَتَّىٰٓ أَنسَوۡكُمۡ ذِكۡرِي وَكُنتُم مِّنۡهُمۡ تَضۡحَكُونَ</div>

> But you took them for a laughingstock, so much so that they made you forget My Remembrance while you used to laugh at them

###### 23:111
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنِّي جَزَيۡتُهُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا صَبَرُوٓاْ أَنَّهُمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡفَآئِزُونَ</div>

> Verily! I have rewarded them this Day for their patience, they are indeed the ones that are successful

###### 23:112
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَٰلَ كَمۡ لَبِثۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ عَدَدَ سِنِينَ</div>

> He (Allah) will say: "What number of years did you stay on earth

###### 23:113
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ لَبِثۡنَا يَوۡمًا أَوۡ بَعۡضَ يَوۡمࣲ فَسۡـَٔلِ ٱلۡعَآدِّينَ</div>

> They will say: "We stayed a day or part of a day. Ask of those who keep account

###### 23:114
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَٰلَ إِن لَّبِثۡتُمۡ إِلَّا قَلِيلࣰ اۖ لَّوۡ أَنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> He (Allah) will say: "You stayed not but a little, if you had only known

###### 23:115
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَحَسِبۡتُمۡ أَنَّمَا خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ عَبَثࣰ ا وَأَنَّكُمۡ إِلَيۡنَا لَا تُرۡجَعُونَ</div>

> Did you think that We had created you in play (without any purpose), and that you would not be brought back to Us

###### 23:116
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَتَعَٰلَى ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡمَلِكُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ</div>

> So Exalted be Allah, the True King, La ilaha illa Huwa (none has the right to be worshipped but He), the Lord of the Supreme Throne

###### 23:117
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَن يَدۡعُ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ لَا بُرۡهَٰنَ لَهُۥ بِهِۦ فَإِنَّمَا حِسَابُهُۥ عِندَ رَبِّهِۦٓۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُفۡلِحُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ</div>

> And whoever invokes (or worships), besides Allah, any other ilah (god), of whom he has no proof, then his reckoning is only with his Lord. Surely! Al-Kafirun (the disbelievers in Allah and in the Oneness of Allah, polytheists, pagans, idolaters, etc.) will not be successful

###### 23:118
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقُل رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ وَٱرۡحَمۡ وَأَنتَ خَيۡرُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ</div>

> And say (O Muhammad SAW): "My Lord! Forgive and have mercy, for You are the Best of those who show mercy
