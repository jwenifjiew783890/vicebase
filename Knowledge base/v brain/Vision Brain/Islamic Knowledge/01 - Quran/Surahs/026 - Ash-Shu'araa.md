# 26. Ash-Shu'araa — The Poets

###### 26:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">طسٓمٓ</div>

> Ta-Sin-Mim. [These letters are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings

###### 26:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تِلۡكَ ءَايَٰتُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ</div>

> These are the Verses of the manifest Book [this Quran, which was promised by Allah in the Taurat (Torah) and the Injeel (Gospel), makes things clear

###### 26:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَعَلَّكَ بَٰخِعࣱ نَّفۡسَكَ أَلَّا يَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> It may be that you (O Muhammad SAW) are going to kill yourself with grief, that they do not become believers [in your Risalah (Messengership) and in your Message of Islamic Monotheism

###### 26:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِن نَّشَأۡ نُنَزِّلۡ عَلَيۡهِم مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ ءَايَةࣰ فَظَلَّتۡ أَعۡنَٰقُهُمۡ لَهَا خَٰضِعِينَ</div>

> If We will, We could send down to them from the heaven a sign, to which they would bend their necks in humility

###### 26:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن ذِكۡرࣲ مِّنَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مُحۡدَثٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ عَنۡهُ مُعۡرِضِينَ</div>

> And never comes there unto them a Reminder as a recent revelation from the Most Beneficent (Allah), but they turn away therefrom

###### 26:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَدۡ كَذَّبُواْ فَسَيَأۡتِيهِمۡ أَنۢبَٰٓؤُاْ مَا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ</div>

> So they have indeed denied (the truth - this Quran), then the news of what they mocked at, will come to them

###### 26:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَمۡ أَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجࣲ كَرِيمٍ</div>

> Do they not observe the earth, how much of every good kind We cause to grow therein

###### 26:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَأٓيَةࣰۖ وَمَا كَانَ أَكۡثَرُهُم مُّؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> Verily, in this is an Ayah (proof or sign), yet most of them (polytheists, pagans, etc., who do not believe in Resurrection) are not believers

###### 26:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ</div>

> And verily, your Lord! He is truly the All-Mighty, the Most Merciful

###### 26:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبُّكَ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنِ ٱئۡتِ ٱلۡقَوۡمَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ</div>

> And (remember) when your Lord called Musa (Moses) (saying): "Go to the people who are Zalimun (polytheists and wrong-doing)

###### 26:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَوۡمَ فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ أَلَا يَتَّقُونَ</div>

> The people of Fir'aun (Pharaoh). Will they not fear Allah and become righteous

###### 26:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّيٓ أَخَافُ أَن يُكَذِّبُونِ</div>

> He said: "My Lord! Verily, I fear that they will belie me

###### 26:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَضِيقُ صَدۡرِي وَلَا يَنطَلِقُ لِسَانِي فَأَرۡسِلۡ إِلَىٰ هَٰرُونَ</div>

> And my breast straitens, and my tongue expresses not well. So send for Harun (Aaron) (to come along with me)

###### 26:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَهُمۡ عَلَيَّ ذَنۢبࣱ فَأَخَافُ أَن يَقۡتُلُونِ</div>

> And they have a charge of crime against me, and I fear they will kill me

###### 26:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ كَلَّاۖ فَٱذۡهَبَا بِـَٔايَٰتِنَآۖ إِنَّا مَعَكُم مُّسۡتَمِعُونَ</div>

> Allah said: "Nay! Go you both with Our Signs. Verily! We shall be with you, listening

###### 26:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأۡتِيَا فِرۡعَوۡنَ فَقُولَآ إِنَّا رَسُولُ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> And when you both come to Fir'aun (Pharaoh), say: 'We are the Messengers of the Lord of the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)

###### 26:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنۡ أَرۡسِلۡ مَعَنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ</div>

> So allow the Children of Israel to go with us

###### 26:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ أَلَمۡ نُرَبِّكَ فِينَا وَلِيدࣰ ا وَلَبِثۡتَ فِينَا مِنۡ عُمُرِكَ سِنِينَ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh)] said [to Musa (Moses)]: "Did we not bring you up among us as a child? And you did dwell many years of your life with us

###### 26:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَعَلۡتَ فَعۡلَتَكَ ٱلَّتِي فَعَلۡتَ وَأَنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ</div>

> And you did your deed, which you did (i.e. the crime of killing a man). And you are one of the ingrates

###### 26:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَعَلۡتُهَآ إِذࣰ ا وَأَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلضَّآلِّينَ</div>

> Musa (Moses) said: "I did it then, when I was an ignorant (as regards my Lord and His Message)

###### 26:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَفَرَرۡتُ مِنكُمۡ لَمَّا خِفۡتُكُمۡ فَوَهَبَ لِي رَبِّي حُكۡمࣰ ا وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ</div>

> So I fled from you when I feared you. But my Lord has granted me Hukman (i.e. religious knowledge, right judgement of the affairs and Prophethood), and appointed me as one of the Messengers

###### 26:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتِلۡكَ نِعۡمَةࣱ تَمُنُّهَا عَلَيَّ أَنۡ عَبَّدتَّ بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ</div>

> And this is the past favour with which you reproach me, that you have enslaved the Children of Israel

###### 26:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَمَا رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh) said: "And what is the Lord of the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)

###### 26:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ</div>

> Musa (Moses) said: "Lord of the heavens and the earth, and all that is between them, if you seek to be convinced with certainty

###### 26:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لِمَنۡ حَوۡلَهُۥٓ أَلَا تَسۡتَمِعُونَ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh) said to those around: "Do you not hear (what he says)

###### 26:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبُّكُمۡ وَرَبُّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> Musa (Moses) said: "Your Lord and the Lord of your ancient fathers

###### 26:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ إِنَّ رَسُولَكُمُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أُرۡسِلَ إِلَيۡكُمۡ لَمَجۡنُونࣱ‏</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh) said: "Verily, your Messenger who has been sent to you is a madman

###### 26:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبُّ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ</div>

> Musa (Moses) said: "Lord of the east and the west, and all that is between them, if you did but understand

###### 26:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لَئِنِ ٱتَّخَذۡتَ إِلَٰهًا غَيۡرِي لَأَجۡعَلَنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمَسۡجُونِينَ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh) said: "If you choose an ilah (god) other than me, I will certainly put you among the prisoners

###### 26:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ أَوَلَوۡ جِئۡتُكَ بِشَيۡءࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> Musa (Moses) said: "Even if I bring you something manifest (and convincing)

###### 26:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَأۡتِ بِهِۦٓ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh) said: "Bring it forth then, if you are of the truthful

###### 26:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَلۡقَىٰ عَصَاهُ فَإِذَا هِيَ ثُعۡبَانࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> So [Musa (Moses)] threw his stick, and behold, it was a serpent, manifest

###### 26:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَزَعَ يَدَهُۥ فَإِذَا هِيَ بَيۡضَآءُ لِلنَّٰظِرِينَ</div>

> And he drew out his hand, and behold, it was white to all beholders

###### 26:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لِلۡمَلَإِ حَوۡلَهُۥٓ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَسَٰحِرٌ عَلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh)] said to the chiefs around him: "Verily! This is indeed a well-versed sorcerer

###### 26:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُرِيدُ أَن يُخۡرِجَكُم مِّنۡ أَرۡضِكُم بِسِحۡرِهِۦ فَمَاذَا تَأۡمُرُونَ</div>

> He wants to drive you out of your land by his sorcery, then what is it your counsel, and what do you command

###### 26:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ أَرۡجِهۡ وَأَخَاهُ وَٱبۡعَثۡ فِي ٱلۡمَدَآئِنِ حَٰشِرِينَ</div>

> They said: "Put him off and his brother (for a while), and send callers to the cities

###### 26:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَأۡتُوكَ بِكُلِّ سَحَّارٍ عَلِيمࣲ‏</div>

> To bring up to you every well-versed sorcerer

###### 26:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَجُمِعَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ لِمِيقَٰتِ يَوۡمࣲ مَّعۡلُومࣲ‏</div>

> So the sorcerers were assembled at a fixed time on a day appointed

###### 26:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقِيلَ لِلنَّاسِ هَلۡ أَنتُم مُّجۡتَمِعُونَ</div>

> And it was said to the people: "Are you (too) going to assemble

###### 26:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَعَلَّنَا نَتَّبِعُ ٱلسَّحَرَةَ إِن كَانُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ</div>

> That we may follow the sorcerers [who were on Fir'aun's (Pharaoh) religion of disbelief] if they are the winners

###### 26:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَمَّا جَآءَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ قَالُواْ لِفِرۡعَوۡنَ أَئِنَّ لَنَا لَأَجۡرًا إِن كُنَّا نَحۡنُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبِينَ</div>

> So when the sorcerers arrived, they said to Fir'aun (Pharaoh): "Will there surely be a reward for us if we are the winners

###### 26:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ نَعَمۡ وَإِنَّكُمۡ إِذࣰ ا لَّمِنَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبِينَ</div>

> He said: "Yes, and you shall then verily be of those brought near (to myself)

###### 26:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لَهُم مُّوسَىٰٓ أَلۡقُواْ مَآ أَنتُم مُّلۡقُونَ</div>

> Musa (Moses) said to them: "Throw what you are going to throw

###### 26:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَلۡقَوۡاْ حِبَالَهُمۡ وَعِصِيَّهُمۡ وَقَالُواْ بِعِزَّةِ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّا لَنَحۡنُ ٱلۡغَٰلِبُونَ</div>

> So they threw their ropes and their sticks, and said: "By the might of Fir'aun (Pharaoh), it is we who will certainly win

###### 26:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَلۡقَىٰ مُوسَىٰ عَصَاهُ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَلۡقَفُ مَا يَأۡفِكُونَ</div>

> Then Musa (Moses) threw his stick, and behold, it swallowed up all the falsehoods which they showed

###### 26:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأُلۡقِيَ ٱلسَّحَرَةُ سَٰجِدِينَ</div>

> And the sorcerers fell down prostrate

###### 26:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ ءَامَنَّا بِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> Saying: "We believe in the Lord of the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)

###### 26:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَبِّ مُوسَىٰ وَهَٰرُونَ</div>

> The Lord of Musa (Moses) and Harun (Aaron)

###### 26:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ ءَامَنتُمۡ لَهُۥ قَبۡلَ أَنۡ ءَاذَنَ لَكُمۡۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكَبِيرُكُمُ ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَكُمُ ٱلسِّحۡرَ فَلَسَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَۚ لَأُقَطِّعَنَّ أَيۡدِيَكُمۡ وَأَرۡجُلَكُم مِّنۡ خِلَٰفࣲ وَلَأُصَلِّبَنَّكُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ</div>

> Fir'aun (Pharaoh)] said: "You have believed in him before I give you leave. Surely, he indeed is your chief, who has taught you magic! So verily, you shall come to know. Verily, I will cut off your hands and your feet on opposite sides, and I will crucify you all

###### 26:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ لَا ضَيۡرَۖ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا مُنقَلِبُونَ</div>

> They said: "No harm! Surely, to our Lord (Allah) we are to return

###### 26:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَطۡمَعُ أَن يَغۡفِرَ لَنَا رَبُّنَا خَطَٰيَٰنَآ أَن كُنَّآ أَوَّلَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> Verily! We really hope that our Lord will forgive us our sins, as we are the first of the believers [in Musa (Moses) and in the Monotheism which he has brought from Allah

###### 26:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَأَوۡحَيۡنَآ إِلَىٰ مُوسَىٰٓ أَنۡ أَسۡرِ بِعِبَادِيٓ إِنَّكُم مُّتَّبَعُونَ</div>

> And We inspired Musa (Moses), saying: "Take away My slaves by night, verily, you will be pursued

###### 26:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَرۡسَلَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ فِي ٱلۡمَدَآئِنِ حَٰشِرِينَ</div>

> Then Fir'aun (Pharaoh) sent callers to (all) the cities

###### 26:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَشِرۡذِمَةࣱ قَلِيلُونَ</div>

> (Saying): "Verily! These indeed are but a small band

###### 26:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُمۡ لَنَا لَغَآئِظُونَ</div>
