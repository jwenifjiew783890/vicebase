# 36. Yaseen — Yaseen

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ يٰسٓ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 83 · **Juz':** 22, 23

**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 36:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يسٓ</div>

> Ya-Sin. [These letters are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings

###### 36:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ</div>

> By the Quran, full of wisdom (i.e. full of laws, evidences, and proofs)

###### 36:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ</div>

> Truly, you (O Muhammad SAW) are one of the Messengers

###### 36:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطࣲ مُّسۡتَقِيمࣲ‏</div>

> On a Straight Path (i.e. on Allah's religion of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 36:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَنزِيلَ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ</div>

> (This is) a Revelation sent down by the AllMighty, the Most Merciful

###### 36:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمࣰ ا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ</div>

> In order that you may warn a people whose forefathers were not warned, so they are heedless

###### 36:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَقَدۡ حَقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَىٰٓ أَكۡثَرِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> Indeed the Word (of punishment) has proved true against most of them, so they will not believe

###### 36:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا فِيٓ أَعۡنَٰقِهِمۡ أَغۡلَٰلࣰ ا فَهِيَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَذۡقَانِ فَهُم مُّقۡمَحُونَ</div>

> Verily! We have put on their necks iron collars reaching to chins, so that their heads are forced up

###### 36:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ سَدࣰّ ا وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ سَدࣰّ ا فَأَغۡشَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> And We have put a barrier before them, and a barrier behind them, and We have covered them up, so that they cannot see

###### 36:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَسَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَأَنذَرۡتَهُمۡ أَمۡ لَمۡ تُنذِرۡهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> It is the same to them whether you warn them or you warn them not, they will not believe

###### 36:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَخَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِمَغۡفِرَةࣲ وَأَجۡرࣲ كَرِيمٍ</div>

> You can only warn him who follows the Reminder (the Quran), and fears the Most Beneficent (Allah) unseen. Bear you to such one the glad tidings of forgiveness, and a generous reward (i.e. Paradise)

###### 36:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَنَكۡتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُواْ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمۡۚ وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ فِيٓ إِمَامࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, We give life to the dead, and We record that which they send before (them), and their traces [their footsteps and walking on the earth with their legs to the mosques for the five compulsory congregational prayers, Jihad (holy fighting in Allah's Cause) and all other good and evil they did, and that which they leave behind], and all things We have recorded with numbers (as a record) in a Clear Book

###### 36:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ إِذۡ جَآءَهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ</div>

> And put forward to them a similitude; the (story of the) dwellers of the town, [It is said that the town was Antioch (Antakiya)], when there came Messengers to them

###### 36:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزۡنَا بِثَالِثࣲ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ إِلَيۡكُم مُّرۡسَلُونَ</div>

> When We sent to them two Messengers, they belied them both, so We reinforced them with a third, and they said: "Verily! We have been sent to you as Messengers

###### 36:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ مَآ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا بَشَرࣱ مِّثۡلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ مِن شَيۡءٍ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا تَكۡذِبُونَ</div>

> They (people of the town) said: "You are only human beings like ourselves, and the Most Beneficent (Allah) has revealed nothing, you are only telling lies

###### 36:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ لَمُرۡسَلُونَ</div>

> The Messengers said: "Our Lord knows that we have been sent as Messengers to you

###### 36:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا عَلَيۡنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ</div>

> And our duty is only to convey plainly (the Message)

###### 36:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرۡنَا بِكُمۡۖ لَئِن لَّمۡ تَنتَهُواْ لَنَرۡجُمَنَّكُمۡ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> They (people) said: "For us, we see an evil omen from you, if you cease not, we will surely stone you, and a painful torment will touch you from us

###### 36:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ طَٰٓئِرُكُم مَّعَكُمۡ أَئِن ذُكِّرۡتُمۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ مُّسۡرِفُونَ</div>

> They (Messengers) said: "Your evil omens be with you! (Do you call it "evil omen") because you are admonished? Nay, but you are a people Musrifun (transgressing all bounds by committing all kinds of great sins, and by disobeying Allah)

###### 36:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلࣱ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ</div>

> And there came running from the farthest part of the town, a man, saying: "O my people! Obey the Messengers

###### 36:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَن لَّا يَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ أَجۡرࣰ ا وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ</div>

> Obey those who ask no wages of you (for themselves), and who are rightly guided

###### 36:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَالِيَ لَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ</div>

> And why should I not worship Him (Allah Alone) Who has created me and to Whom you shall be returned

###### 36:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَتَّخِذُ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةً إِن يُرِدۡنِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ بِضُرࣲّ لَّا تُغۡنِ عَنِّي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا وَلَا يُنقِذُونِ</div>

> Shall I take besides Him aliha (gods), if the Most Beneficent (Allah) intends me any harm, their intercession will be of no use for me whatsoever, nor can they save me

###### 36:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنِّيٓ إِذࣰ ا لَّفِي ضَلَٰلࣲ مُّبِينٍ</div>

> Then verily, I should be in plain error

###### 36:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنِّيٓ ءَامَنتُ بِرَبِّكُمۡ فَٱسۡمَعُونِ</div>

> Verily! I have believed in your Lord, so listen to me

###### 36:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قِيلَ ٱدۡخُلِ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۖ قَالَ يَٰلَيۡتَ قَوۡمِي يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> It was said (to him when the disbelievers killed him): "Enter Paradise." He said: "Would that my people knew

###### 36:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بِمَا غَفَرَ لِي رَبِّي وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ</div>

> That my Lord (Allah) has forgiven me, and made me of the honoured ones

###### 36:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِن جُندࣲ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا كُنَّا مُنزِلِينَ</div>

> And We sent not against his people after him a host from heaven, nor do We send (such a thing)

###### 36:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِن كَانَتۡ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ فَإِذَا هُمۡ خَٰمِدُونَ</div>

> It was but one Saihah (shout, etc.) and lo! They (all) were silent (dead-destroyed)

###### 36:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰحَسۡرَةً عَلَى ٱلۡعِبَادِۚ مَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ</div>

> Alas for mankind! There never came a Messenger to them but they used to mock at him

###### 36:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَا يَرۡجِعُونَ</div>

> Do they not see how many of the generations We have destroyed before them? Verily, they will not return to them

###### 36:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن كُلࣱّ لَّمَّا جَمِيعࣱ لَّدَيۡنَا مُحۡضَرُونَ</div>

> And surely, all, everyone of them will be brought before Us

###### 36:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ أَحۡيَيۡنَٰهَا وَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهَا حَبࣰّ ا فَمِنۡهُ يَأۡكُلُونَ</div>

> And a sign for them is the dead land. We gave it life, and We brought forth from it grains, so that they eat thereof

###### 36:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا جَنَّٰتࣲ مِّن نَّخِيلࣲ وَأَعۡنَٰبࣲ وَفَجَّرۡنَا فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلۡعُيُونِ</div>

> And We have made therein gardens of date-palms and grapes, and We have caused springs of water to gush forth therein

###### 36:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِيَأۡكُلُواْ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦ وَمَا عَمِلَتۡهُ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ أَفَلَا يَشۡكُرُونَ</div>

> So that they may eat of the fruit thereof, and their hands made it not. Will they not, then, give thanks

###### 36:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَزۡوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَمِمَّا لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Glory be to Him, Who has created all the pairs of that which the earth produces, as well as of their own (human) kind (male and female), and of that which they know not

###### 36:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمُ ٱلَّيۡلُ نَسۡلَخُ مِنۡهُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فَإِذَا هُم مُّظۡلِمُونَ</div>

> And a sign for them is the night, We withdraw therefrom the day, and behold, they are in darkness

###### 36:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلشَّمۡسُ تَجۡرِي لِمُسۡتَقَرࣲّ لَّهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ</div>

> And the sun runs on its fixed course for a term (appointed). That is the Decree of the All-Mighty, the All-Knowing

###### 36:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡقَمَرَ قَدَّرۡنَٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلۡعُرۡجُونِ ٱلۡقَدِيمِ</div>

> And the moon, We have measured for it mansions (to traverse) till it returns like the old dried curved date stalk

###### 36:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ يَنۢبَغِي لَهَآ أَن تُدۡرِكَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيۡلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَكُلࣱّ فِي فَلَكࣲ يَسۡبَحُونَ</div>

> It is not for the sun to overtake the moon, nor does the night outstrip the day. They all float, each in an orbit

###### 36:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَايَةࣱ لَّهُمۡ أَنَّا حَمَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ</div>

> And an Ayah (sign) for them is that We bore their offspring in the laden ship [of Nuh (Noah]

###### 36:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَخَلَقۡنَا لَهُم مِّن مِّثۡلِهِۦ مَا يَرۡكَبُونَ</div>

> And We have created for them of the like thereunto, so on them they ride

###### 36:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن نَّشَأۡ نُغۡرِقۡهُمۡ فَلَا صَرِيخَ لَهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنقَذُونَ</div>

> And if We will, We shall drown them, and there will be no shout (or helper) for them (to hear their cry for help) nor will they be saved

###### 36:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا رَحۡمَةࣰ مِّنَّا وَمَتَٰعًا إِلَىٰ حِينࣲ‏</div>

> Unless it be a mercy from Us, and as an enjoyment for a while

###### 36:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱتَّقُواْ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ</div>

> And when it is said to them: "Beware of that which is before you (worldly torments), and that which is behind you (torments in the Hereafter), in order that you may receive Mercy (i.e. if you believe in Allah's Religion Islamic Monotheism, and avoid polytheism, and obey Allah with righteous deeds)

###### 36:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا تَأۡتِيهِم مِّنۡ ءَايَةࣲ مِّنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ إِلَّا كَانُواْ عَنۡهَا مُعۡرِضِينَ</div>

> And never came an Ayah from among the Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.) of their Lord to them, but they did turn away from it

###### 36:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ أَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنُطۡعِمُ مَن لَّوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ أَطۡعَمَهُۥٓ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> And when it is said to them: "Spend of that with which Allah has provided you," those who disbelieve say to those who believe: "Shall we feed those whom, if Allah willed, He (Himself) would have fed? You are only in a plain error

###### 36:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ</div>

> And they say: "When will this promise (i.e. Resurrection) be fulfilled, if you are truthful

###### 36:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ تَأۡخُذُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَخِصِّمُونَ</div>

> They await only but a single Saihah (shout, etc.), which will seize them while they are disputing

###### 36:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ تَوۡصِيَةࣰ وَلَآ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ</div>

> Then they will not be able to make bequest, nor they will return to their family

###### 36:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَإِذَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَنسِلُونَ</div>

> And the Trumpet will be blown (i.e. the second blowing) and behold! From the graves they will come out quickly to their Lord

###### 36:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا مَنۢ بَعَثَنَا مِن مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜۗ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَصَدَقَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ</div>

> They will say: "Woe to us! Who has raised us up from our place of sleep." (It will be said to them): "This is what the Most Beneficent (Allah) had promised, and the Messengers spoke truth

###### 36:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِن كَانَتۡ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ فَإِذَا هُمۡ جَمِيعࣱ لَّدَيۡنَا مُحۡضَرُونَ</div>

> It will be but a single Saihah (shout, etc.), so behold! They will all be brought up before Us

###### 36:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسࣱ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا وَلَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ</div>

> This Day (Day of Resurrection), none will be wronged in anything, nor will you be requited anything except that which you used to do

###### 36:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي شُغُلࣲ فَٰكِهُونَ</div>

> Verily, the dwellers of the Paradise, that Day, will be busy in joyful things

###### 36:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُمۡ فِي ظِلَٰلٍ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ مُتَّكِـُٔونَ</div>

> They and their wives will be in pleasant shade, reclining on thrones

###### 36:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَهُمۡ فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةࣱ وَلَهُم مَّا يَدَّعُونَ</div>

> They will have therein fruits (of all kinds) and all that they ask for

###### 36:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَلَٰمࣱ قَوۡلࣰ ا مِّن رَّبࣲّ رَّحِيمࣲ‏</div>

> (It will be said to them): Salamun (peace be on you), a Word from the Lord (Allah), Most Merciful

###### 36:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱمۡتَٰزُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ</div>

> (It will be said): "And O you Al-Mujrimun (criminals, polytheists, sinners, disbelievers in the Islamic Monotheism, wicked evil ones, etc.)! Get you apart this Day (from the believers)

###### 36:60
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞أَلَمۡ أَعۡهَدۡ إِلَيۡكُمۡ يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ أَن لَّا تَعۡبُدُواْ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوࣱّ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> Did I not ordain for you, O Children of Adam, that you should not worship Shaitan (Satan). Verily, he is a plain enemy to you

###### 36:61
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنِ ٱعۡبُدُونِيۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطࣱ مُّسۡتَقِيمࣱ‏</div>

> And that you should worship Me [Alone Islamic Monotheism, and set up not rivals, associate-gods with Me]. That is a Straight Path

###### 36:62
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ أَضَلَّ مِنكُمۡ جِبِلࣰّ ا كَثِيرًاۖ أَفَلَمۡ تَكُونُواْ تَعۡقِلُونَ</div>

> And indeed he (Satan) did lead astray a great multitude of you. Did you not, then, understand

###### 36:63
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ</div>

> This is Hell which you were promised

###### 36:64
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱصۡلَوۡهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ</div>

> Burn therein this Day, for that you used to disbelieve

###### 36:65
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَخۡتِمُ عَلَىٰٓ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتُكَلِّمُنَآ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَتَشۡهَدُ أَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ</div>

> This Day, We shall seal up their mouths, and their hands will speak to Us, and their legs will bear witness to what they used to earn. (It is said that one's left thigh will be the first to bear the witness). [Tafsir At-Tabari, Vol. 22, Page]

###### 36:66
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَطَمَسۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنِهِمۡ فَٱسۡتَبَقُواْ ٱلصِّرَٰطَ فَأَنَّىٰ يُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> And if it had been Our Will, We would surely have wiped out (blinded) their eyes, so that they would struggle for the Path, how then would they see

###### 36:67
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَمَسَخۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِهِمۡ فَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ مُضِيࣰّ ا وَلَا يَرۡجِعُونَ</div>

> And if it had been Our Will, We could have transformed them (into animals or lifeless objects) in their places. Then they should have been unable to go forward (move about) nor they could have turned back. [As it happened with the Jews see Verse 7:166 The Quran]

###### 36:68
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَن نُّعَمِّرۡهُ نُنَكِّسۡهُ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِۚ أَفَلَا يَعۡقِلُونَ</div>

> And he whom We grant long life, We reverse him in creation (weakness after strength). Will they not then understand

###### 36:69
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا عَلَّمۡنَٰهُ ٱلشِّعۡرَ وَمَا يَنۢبَغِي لَهُۥٓۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ وَقُرۡءَانࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> And We have not taught him (Muhammad SAW) poetry, nor is it meet for him. This is only a Reminder and a plain Quran

###### 36:70
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّيُنذِرَ مَن كَانَ حَيࣰّ ا وَيَحِقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ</div>

> That he or it (Muhammad SAW or the Quran) may give warning to him who is living (a healthy minded the believer), and that Word (charge) may be justified against the disbelievers (dead, as they reject the warnings)

###### 36:71
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَا لَهُم مِّمَّا عَمِلَتۡ أَيۡدِينَآ أَنۡعَٰمࣰ ا فَهُمۡ لَهَا مَٰلِكُونَ</div>

> Do they not see that We have created for them of what Our Hands have created, the cattle, so that they are their owners

###### 36:72
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَذَلَّلۡنَٰهَا لَهُمۡ فَمِنۡهَا رَكُوبُهُمۡ وَمِنۡهَا يَأۡكُلُونَ</div>

> And We have subdued them unto them so that some of them they have for riding and some they eat

###### 36:73
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَهُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ وَمَشَارِبُۚ أَفَلَا يَشۡكُرُونَ</div>

> And they have (other) benefits from them (besides), and they get (milk) to drink, will they not then be grateful

###### 36:74
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةࣰ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ</div>

> And they have taken besides Allah aliha (gods), hoping that they might be helped (by those so called gods)

###### 36:75
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ نَصۡرَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُمۡ جُندࣱ مُّحۡضَرُونَ</div>

> They cannot help them, but they will be brought forward as a troop against those who worshipped them (at the time of Reckoning)

###### 36:76
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَا يَحۡزُنكَ قَوۡلُهُمۡۘ إِنَّا نَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَ</div>

> So let not their speech, then, grieve you (O Muhammad SAW). Verily, We know what they conceal and what they reveal

###### 36:77
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن نُّطۡفَةࣲ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> Does not man see that We have created him from Nutfah (mixed male and female discharge semen drops). Yet behold! He (stands forth) as an open opponent

###### 36:78
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَضَرَبَ لَنَا مَثَلࣰ ا وَنَسِيَ خَلۡقَهُۥۖ قَالَ مَن يُحۡيِ ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ وَهِيَ رَمِيمࣱ‏</div>

> And he puts forth for Us a parable, and forgets his own creation. He says: "Who will give life to these bones when they have rotted away and became dust

###### 36:79
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ يُحۡيِيهَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةࣲۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ خَلۡقٍ عَلِيمٌ</div>

> Say: (O Muhammad SAW) "He will give life to them Who created them for the first time! And He is the All-Knower of every creation

###### 36:80
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ ٱلۡأَخۡضَرِ نَارࣰ ا فَإِذَآ أَنتُم مِّنۡهُ تُوقِدُونَ</div>

> He, Who produces for you fire out of the green tree, when behold! You kindle therewith

###### 36:81
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَلَيۡسَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَخۡلُقَ مِثۡلَهُمۚ بَلَىٰ وَهُوَ ٱلۡخَلَّٰقُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ</div>

> Is not He, Who created the heavens and the earth Able to create the like of them? Yes, indeed! He is the All-Knowing Supreme Creator

###### 36:82
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ</div>

> Verily, His Command, when He intends a thing, is only that He says to it, "Be!" and it is

###### 36:83
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ</div>

> So Glorified is He and Exalted above all that they associate with Him, and in Whose Hands is the dominion of all things, and to Him you shall be returned
