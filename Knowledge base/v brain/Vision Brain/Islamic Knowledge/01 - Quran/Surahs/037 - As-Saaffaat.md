# 37. As-Saaffaat — Those drawn up in Ranks

###### 37:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلصَّٰٓفَّٰتِ صَفࣰّ ا</div>

> By those (angels) ranged in ranks (or rows)

###### 37:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلزَّٰجِرَٰتِ زَجۡرࣰ ا</div>

> By those (angels) who drive the clouds in a good way

###### 37:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلتَّٰلِيَٰتِ ذِكۡرًا</div>

> By those (angels) who bring the Book and the Quran from Allah to mankind [Tafsir Ibn Kathir]

###### 37:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ إِلَٰهَكُمۡ لَوَٰحِدࣱ‏</div>

> Verily your Ilah (God) is indeed One (i.e. Allah)

###### 37:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا وَرَبُّ ٱلۡمَشَٰرِقِ</div>

> Lord of the heavens and of the earth, and all that is between them, and Lord of every point of the sun's risings. (None has the right to be worshipped but Allah)

###### 37:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِزِينَةٍ ٱلۡكَوَاكِبِ</div>

> Verily! We have adorned the near heaven with the stars (for beauty)

###### 37:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحِفۡظࣰ ا مِّن كُلِّ شَيۡطَٰنࣲ مَّارِدࣲ‏</div>

> And to guard against every rebellious devil

###### 37:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا يَسَّمَّعُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡمَلَإِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ وَيُقۡذَفُونَ مِن كُلِّ جَانِبࣲ‏</div>

> They cannot listen to the higher group (angels) for they are pelted from every side

###### 37:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">دُحُورࣰ اۖ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ وَاصِبٌ</div>

> Outcast, and theirs is a constant (or painful) torment

###### 37:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَنۡ خَطِفَ ٱلۡخَطۡفَةَ فَأَتۡبَعَهُۥ شِهَابࣱ ثَاقِبࣱ‏</div>

> Except such as snatch away something by stealing and they are pursued by a flaming fire of piercing brightness

###### 37:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱسۡتَفۡتِهِمۡ أَهُمۡ أَشَدُّ خَلۡقًا أَم مَّنۡ خَلَقۡنَآۚ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُم مِّن طِينࣲ لَّازِبِۭ</div>

> Then ask them (i.e. these polytheists, O Muhammad SAW): "Are they stronger as creation, or those (others like the heavens and the earth and the mountains, etc.) whom We have created?" Verily, We created them of a sticky clay

###### 37:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ عَجِبۡتَ وَيَسۡخَرُونَ</div>

> Nay, you (O Muhammad SAW) wondered (at their insolence) while they mock (at you and at the Quran)

###### 37:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ذُكِّرُواْ لَا يَذۡكُرُونَ</div>

> And when they are reminded, they pay no attention

###### 37:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا رَأَوۡاْ ءَايَةࣰ يَسۡتَسۡخِرُونَ</div>

> And when they see an Ayah (a sign, a proof, or an evidence) from Allah, they mock at it

###### 37:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُوٓاْ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرࣱ مُّبِينٌ</div>

> And they say: "This is nothing but evident magic

###### 37:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابࣰ ا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ</div>

> When we are dead and have become dust and bones, shall we (then) verily be resurrected

###### 37:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَءَابَآؤُنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ</div>

> And also our fathers of old

###### 37:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ نَعَمۡ وَأَنتُمۡ دَٰخِرُونَ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "Yes, and you shall then be humiliated

###### 37:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ زَجۡرَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ فَإِذَا هُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ</div>

> It will be a single Zajrah [shout (i.e. the second blowing of the Trumpet)], and behold, they will be staring

###### 37:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> They will say: "Woe to us! This is the Day of Recompense

###### 37:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> (It will be said): "This is the Day of Judgement which you used to deny

###### 37:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞ٱحۡشُرُواْ ٱلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ وَأَزۡوَٰجَهُمۡ وَمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡبُدُونَ</div>

> (It will be said to the angels): "Assemble those who did wrong, together with their companions (from the devils) and what they used to worship

###### 37:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَٱهۡدُوهُمۡ إِلَىٰ صِرَٰطِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> Instead of Allah, and lead them on to the way of flaming Fire (Hell)

###### 37:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقِفُوهُمۡۖ إِنَّهُم مَّسۡـُٔولُونَ</div>

> But stop them, verily they are to be questioned

###### 37:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا لَكُمۡ لَا تَنَاصَرُونَ</div>

> What is the matter with you? Why do you not help one another (as you used to do in the world)

###### 37:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ هُمُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ مُسۡتَسۡلِمُونَ</div>

> Nay, but that Day they shall surrender

###### 37:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضࣲ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ</div>

> And they will turn to one another and question one another

###### 37:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّكُمۡ كُنتُمۡ تَأۡتُونَنَا عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ</div>

> They will say: "It was you who used to come to us from the right side [i.e. from the right side of one of us and beautify for us every evil, order us for polytheism, and stop us from the truth i.e. Islamic Monotheism and from every good deed]

###### 37:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ بَل لَّمۡ تَكُونُواْ مُؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> They will reply: "Nay, you yourselves were not believers

###### 37:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا كَانَ لَنَا عَلَيۡكُم مِّن سُلۡطَٰنِۭۖ بَلۡ كُنتُمۡ قَوۡمࣰ ا طَٰغِينَ</div>

> And we had no authority over you. Nay! But you were transgressing people (disobedient, polytheists, and disbelievers)

###### 37:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَحَقَّ عَلَيۡنَا قَوۡلُ رَبِّنَآۖ إِنَّا لَذَآئِقُونَ</div>

> So now the Word of our Lord has been justified against us, that we shall certainly (have to) taste (the torment)

###### 37:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَغۡوَيۡنَٰكُمۡ إِنَّا كُنَّا غَٰوِينَ</div>

> So we led you astray because we were ourselves astray

###### 37:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ مُشۡتَرِكُونَ</div>

> Then verily, that Day, they will (all) share in the torment

###### 37:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَفۡعَلُ بِٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> Certainly, that is how We deal with Al-Mujrimun (polytheists, sinners, criminals, the disobedient to Allah, etc)

###### 37:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُوٓاْ إِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ يَسۡتَكۡبِرُونَ</div>

> Truly, when it was said to them: La ilaha ill-Allah "(none has the right to be worshipped but Allah)," they puffed themselves up with pride (i.e. denied it)

###### 37:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَقُولُونَ أَئِنَّا لَتَارِكُوٓاْ ءَالِهَتِنَا لِشَاعِرࣲ مَّجۡنُونِۭ</div>

> And (they) said: "Are we going to abandon our aliha (gods) for the sake of a mad poet

###### 37:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ جَآءَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَصَدَّقَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ</div>

> Nay! he (Muhammad SAW) has come with the truth (i.e. Allah's Religion - Islamic Monotheism and this Quran) and he confirms the Messengers (before him who brought Allah's religion - Islamic Monotheism)

###### 37:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّكُمۡ لَذَآئِقُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡأَلِيمِ</div>

> Verily, you (pagans of Makkah) are going to taste the painful torment

###### 37:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ</div>

> And you will be requited nothing except for what you used to do (evil deeds, sins, and Allah's disobedience which you used to do in this world)

###### 37:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ</div>

> Save the chosen slaves of Allah (faithful, obedient, true believers of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 37:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ لَهُمۡ رِزۡقࣱ مَّعۡلُومࣱ‏</div>

> For them there will be a known provision (in Paradise)

###### 37:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَٰكِهُ وَهُم مُّكۡرَمُونَ</div>

> Fruits; and they shall be honoured

###### 37:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ</div>

> In the Gardens of delight (Paradise)

###### 37:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَىٰ سُرُرࣲ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ</div>

> Facing one another on thrones

###### 37:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِكَأۡسࣲ مِّن مَّعِينِۭ</div>

> Round them will be passed a cup of pure wine

###### 37:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَيۡضَآءَ لَذَّةࣲ لِّلشَّٰرِبِينَ</div>

> White, delicious to the drinkers

###### 37:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا فِيهَا غَوۡلࣱ وَلَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا يُنزَفُونَ</div>

> Neither they will have Ghoul (any kind of hurt, abdominal pain, headache, a sin, etc.) from that, nor will they suffer intoxication therefrom

###### 37:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَعِندَهُمۡ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ عِينࣱ‏</div>

> And with them will be chaste females, restraining their glances (desiring none except their husbands), with wide and beautiful eyes

###### 37:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَأَنَّهُنَّ بَيۡضࣱ مَّكۡنُونࣱ‏</div>

> (Delicate and pure) as if they were (hidden) eggs (well) preserved

###### 37:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضࣲ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ</div>

> Then they will turn to one another, mutually questioning

###### 37:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ قَآئِلࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ إِنِّي كَانَ لِي قَرِينࣱ‏</div>

> A speaker of them will say: "Verily, I had a companion (in the world)

###### 37:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَقُولُ أَءِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُصَدِّقِينَ</div>

> Who used to say: "Are you among those who believe (in resurrection after death)

###### 37:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابࣰ ا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَدِينُونَ</div>

> (That) when we die and become dust and bones, shall we indeed (be raised up) to receive reward or punishment (according to our deeds)

###### 37:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ هَلۡ أَنتُم مُّطَّلِعُونَ</div>

> (The man) said: "Will you look down

###### 37:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱطَّلَعَ فَرَءَاهُ فِي سَوَآءِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> So he looked down and saw him in the midst of the Fire

###### 37:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ تَٱللَّهِ إِن كِدتَّ لَتُرۡدِينِ</div>

> He said: "By Allah! You have nearly ruined me

###### 37:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡلَا نِعۡمَةُ رَبِّي لَكُنتُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُحۡضَرِينَ</div>

> Had it not been for the Grace of my Lord, I would certainly have been among those brought forth (to Hell)

###### 37:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَيِّتِينَ</div>

> (Allah informs about that true believer that he said): "Are we then not to die (any more)

###### 37:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَوۡتَتَنَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُعَذَّبِينَ</div>

> Except our first death, and we shall not be punished? (after we have entered Paradise)

###### 37:60
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ</div>

> Truly, this is the supreme success

###### 37:61
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِمِثۡلِ هَٰذَا فَلۡيَعۡمَلِ ٱلۡعَٰمِلُونَ</div>

> For the like of this let the workers work

###### 37:62
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَذَٰلِكَ خَيۡرࣱ نُّزُلًا أَمۡ شَجَرَةُ ٱلزَّقُّومِ</div>

> Is that (Paradise) better entertainment or the tree of Zaqqum (a horrible tree in Hell)

###### 37:63
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهَا فِتۡنَةࣰ لِّلظَّٰلِمِينَ</div>

> Truly We have made it (as) a trail for the Zalimun (polytheists, disbelievers, wrong-doers, etc)

###### 37:64
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهَا شَجَرَةࣱ تَخۡرُجُ فِيٓ أَصۡلِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> Verily, it is a tree that springs out of the bottom of Hell-fire

###### 37:65
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">طَلۡعُهَا كَأَنَّهُۥ رُءُوسُ ٱلشَّيَٰطِينِ</div>

> The shoots of its fruit-stalks are like the heads of Shayatin (devils)

###### 37:66
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّهُمۡ لَأٓكِلُونَ مِنۡهَا فَمَالِـُٔونَ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡبُطُونَ</div>
