# 38. Saad — The letter Saad

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ صٓ</div>

###### 38:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">صٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ذِي ٱلذِّكۡرِ</div>

> Sad [These letters (Sad etc.) are one of the miracles of the Quran and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings]. By the Quran full of reminding

###### 38:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي عِزَّةࣲ وَشِقَاقࣲ‏</div>

> Nay, those who desbelieve are in false pride and opposition

###### 38:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّن قَرۡنࣲ فَنَادَواْ وَّلَاتَ حِينَ مَنَاصࣲ‏</div>

> How many a generation We have destroyed before them, and they cried out when there was no longer time for escape

###### 38:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَعَجِبُوٓاْ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡۖ وَقَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا سَٰحِرࣱ كَذَّابٌ</div>

> And they (Arab pagans) wonder that a warner (Prophet Muhammad SAW) has come to them from among themselves! And the disbelievers say: "This (Prophet Muhammad SAW) is a sorcerer, a liar

###### 38:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَجَعَلَ ٱلۡأٓلِهَةَ إِلَٰهࣰ ا وَٰحِدًاۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَشَيۡءٌ عُجَابࣱ‏</div>

> Has he made the aliha (gods) (all) into One Ilah (God - Allah). Verily, this is a curious thing

###### 38:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱنطَلَقَ ٱلۡمَلَأُ مِنۡهُمۡ أَنِ ٱمۡشُواْ وَٱصۡبِرُواْ عَلَىٰٓ ءَالِهَتِكُمۡۖ إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَشَيۡءࣱ يُرَادُ</div>

> And the leaders among them went about (saying): "Go on, and remain constant to your aliha (gods)! Verily, This is a thing designed (against you)

###### 38:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا سَمِعۡنَا بِهَٰذَا فِي ٱلۡمِلَّةِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا ٱخۡتِلَٰقٌ</div>

> We have not heard (the like) of this among the people of these later days. This is nothing but an invention

###### 38:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَءُنزِلَ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلذِّكۡرُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِنَاۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي شَكࣲّ مِّن ذِكۡرِيۚ بَل لَّمَّا يَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِ</div>

> Has the Reminder been sent down to him (alone) from among us?" Nay! but they are in doubt about My Reminder (this Quran)! Nay, but they have not tasted (My) Torment

###### 38:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ عِندَهُمۡ خَزَآئِنُ رَحۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡوَهَّابِ</div>

> Or have they the treasures of the Mercy of your Lord, the All-Mighty, the Real Bestower

###### 38:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَهُم مُّلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَاۖ فَلۡيَرۡتَقُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَسۡبَٰبِ</div>

> Or is it that the dominion of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them is theirs? If so, let them ascend up with means (to the heavens)

###### 38:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">جُندࣱ مَّا هُنَالِكَ مَهۡزُومࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابِ</div>

> (As they denied Allah's Message) they will be a defeated host like the confederates of the old times (who were defeated)

###### 38:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحࣲ وَعَادࣱ وَفِرۡعَوۡنُ ذُو ٱلۡأَوۡتَادِ</div>

> Before them (were many who) belied Messengers, the people of Nuh (Noah); and 'Ad; and Fir'aun (Pharaoh) the man of stakes (with which he used to punish the people)

###### 38:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَثَمُودُ وَقَوۡمُ لُوطࣲ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ لۡـَٔيۡكَةِۚ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡأَحۡزَابُ</div>

> And Thamud, and the people of Lout (Lot), and the dwellers of the wood; such were the confederates

###### 38:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِن كُلٌّ إِلَّا كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ عِقَابِ</div>

> Not one of them but belied the Messengers, therefore My Torment was justified

###### 38:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يَنظُرُ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ مَّا لَهَا مِن فَوَاقࣲ‏</div>

> And these only wait for a single Saihah [shout (i.e. the blowing of the Trumpet by the angel Israfil Sarafil)] there will be no pause or ending thereto [till everything will perish except Allah (the only God full of Majesty, Bounty and Honour]

###### 38:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ رَبَّنَا عَجِّل لَّنَا قِطَّنَا قَبۡلَ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡحِسَابِ</div>

> They say: "Our Lord! Hasten to us Qittana (i.e. our Record of good and bad deeds so that we see it) before the Day of Reckoning

###### 38:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَا دَاوُۥدَ ذَا ٱلۡأَيۡدِۖ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ</div>

> Be patient (O Muhammad SAW) of what they say, and remember Our slave Dawud (David), endued with power. Verily, he was ever oft-returning in all matters and in repentance (toward Allah)

###### 38:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا سَخَّرۡنَا ٱلۡجِبَالَ مَعَهُۥ يُسَبِّحۡنَ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ وَٱلۡإِشۡرَاقِ</div>

> Verily, We made the mountains to glorify Our Praises with him [Dawud (David)] in the 'Ashi (i.e. after the mid-day till sunset) and Ishraq (i.e. after the sunrise till mid-day)

###### 38:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلطَّيۡرَ مَحۡشُورَةࣰۖ كُلࣱّ لَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابࣱ‏</div>

> And (so did) the birds assembled: all with him [Dawud (David)] did turn (to Allah i.e. glorified His Praises)

###### 38:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَشَدَدۡنَا مُلۡكَهُۥ وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ وَفَصۡلَ ٱلۡخِطَابِ</div>

> We made his kingdom strong and gave him Al-Hikmah (Prophethood, etc.) and sound judgement in speech and decision

###### 38:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَهَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ نَبَؤُاْ ٱلۡخَصۡمِ إِذۡ تَسَوَّرُواْ ٱلۡمِحۡرَابَ</div>

> And has the news of the litigants reached you? When they climbed over the wall into (his) Mihrab (a praying place or a private room)

###### 38:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَىٰ دَاوُۥدَ فَفَزِعَ مِنۡهُمۡۖ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡۖ خَصۡمَانِ بَغَىٰ بَعۡضُنَا عَلَىٰ بَعۡضࣲ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَنَا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تُشۡطِطۡ وَٱهۡدِنَآ إِلَىٰ سَوَآءِ ٱلصِّرَٰطِ</div>

> When they entered in upon Dawud (David), he was terrified of them, they said: "Fear not! (We are) two litigants, one of whom has wronged the other, therefore judge between us with truth, and treat us not with injustice, and guide us to the Right Way

###### 38:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذَآ أَخِي لَهُۥ تِسۡعࣱ وَتِسۡعُونَ نَعۡجَةࣰ وَلِيَ نَعۡجَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ فَقَالَ أَكۡفِلۡنِيهَا وَعَزَّنِي فِي ٱلۡخِطَابِ</div>

> Verily, this my brother (in religion) has ninety nine ewes, while I have (only) one ewe, and he says: "Hand it over to me, and he overpowered me in speech

###### 38:24 🕋
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لَقَدۡ ظَلَمَكَ بِسُؤَالِ نَعۡجَتِكَ إِلَىٰ نِعَاجِهِۦۖ وَإِنَّ كَثِيرࣰ ا مِّنَ ٱلۡخُلَطَآءِ لَيَبۡغِي بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضٍ إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ وَقَلِيلࣱ مَّا هُمۡۗ وَظَنَّ دَاوُۥدُ أَنَّمَا فَتَنَّٰهُ فَٱسۡتَغۡفَرَ رَبَّهُۥ وَخَرَّۤ رَاكِعࣰ اۤ وَأَنَابَ۩</div>

> Dawud (David)] said (immediately without listening to the opponent): "He has wronged you in demanding your ewe in addition to his ewes. And, verily, many partners oppress one another, except those who believe and do righteous good deeds, and they are few." And Dawud (David) guessed that We have tried him and he sought Forgiveness of his Lord, and he fell down prostrate and turned (to Allah) in repentance

###### 38:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَغَفَرۡنَا لَهُۥ ذَٰلِكَۖ وَإِنَّ لَهُۥ عِندَنَا لَزُلۡفَىٰ وَحُسۡنَ مَـَٔابࣲ‏</div>

> So We forgave him that, and verily, for him is a near access to Us, and a good place of (final) return (Paradise)

###### 38:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰدَاوُۥدُ إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰكَ خَلِيفَةࣰ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ فَٱحۡكُم بَيۡنَ ٱلنَّاسِ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَا تَتَّبِعِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ فَيُضِلَّكَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَضِلُّونَ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ لَهُمۡ عَذَابࣱ شَدِيدُۢ بِمَا نَسُواْ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡحِسَابِ</div>

> O Dawud (David)! Verily! We have placed you as a successor on earth, so judge you between men in truth (and justice) and follow not your desire for it will mislead you from the Path of Allah. Verily! Those who wander astray from the Path of Allah (shall) have a severe torment, because they forgot the Day of Reckoning

###### 38:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا بَٰطِلࣰ اۚ ذَٰلِكَ ظَنُّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْۚ فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ</div>

> And We created not the heaven and the earth and all that is between them without purpose! That is the consideration of those who disbelieve! Then woe to those who disbelieve (in Islamic Monotheism) from the Fire

###### 38:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ نَجۡعَلُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ كَٱلۡمُفۡسِدِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ نَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ كَٱلۡفُجَّارِ</div>

> Shall We treat those who believe (in the Oneness of Allah Islamic Monotheism) and do righteous good deeds, as Mufsidun (those who associate partners in worship with Allah and commit crimes) on earth? Or shall We treat the Muttaqun (pious - see V. 2:2), as the Fujjar (criminals, disbelievers, wicked, etc)

###### 38:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَيۡكَ مُبَٰرَكࣱ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓاْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ</div>

> (This is) a Book (the Quran) which We have sent down to you, full of blessings that they may ponder over its Verses, and that men of understanding may remember

###### 38:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوَهَبۡنَا لِدَاوُۥدَ سُلَيۡمَٰنَۚ نِعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابٌ</div>

> And to Dawud (David) We gave Sulaiman (Solomon). How excellent (a) slave! Verily, he was ever oft-returning in repentance (to Us)

###### 38:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ عُرِضَ عَلَيۡهِ بِٱلۡعَشِيِّ ٱلصَّٰفِنَٰتُ ٱلۡجِيَادُ</div>

> When there were displayed before him, in the afternoon, well trained horses of the highest breed [for Jihad (holy fighting in Allah's Cause]

###### 38:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالَ إِنِّيٓ أَحۡبَبۡتُ حُبَّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّي حَتَّىٰ تَوَارَتۡ بِٱلۡحِجَابِ</div>

> And he said: "Alas! I did love the good (these horses) instead of remembering my Lord (in my 'Asr prayer)" till the time was over, and (the sun) had hidden in the veil (of night)

###### 38:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رُدُّوهَا عَلَيَّۖ فَطَفِقَ مَسۡحَۢا بِٱلسُّوقِ وَٱلۡأَعۡنَاقِ</div>

> Then he said "Bring them (horses) back to me." Then he began to pass his hand over their legs and their necks (till the end of the display)

###### 38:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا سُلَيۡمَٰنَ وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا عَلَىٰ كُرۡسِيِّهِۦ جَسَدࣰ ا ثُمَّ أَنَابَ</div>

> And, indeed We did try Sulaiman (Solomon) and We placed on his throne Jasadan (a devil, so he lost his kingdom for a while) but he did return (to his throne and kingdom by the Grace of Allah and he did return) to Allah with obedience and in repentance

###### 38:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَهَبۡ لِي مُلۡكࣰ ا لَّا يَنۢبَغِي لِأَحَدࣲ مِّنۢ بَعۡدِيٓۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ</div>

> He said: "My Lord! Forgive me, and bestow upon me a kingdom such as shall not belong to any other after me: Verily, You are the Bestower

###### 38:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَخَّرۡنَا لَهُ ٱلرِّيحَ تَجۡرِي بِأَمۡرِهِۦ رُخَآءً حَيۡثُ أَصَابَ</div>

> So, We subjected to him the wind, it blew gently to his order whithersoever he willed

###### 38:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلشَّيَٰطِينَ كُلَّ بَنَّآءࣲ وَغَوَّاصࣲ‏</div>

> And also the Shayatin (devils) from the jinns (including) every kind of builder and diver

###### 38:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَاخَرِينَ مُقَرَّنِينَ فِي ٱلۡأَصۡفَادِ</div>

> And also others bound in fetters

###### 38:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا عَطَآؤُنَا فَٱمۡنُنۡ أَوۡ أَمۡسِكۡ بِغَيۡرِ حِسَابࣲ‏</div>

> Saying of Allah to Sulaiman (Solomon)]: "This is Our gift, so spend you or withhold, no account will be asked

###### 38:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ لَهُۥ عِندَنَا لَزُلۡفَىٰ وَحُسۡنَ مَـَٔابࣲ‏</div>

> And verily, he enjoyed a near access to Us, and a good final return (Paradise)

###### 38:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرۡ عَبۡدَنَآ أَيُّوبَ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنُ بِنُصۡبࣲ وَعَذَابٍ</div>

> And remember Our slave Ayub (Job), when he invoked his Lord (saying): "Verily! Shaitan (Satan) has touched me with distress (by losing my health) and torment (by losing my wealth)

###### 38:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱرۡكُضۡ بِرِجۡلِكَۖ هَٰذَا مُغۡتَسَلُۢ بَارِدࣱ وَشَرَابࣱ‏</div>

> (Allah said to him): "Strike the ground with your foot: This is a spring of water to wash in, cool and a (refreshing) drink

###### 38:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوَهَبۡنَا لَهُۥٓ أَهۡلَهُۥ وَمِثۡلَهُم مَّعَهُمۡ رَحۡمَةࣰ مِّنَّا وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ</div>

> And We gave him (back) his family, and along with them the like thereof, as a Mercy from Us, and a Reminder for those who understand

###### 38:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَخُذۡ بِيَدِكَ ضِغۡثࣰ ا فَٱضۡرِب بِّهِۦ وَلَا تَحۡنَثۡۗ إِنَّا وَجَدۡنَٰهُ صَابِرࣰ اۚ نِّعۡمَ ٱلۡعَبۡدُ إِنَّهُۥٓ أَوَّابࣱ‏</div>

> And take in your hand a bundle of thin grass and strike therewith (your wife), and break not your oath. Truly! We found him patient. How excellent (a) slave! Verily, he was ever oft-returning in repentance (to Us)

###### 38:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرۡ عِبَٰدَنَآ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَإِسۡحَٰقَ وَيَعۡقُوبَ أُوْلِي ٱلۡأَيۡدِي وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرِ</div>

> And remember Our slaves, Ibrahim (Abraham), Ishaque (Isaac), and Ya'qub (Jacob), (all) owners of strength (in worshipping Us) and (also) of religious understanding

###### 38:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَخۡلَصۡنَٰهُم بِخَالِصَةࣲ ذِكۡرَى ٱلدَّارِ</div>

> Verily, We did choose them by granting them (a good thing, i.e.) the remembrance of the home [in the Hereafter and they used to make the people remember it, and also they used to invite the people to obey Allah and to do good deeds for the Hereafter]

###### 38:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُمۡ عِندَنَا لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُصۡطَفَيۡنَ ٱلۡأَخۡيَارِ</div>

> And they are with Us, verily, of the chosen and the best

###### 38:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرۡ إِسۡمَٰعِيلَ وَٱلۡيَسَعَ وَذَا ٱلۡكِفۡلِۖ وَكُلࣱّ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَخۡيَارِ</div>

> And remember Isma'il (Ishmael), Al-Yasa'a (Elisha), and Dhul-Kifl (Isaiah), all are among the best

###### 38:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا ذِكۡرࣱۚ وَإِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ لَحُسۡنَ مَـَٔابࣲ‏</div>

> This is a Reminder, and verily, for the Muttaqun (pious and righteous persons - see V. 2:2) is a good final return (Paradise)

###### 38:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">جَنَّٰتِ عَدۡنࣲ مُّفَتَّحَةࣰ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَبۡوَٰبُ</div>

> Adn (Edn) Paradise (everlasting Gardens), whose doors will be open for them, [It is said (in Tafsir At-Tabari, Part 23, Page 174) that one can speak to the doors, just one tells it to open and close, and it will open or close as it is ordered

###### 38:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا يَدۡعُونَ فِيهَا بِفَٰكِهَةࣲ كَثِيرَةࣲ وَشَرَابࣲ‏</div>

> Therein they will recline; therein they will call for fruits in abundance and drinks

###### 38:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَعِندَهُمۡ قَٰصِرَٰتُ ٱلطَّرۡفِ أَتۡرَابٌ</div>

> And beside them will be chaste females (virgins) restraining their glances only for their husbands, (and) of equal ages

###### 38:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡحِسَابِ</div>

> This it is what you (Al-Muttaqun - the pious) are promised for the Day of Reckoning

###### 38:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَرِزۡقُنَا مَا لَهُۥ مِن نَّفَادٍ</div>

> (It will be said to them)! Verily, this is Our Provision which will never finish

###### 38:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَاۚ وَإِنَّ لِلطَّٰغِينَ لَشَرَّ مَـَٔابࣲ‏</div>

> This is so! And for the Taghun (transgressors, disobedient to Allah and His Messenger - disbelievers in the Oneness of Allah, criminals, etc.), will be an evil final return (Fire)

###### 38:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">جَهَنَّمَ يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمِهَادُ</div>

> Hell! Where they will burn, and worst (indeed) is that place to rest

###### 38:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا فَلۡيَذُوقُوهُ حَمِيمࣱ وَغَسَّاقࣱ‏</div>

> This is so! Then let them taste it, a boiling fluid and dirty wound discharges

###### 38:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَاخَرُ مِن شَكۡلِهِۦٓ أَزۡوَٰجٌ</div>

> And other torments of similar kind, all together

###### 38:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا فَوۡجࣱ مُّقۡتَحِمࣱ مَّعَكُمۡ لَا مَرۡحَبَۢا بِهِمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ صَالُواْ ٱلنَّارِ</div>

> This is a troop entering with you (in Hell), no welcome for them! Verily, they shall burn in the Fire

###### 38:60
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ لَا مَرۡحَبَۢا بِكُمۡۖ أَنتُمۡ قَدَّمۡتُمُوهُ لَنَاۖ فَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡقَرَارُ</div>

> (The followers of the misleaders will say): "Nay, you (too)! No welcome for you! It is you (misleaders) who brought this upon us (because you misled us in the world), so evil is this place to stay in

###### 38:61
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ رَبَّنَا مَن قَدَّمَ لَنَا هَٰذَا فَزِدۡهُ عَذَابࣰ ا ضِعۡفࣰ ا فِي ٱلنَّارِ</div>

> They will say: "Our Lord! Whoever brought this upon us, add to him a double torment in the Fire

###### 38:62
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ مَا لَنَا لَا نَرَىٰ رِجَالࣰ ا كُنَّا نَعُدُّهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَشۡرَارِ</div>

> And they will say: "What is the matter with us that we see not men whom we used to count among the bad ones

###### 38:63
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَتَّخَذۡنَٰهُمۡ سِخۡرِيًّا أَمۡ زَاغَتۡ عَنۡهُمُ ٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرُ</div>

> Did we take them as an object of mockery, or have (our) eyes failed to perceive them

###### 38:64
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ لَحَقࣱّ تَخَاصُمُ أَهۡلِ ٱلنَّارِ</div>

> Verily, that is the very truth, the mutual dispute of the people of the Fire

###### 38:65
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ مُنذِرࣱۖ وَمَا مِنۡ إِلَٰهٍ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّارُ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "I am only a warner and there is no Ilah (God) except Allah (none has the right to be worshipped but Allah) the One, the Irresistible

###### 38:66
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفَّٰرُ</div>

> The Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, the All-Mighty, the Oft-Forgiving

###### 38:67
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ هُوَ نَبَؤٌاْ عَظِيمٌ</div>

> Say: "That (this Quran) is a great news

###### 38:68
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنتُمۡ عَنۡهُ مُعۡرِضُونَ</div>

> From which you turn away

###### 38:69
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا كَانَ لِيَ مِنۡ عِلۡمِۭ بِٱلۡمَلَإِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰٓ إِذۡ يَخۡتَصِمُونَ</div>

> I had no knowledge of the chiefs (angels) on high when they were disputing and discussing (about the creation of Adam)

###### 38:70
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِن يُوحَىٰٓ إِلَيَّ إِلَّآ أَنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينٌ</div>

> Only this has been inspired to me, that I am a plain warner

###### 38:71
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّي خَٰلِقُۢ بَشَرࣰ ا مِّن طِينࣲ‏</div>

> (Remember) when your Lord said to the angels: "Truly, I am going to create man from clay

###### 38:72
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا سَوَّيۡتُهُۥ وَنَفَخۡتُ فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِي فَقَعُواْ لَهُۥ سَٰجِدِينَ</div>

> So when I have fashioned him and breathed into him (his) soul created by Me, then you fall down prostrate to him

###### 38:73
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَجَدَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ كُلُّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعُونَ</div>

> So the angels prostrated themselves, all of them

###### 38:74
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّآ إِبۡلِيسَ ٱسۡتَكۡبَرَ وَكَانَ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ</div>

> Except Iblis (Satan) he was proud and was one of the disbelievers

###### 38:75
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ يَٰٓإِبۡلِيسُ مَا مَنَعَكَ أَن تَسۡجُدَ لِمَا خَلَقۡتُ بِيَدَيَّۖ أَسۡتَكۡبَرۡتَ أَمۡ كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَالِينَ</div>

> (Allah) said: "O Iblis (Satan)! What prevents you from prostrating yourself to one whom I have created with Both My Hands. Are you too proud (to fall prostrate to Adam) or are you one of the high exalted

###### 38:76
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ أَنَا۠ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡهُ خَلَقۡتَنِي مِن نَّارࣲ وَخَلَقۡتَهُۥ مِن طِينࣲ‏</div>

> Iblis (Satan)] said: "I am better than he, You created me from fire, and You created him from clay

###### 38:77
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَٱخۡرُجۡ مِنۡهَا فَإِنَّكَ رَجِيمࣱ‏</div>

> (Allah) said: "Then get out from here, for verily, you are outcast

###### 38:78
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكَ لَعۡنَتِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> And verily!, My Curse is on you till the Day of Recompense

###### 38:79
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ فَأَنظِرۡنِيٓ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ يُبۡعَثُونَ</div>

> Iblis (Satan)] said: "My Lord! Give me then respite till the Day the (dead) are resurrected

###### 38:80
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَإِنَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُنظَرِينَ</div>

> (Allah) said: "Verily! You are of those allowed respite

###### 38:81
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡوَقۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡلُومِ</div>

> Till the Day of the time appointed

###### 38:82
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَبِعِزَّتِكَ لَأُغۡوِيَنَّهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ</div>

> Iblis (Satan)] said: "By Your Might, then I will surely mislead them all

###### 38:83
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنۡهُمُ ٱلۡمُخۡلَصِينَ</div>

> Except Your chosen slaves amongst them (faithful, obedient, true believers of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 38:84
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ فَٱلۡحَقُّ وَٱلۡحَقَّ أَقُولُ</div>

> (Allah) said: "The Truth is, and the Truth I say

###### 38:85
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَأَمۡلَأَنَّ جَهَنَّمَ مِنكَ وَمِمَّن تَبِعَكَ مِنۡهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ</div>

> That I will fill Hell with you [Iblis (Satan)] and those of them (mankind) that follow you, together

###### 38:86
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ مَآ أَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۡ أَجۡرࣲ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ مِنَ ٱلۡمُتَكَلِّفِينَ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "No wage do I ask of you for this (the Quran), nor am I one of the Mutakallifun (those who pretend and fabricate things which do not exist)

###### 38:87
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> It (this Quran) is only a Reminder for all the 'Alamin (mankind and jinns)

###### 38:88
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَتَعۡلَمُنَّ نَبَأَهُۥ بَعۡدَ حِينِۭ</div>

> And you shall certainly know the truth of it after a while
