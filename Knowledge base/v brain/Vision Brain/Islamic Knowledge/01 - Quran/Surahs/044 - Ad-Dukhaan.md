# 44. Ad-Dukhaan — The Smoke

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الدُّخَانِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 59 · **Juz':** 25

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 44:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حمٓ</div>

> Ha-Mim. [These letters are one of the miracles of the Quran and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings

###### 44:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ</div>

> By the manifest Book (this Quran) that makes things clear

###### 44:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةࣲ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُنذِرِينَ</div>

> We sent it (this Quran) down on a blessed night [(i.e. night of Qadr, Surah No: 97) in the month of Ramadan,, the 9th month of the Islamic calendar]. Verily, We are ever warning [mankind that Our Torment will reach those who disbelieve in Our Oneness of Lordship and in Our Oneness of worship]

###### 44:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِيهَا يُفۡرَقُ كُلُّ أَمۡرٍ حَكِيمٍ</div>

> Therein (that night) is decreed every matter of ordainments

###### 44:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡرࣰ ا مِّنۡ عِندِنَآۚ إِنَّا كُنَّا مُرۡسِلِينَ</div>

> Amran (i.e. a Command or this Quran or the Decree of every matter) from Us. Verily, We are ever sending (the Messengers)

###### 44:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَحۡمَةࣰ مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ</div>

> (As) a Mercy from your Lord. Verily! He is the All-Hearer, the All-Knower

###### 44:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ</div>

> The Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, if you (but) have a faith with certainty

###### 44:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ يُحۡيِۦ وَيُمِيتُۖ رَبُّكُمۡ وَرَبُّ ءَابَآئِكُمُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> La ilaha illa Huwa (none has the right to be worshipped but He). It is He Who gives life and causes death, your Lord and the Lord of your fore-fathers

###### 44:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي شَكࣲّ يَلۡعَبُونَ</div>

> Nay! They play in doubt

###### 44:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ يَوۡمَ تَأۡتِي ٱلسَّمَآءُ بِدُخَانࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> Then wait you for the Day when the sky will bring forth a visible smoke

###### 44:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَغۡشَى ٱلنَّاسَۖ هَٰذَا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> Covering the people, this is a painful torment

###### 44:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَّبَّنَا ٱكۡشِفۡ عَنَّا ٱلۡعَذَابَ إِنَّا مُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> (They will say): "Our Lord! Remove the torment from us, really we shall become believers

###### 44:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنَّىٰ لَهُمُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ وَقَدۡ جَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> How can there be for them an admonition (at the time when the torment has reached them), when a Messenger explaining things clearly has already come to them

###### 44:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ تَوَلَّوۡاْ عَنۡهُ وَقَالُواْ مُعَلَّمࣱ مَّجۡنُونٌ</div>

> Then they had turned away from him (Messenger Muhammad SAW) and said: "One (Muhammad SAW) taught (by a human being), a madman

###### 44:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا كَاشِفُواْ ٱلۡعَذَابِ قَلِيلًاۚ إِنَّكُمۡ عَآئِدُونَ</div>

> Verily, We shall remove the torment for a while. Verily! You will revert

###### 44:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ نَبۡطِشُ ٱلۡبَطۡشَةَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ إِنَّا مُنتَقِمُونَ</div>

> On the Day when We shall seize you with the greatest grasp. Verily, We will exact retribution

###### 44:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَلَقَدۡ فَتَنَّا قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَجَآءَهُمۡ رَسُولࣱ كَرِيمٌ</div>

> And indeed We tried before them Fir'aun's (Pharaoh) people, when there came to them a noble Messenger [i.e. Musa (Moses]

###### 44:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنۡ أَدُّوٓاْ إِلَيَّ عِبَادَ ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ رَسُولٌ أَمِينࣱ‏</div>

> Saying: "Restore to me the slaves of Allah (i.e. the Children of Israel). Verily! I am to you a Messenger worthy of all trust

###### 44:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَن لَّا تَعۡلُواْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّيٓ ءَاتِيكُم بِسُلۡطَٰنࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> And exalt not (yourselves) against Allah. Truly, I have come to you with a manifest authority

###### 44:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنِّي عُذۡتُ بِرَبِّي وَرَبِّكُمۡ أَن تَرۡجُمُونِ</div>

> And truly, I seek refuge in my Lord and your Lord, lest you stone me (or call me a sorcerer or kill me)

###### 44:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن لَّمۡ تُؤۡمِنُواْ لِي فَٱعۡتَزِلُونِ</div>

> But if you believe me not, then keep away from me and leave me alone

###### 44:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ قَوۡمࣱ مُّجۡرِمُونَ</div>

> (But they were aggressive), so he [Musa (Moses)] called upon his Lord (saying): "These are indeed the people who are Mujrimun (disbelievers, polytheists, sinners, criminals, etc)

###### 44:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَسۡرِ بِعِبَادِي لَيۡلًا إِنَّكُم مُّتَّبَعُونَ</div>

> (Allah said): "Depart you with My slaves by night. Surely, you will be pursued

###### 44:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱتۡرُكِ ٱلۡبَحۡرَ رَهۡوًاۖ إِنَّهُمۡ جُندࣱ مُّغۡرَقُونَ</div>

> And leave the sea as it is (quiet and divided). Verily, They are a host to be drowned

###### 44:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَمۡ تَرَكُواْ مِن جَنَّٰتࣲ وَعُيُونࣲ‏</div>

> How many of gardens and springs do they [Fir'aun's (Pharaoh) people] left

###### 44:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَزُرُوعࣲ وَمَقَامࣲ كَرِيمࣲ‏</div>

> And green crops (fields etc.) and goodly places

###### 44:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَعۡمَةࣲ كَانُواْ فِيهَا فَٰكِهِينَ</div>

> And comforts of life wherein they used to take delight

###### 44:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَۖ وَأَوۡرَثۡنَٰهَا قَوۡمًا ءَاخَرِينَ</div>

> Thus (it was)! And We made other people inherit them (i.e. We made the Children of Israel to inherit the kingdom of Egypt)

###### 44:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا بَكَتۡ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ وَٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُنظَرِينَ</div>

> And the heavens and the earth wept not for them, nor were they given a respite

###### 44:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ نَجَّيۡنَا بَنِيٓ إِسۡرَٰٓءِيلَ مِنَ ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلۡمُهِينِ</div>

> And indeed We saved the Children of Israel from the humiliating torment

###### 44:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِن فِرۡعَوۡنَۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ عَالِيࣰ ا مِّنَ ٱلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ</div>

> From Fir'aun (Pharaoh); Verily! He was arrogant and was of the Musrifun (those who transgress beyond bound in spending and other things and commit great sins)

###### 44:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدِ ٱخۡتَرۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ عِلۡمٍ عَلَى ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> And We chose them (the Children of Israel) above the 'Alamin (mankind, and jinns) [during the time of Musa (Moses)] with knowledge

###### 44:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَاتَيۡنَٰهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓيَٰتِ مَا فِيهِ بَلَٰٓؤࣱ اْ مُّبِينٌ</div>

> And granted them signs in which there was a plain trial

###### 44:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَيَقُولُونَ</div>

> Verily, these (Quraish) people are saying

###### 44:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّا مَوۡتَتُنَا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمُنشَرِينَ</div>

> There is nothing but our first death, and we shall not be resurrected

###### 44:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأۡتُواْ بِـَٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ</div>

> Then bring back our fore-fathers, if you speak the truth

###### 44:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَهُمۡ خَيۡرٌ أَمۡ قَوۡمُ تُبَّعࣲ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَٰهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ مُجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> Are they better or the people of Tubba' and those before them? We destroyed them because they were indeed Mujrimun (disbelievers, polytheists, sinners, criminals, etc)

###### 44:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ</div>

> And We created not the heavens and the earth, and all that is between them, for mere play

###### 44:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا خَلَقۡنَٰهُمَآ إِلَّا بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> We created them not except with truth (i.e. to examine and test those who are obedient and those who are disobedient and then reward the obedient ones and punish the disobedient ones), but most of them know not

###### 44:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ مِيقَٰتُهُمۡ أَجۡمَعِينَ</div>

> Verily, the Day of Judgement (when Allah will judge between the creatures) is the time appointed for all of them

###### 44:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ لَا يُغۡنِي مَوۡلًى عَن مَّوۡلࣰ ى شَيۡـࣰٔ ا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ</div>

> The Day when Maulan (a near relative) cannot avail Maulan (a near relative) in aught, and no help can they receive

###### 44:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَن رَّحِمَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ</div>

> Except him on whom Allah has Mercy. Verily, He is the All-Mighty, the Most Merciful

###### 44:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ شَجَرَتَ ٱلزَّقُّومِ</div>

> Verily, the tree of Zaqqum

###### 44:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">طَعَامُ ٱلۡأَثِيمِ</div>

> Will be the food of the sinners

###### 44:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ يَغۡلِي فِي ٱلۡبُطُونِ</div>

> Like boiling oil, it will boil in the bellies

###### 44:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَغَلۡيِ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ</div>

> Like the boiling of scalding water

###### 44:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خُذُوهُ فَٱعۡتِلُوهُ إِلَىٰ سَوَآءِ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> (It will be said) "Seize him and drag him into the midst of blazing Fire

###### 44:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ صُبُّواْ فَوۡقَ رَأۡسِهِۦ مِنۡ عَذَابِ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ</div>

> Then pour over his head the torment of boiling water

###### 44:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذُقۡ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡكَرِيمُ</div>

> Taste you (this)! Verily, you were (pretending to be) the mighty, the generous

###### 44:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذَا مَا كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَمۡتَرُونَ</div>

> Verily! This is that whereof you used to doubt

###### 44:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي مَقَامٍ أَمِينࣲ‏</div>

> Verily! The Muttaqun (pious - see V. 2:2), will be in place of Security (Paradise)

###### 44:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ وَعُيُونࣲ‏</div>

> Among Gardens and Springs

###### 44:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَلۡبَسُونَ مِن سُندُسࣲ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقࣲ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ</div>

> Dressed in fine silk and (also) in thick silk, facing each other

###### 44:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَ وَزَوَّجۡنَٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينࣲ‏</div>

> So (it will be), and We shall marry them to Houris (female fair ones) with wide, lovely eyes

###### 44:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَدۡعُونَ فِيهَا بِكُلِّ فَٰكِهَةٍ ءَامِنِينَ</div>

> They will call therein for every kind of fruit in peace and security

###### 44:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا ٱلۡمَوۡتَ إِلَّا ٱلۡمَوۡتَةَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰۖ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> They will never taste death therein except the first death (of this world), and He will save them from the torment of the blazing Fire

###### 44:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَضۡلࣰ ا مِّن رَّبِّكَۚ ذَٰلِكَ هُوَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ</div>

> As a Bounty from your Lord! That will be the supreme success

###### 44:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّمَا يَسَّرۡنَٰهُ بِلِسَانِكَ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَذَكَّرُونَ</div>

> Certainly, We have made this (Quran) easy in your tongue, in order that they may remember

###### 44:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱرۡتَقِبۡ إِنَّهُم مُّرۡتَقِبُونَ</div>

> Wait then (O Muhammad SAW); Verily, they (too) are waiting
