# 50. Qaaf — The letter Qaaf

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ قٓ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 45 · **Juz':** 26

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 50:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قٓۚ وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡمَجِيدِ</div>

> Qaf. [These letters (Qaf, etc.) are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings]. By the Glorious Quran

###### 50:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ عَجِبُوٓاْ أَن جَآءَهُم مُّنذِرࣱ مِّنۡهُمۡ فَقَالَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا شَيۡءٌ عَجِيبٌ</div>

> Nay, they wonder that there has come to them a warner (Muhammad SAW) from among themselves. So the disbelievers say: "This is a strange thing

###### 50:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَءِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابࣰ اۖ ذَٰلِكَ رَجۡعُۢ بَعِيدࣱ‏</div>

> When we are dead and have become dust (shall we be resurrected?) That is a far return

###### 50:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَدۡ عَلِمۡنَا مَا تَنقُصُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مِنۡهُمۡۖ وَعِندَنَا كِتَٰبٌ حَفِيظُۢ</div>

> We know that which the earth takes of them (their dead bodies), and with Us is a Book preserved (i.e. the Book of Decrees)

###### 50:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ كَذَّبُواْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ لَمَّا جَآءَهُمۡ فَهُمۡ فِيٓ أَمۡرࣲ مَّرِيجٍ</div>

> Nay, but they have denied the truth (this Quran) when it has come to them, so they are in a confused state (can not differentiate between right and wrong)

###### 50:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَلَمۡ يَنظُرُوٓاْ إِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ كَيۡفَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا وَزَيَّنَّٰهَا وَمَا لَهَا مِن فُرُوجࣲ‏</div>

> Have they not looked at the heaven above them, how We have made it and adorned it, and there are no rifts in it

###### 50:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجِۭ بَهِيجࣲ‏</div>

> And the earth! We have spread it out, and set thereon mountains standing firm, and have produced therein every kind of lovely growth (plants)

###### 50:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَبۡصِرَةࣰ وَذِكۡرَىٰ لِكُلِّ عَبۡدࣲ مُّنِيبࣲ‏</div>

> An insight and a Reminder for every slave turning to Allah (i.e. the one who believes in Allah and performs deeds of His obedience, and always begs His pardon)

###### 50:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَزَّلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءࣰ مُّبَٰرَكࣰ ا فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا بِهِۦ جَنَّٰتࣲ وَحَبَّ ٱلۡحَصِيدِ</div>

> And We send down blessed water (rain) from the sky, then We produce therewith gardens and grain (every kind of harvests) that are reaped

###### 50:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّخۡلَ بَاسِقَٰتࣲ لَّهَا طَلۡعࣱ نَّضِيدࣱ‏</div>

> And tall date-palms, with ranged clusters

###### 50:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رِّزۡقࣰ ا لِّلۡعِبَادِۖ وَأَحۡيَيۡنَا بِهِۦ بَلۡدَةࣰ مَّيۡتࣰ اۚ كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡخُرُوجُ</div>

> A provision for (Allah's) slaves. And We give life therewith to a dead land. Thus will be the resurrection (of the dead)

###### 50:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحࣲ وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلرَّسِّ وَثَمُودُ</div>

> Denied before them (i.e. these pagans of Makkah who denied you, O Muhammad SAW) the people of Nuh (Noah), and the dwellers of Rass, and the Thamud

###### 50:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَعَادࣱ وَفِرۡعَوۡنُ وَإِخۡوَٰنُ لُوطࣲ‏</div>

> And 'Ad, and Fir'aun (Pharaoh), and the brethren of Lout (Lot)

###### 50:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأَيۡكَةِ وَقَوۡمُ تُبَّعࣲۚ كُلࣱّ كَذَّبَ ٱلرُّسُلَ فَحَقَّ وَعِيدِ</div>

> And the dwellers of the Wood, and the people of Tubba'; everyone of them denied (their) Messengers, so My Threat took effect

###### 50:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَعَيِينَا بِٱلۡخَلۡقِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ فِي لَبۡسࣲ مِّنۡ خَلۡقࣲ جَدِيدࣲ‏</div>

> Were We then tired with the first creation? Nay, They are in confused doubt about a new creation (i.e. Resurrection)

###### 50:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ وَنَعۡلَمُ مَا تُوَسۡوِسُ بِهِۦ نَفۡسُهُۥۖ وَنَحۡنُ أَقۡرَبُ إِلَيۡهِ مِنۡ حَبۡلِ ٱلۡوَرِيدِ</div>

> And indeed We have created man, and We know what his ownself whispers to him. And We are nearer to him than his jugular vein (by Our Knowledge)

###### 50:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ يَتَلَقَّى ٱلۡمُتَلَقِّيَانِ عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ قَعِيدࣱ‏</div>

> (Remember!) that the two receivers (recording angels) receive (each human being after he or she has attained the age of puberty), one sitting on the right and one on the left (to note his or her actions)

###### 50:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّا يَلۡفِظُ مِن قَوۡلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيۡهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدࣱ‏</div>

> Not a word does he (or she) utter, but there is a watcher by him ready (to record it)

###### 50:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَآءَتۡ سَكۡرَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنۡهُ تَحِيدُ</div>

> And the stupor of death will come in truth: "This is what you have been avoiding

###### 50:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡوَعِيدِ</div>

> And the Trumpet will be blown, that will be the Day whereof warning (had been given) (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 50:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَآءَتۡ كُلُّ نَفۡسࣲ مَّعَهَا سَآئِقࣱ وَشَهِيدࣱ‏</div>

> And every person will come forth along with an (angel) to drive (him), and an (angel) to bear witness

###### 50:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّقَدۡ كُنتَ فِي غَفۡلَةࣲ مِّنۡ هَٰذَا فَكَشَفۡنَا عَنكَ غِطَآءَكَ فَبَصَرُكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ حَدِيدࣱ‏</div>

> (It will be said to the sinners): "Indeed you were heedless of this, now We have removed your covering, and sharp is your sight this Day

###### 50:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ هَٰذَا مَا لَدَيَّ عَتِيدٌ</div>

> And his companion (angel) will say: "Here is (this Record) ready with me

###### 50:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلۡقِيَا فِي جَهَنَّمَ كُلَّ كَفَّارٍ عَنِيدࣲ‏</div>

> (And it will be said): "Both of you throw (Order from Allah to the two angels) into Hell, every stubborn disbeliever (in the Oneness of Allah, in His Messengers, etc)

###### 50:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّنَّاعࣲ لِّلۡخَيۡرِ مُعۡتَدࣲ مُّرِيبٍ</div>

> Hinderer of good, transgressor, doubter

###### 50:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَ فَأَلۡقِيَاهُ فِي ٱلۡعَذَابِ ٱلشَّدِيدِ</div>

> Who set up another ilah (god) with Allah, then (both of you) cast him in the severe torment

###### 50:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞قَالَ قَرِينُهُۥ رَبَّنَا مَآ أَطۡغَيۡتُهُۥ وَلَٰكِن كَانَ فِي ضَلَٰلِۭ بَعِيدࣲ‏</div>

> His companion (Satan devil)] will say: "Our Lord! I did not push him to transgress, (in disbelief, oppression, and evil deeds) but he was himself in error far astray

###### 50:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ لَا تَخۡتَصِمُواْ لَدَيَّ وَقَدۡ قَدَّمۡتُ إِلَيۡكُم بِٱلۡوَعِيدِ</div>

> Allah will say: "Dispute not in front of Me, I had already, in advance, sent you the threat

###### 50:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا يُبَدَّلُ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ لَدَيَّ وَمَآ أَنَا۠ بِظَلَّٰمࣲ لِّلۡعَبِيدِ</div>

> The Sentence that comes from Me cannot be changed, and I am not unjust (to the least) to the slaves

###### 50:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ نَقُولُ لِجَهَنَّمَ هَلِ ٱمۡتَلَأۡتِ وَتَقُولُ هَلۡ مِن مَّزِيدࣲ‏</div>

> On the Day when We will say to Hell: "Are you filled?" It will say: "Are there any more (to come)

###### 50:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأُزۡلِفَتِ ٱلۡجَنَّةُ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ غَيۡرَ بَعِيدٍ</div>

> And Paradise will be brought near to the Muttaqun (pious - see V. 2:2) not far off

###### 50:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِكُلِّ أَوَّابٍ حَفِيظࣲ‏</div>

> (It will be said): "This is what you were promised, - (it is) for those oft-returning (to Allah) in sincere repentance, and those who preserve their covenant with Allah (by obeying Him in all what He has ordered, and worship none but Allah Alone, i.e. follow Allah's Religion, Islamic Monotheism)

###### 50:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّنۡ خَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَجَآءَ بِقَلۡبࣲ مُّنِيبٍ</div>

> Who feared the Most Beneficent (Allah) in the Ghaib (unseen): (i.e. in this worldly life before seeing and meeting Him), and brought a heart turned in repentance (to Him - and absolutely free from each and every kind of polytheism)

###### 50:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱدۡخُلُوهَا بِسَلَٰمࣲۖ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡخُلُودِ</div>

> Enter you therein in peace and security; this is a Day of eternal life

###### 50:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَهُم مَّا يَشَآءُونَ فِيهَا وَلَدَيۡنَا مَزِيدࣱ‏</div>

> There they will have all that they desire, and We have more (for them, i.e. a glance at the All-Mighty, All-Majestic)

###### 50:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّن قَرۡنٍ هُمۡ أَشَدُّ مِنۡهُم بَطۡشࣰ ا فَنَقَّبُواْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ هَلۡ مِن مَّحِيصٍ</div>

> And how many a generation We have destroyed before them, who were stronger in power than them, and (when Our Torment came) they ran for a refuge in the land! Could they find any place of refuge (for them to save themselves from destruction)

###### 50:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَذِكۡرَىٰ لِمَن كَانَ لَهُۥ قَلۡبٌ أَوۡ أَلۡقَى ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَهُوَ شَهِيدࣱ‏</div>

> Verily, therein is indeed a reminder for him who has a heart or gives ear while he is heedful

###### 50:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامࣲ وَمَا مَسَّنَا مِن لُّغُوبࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed We created the heavens and the earth and all between them in six Days and nothing of fatigue touched Us

###### 50:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ قَبۡلَ طُلُوعِ ٱلشَّمۡسِ وَقَبۡلَ ٱلۡغُرُوبِ</div>

> So bear with patience (O Muhammad SAW) all that they say, and glorify the Praises of your Lord, before the rising of the sun and before (its) setting (i.e. the Fajr, Zuhr, and 'Asr prayers)

###### 50:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَأَدۡبَٰرَ ٱلسُّجُودِ</div>

> And during a part of the night (also), glorify His praises (i.e. Maghrib and 'Isha prayers), and (so likewise) after the prayers [As-Sunnah, Nawafil optional and additional prayers, and also glorify, praise and magnify Allah - Subhan Allah, Alhamdu lillah, Allahu-Akbar]

###### 50:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱسۡتَمِعۡ يَوۡمَ يُنَادِ ٱلۡمُنَادِ مِن مَّكَانࣲ قَرِيبࣲ‏</div>

> And listen on the Day when the caller will call from a near place

###### 50:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَسۡمَعُونَ ٱلصَّيۡحَةَ بِٱلۡحَقِّۚ ذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡخُرُوجِ</div>

> The Day when they will hear As-Saihah (shout, etc.) in truth, that will be the Day of coming out (from the graves i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 50:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِۦ وَنُمِيتُ وَإِلَيۡنَا ٱلۡمَصِيرُ</div>

> Verily, We it is Who give life and cause death; and to Us is the final return

###### 50:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ تَشَقَّقُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ عَنۡهُمۡ سِرَاعࣰ اۚ ذَٰلِكَ حَشۡرٌ عَلَيۡنَا يَسِيرࣱ‏</div>

> On the Day when the earth shall be cleft, from off them, (they will come out) hastening forth. That will be a gathering, quite easy for Us

###### 50:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَّحۡنُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يَقُولُونَۖ وَمَآ أَنتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِجَبَّارࣲۖ فَذَكِّرۡ بِٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ مَن يَخَافُ وَعِيدِ</div>

> We know of best what they say; and you (O Muhammad SAW) are not a tyrant over them (to force them to Belief). But warn by the Quran, him who fears My Threat
