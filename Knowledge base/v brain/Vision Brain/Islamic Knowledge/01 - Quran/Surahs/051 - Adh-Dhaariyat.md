# 51. Adh-Dhaariyat — The Winnowing Winds

###### 51:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلذَّٰرِيَٰتِ ذَرۡوࣰ ا</div>

> By (the winds) that scatter dust

###### 51:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡحَٰمِلَٰتِ وِقۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And (the clouds) that bear heavy weight of water

###### 51:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡجَٰرِيَٰتِ يُسۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And (the ships) that float with ease and gentleness

###### 51:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡمُقَسِّمَٰتِ أَمۡرًا</div>

> And those (angels) who distribute (provisions, rain, and other blessings) by (Allah's) Command

###### 51:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَصَادِقࣱ‏</div>

> Verily, that which you are promised (i.e. Resurrection in the Hereafter and receiving the reward or punishment of good or bad deeds, etc.) is surely true

###### 51:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ لَوَٰقِعࣱ‏</div>

> And verily, the Recompense is sure to happen

###### 51:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡحُبُكِ</div>

> By the heaven full of paths

###### 51:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّكُمۡ لَفِي قَوۡلࣲ مُّخۡتَلِفࣲ‏</div>

> Certainly, you have different ideas (about Muhammad SAW and the Quran)

###### 51:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُؤۡفَكُ عَنۡهُ مَنۡ أُفِكَ</div>

> Turned aside therefrom (i.e. from Muhammad SAW and the Quran) is he who is turned aside (by the Decree and Preordainment of Allah)

###### 51:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُتِلَ ٱلۡخَرَّٰصُونَ</div>

> Cursed be the liars

###### 51:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي غَمۡرَةࣲ سَاهُونَ</div>

> Who are under a cover of heedlessness (think not about the gravity of the Hereafter)

###### 51:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَسۡـَٔلُونَ أَيَّانَ يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> They ask; "When will be the Day of Recompense

###### 51:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ هُمۡ عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ يُفۡتَنُونَ</div>

> (It will be) a Day when they will be tried (i.e. burnt) over the Fire

###### 51:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذُوقُواْ فِتۡنَتَكُمۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَسۡتَعۡجِلُونَ</div>

> Taste you your trial (burning)! This is what you used to ask to be hastened

###### 51:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ وَعُيُونٍ</div>

> Verily, the Muttaqun (pious - see V. 2:2) will be in the midst of Gardens and Springs (in the Paradise)

###### 51:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَاخِذِينَ مَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡۚ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُحۡسِنِينَ</div>

> Taking joy in the things which their Lord has given them. Verily, they were before this Muhsinun (good-doers - see V)

###### 51:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَانُواْ قَلِيلࣰ ا مِّنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ مَا يَهۡجَعُونَ</div>

> They used to sleep but little by night [invoking their Lord (Allah) and praying, with fear and hope

###### 51:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبِٱلۡأَسۡحَارِ هُمۡ يَسۡتَغۡفِرُونَ</div>

> And in the hours before dawn, they were (found) asking (Allah) for forgiveness

###### 51:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقࣱّ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ</div>

> And in their properties there was the right of the beggar, and the Mahrum (the poor who does not ask the others)

###### 51:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ ءَايَٰتࣱ لِّلۡمُوقِنِينَ</div>

> And on the earth are signs for those who have Faith with certainty

###### 51:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِيٓ أَنفُسِكُمۡۚ أَفَلَا تُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> And also in your ownselves. Will you not then see

###### 51:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ رِزۡقُكُمۡ وَمَا تُوعَدُونَ</div>

> And in the heaven is your provision, and that which you are promised

###### 51:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَرَبِّ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ إِنَّهُۥ لَحَقࣱّ مِّثۡلَ مَآ أَنَّكُمۡ تَنطِقُونَ</div>

> Then, by the Lord of the heaven and the earth, it is the truth (i.e. what has been promised to you), just as it is the truth that you can speak

###### 51:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ضَيۡفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ</div>

> Has the story reached you, of the honoured guests [three angels; Jibrael (Gabriel) along with another two] of Ibrahim (Abraham)

###### 51:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ دَخَلُواْ عَلَيۡهِ فَقَالُواْ سَلَٰمࣰ اۖ قَالَ سَلَٰمࣱ قَوۡمࣱ مُّنكَرُونَ</div>

> When they came in to him, and said, "Salam, (peace be upon you)!" He answered; "Salam, (peace be upon you)," and said: "You are a people unknown to me

###### 51:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَرَاغَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ فَجَآءَ بِعِجۡلࣲ سَمِينࣲ‏</div>

> Then he turned to his household, so brought out a roasted calf [as the property of Ibrahim (Abraham) was mainly cows

###### 51:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَرَّبَهُۥٓ إِلَيۡهِمۡ قَالَ أَلَا تَأۡكُلُونَ</div>

> And placed it before them, (saying): "Will you not eat

###### 51:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَوۡجَسَ مِنۡهُمۡ خِيفَةࣰۖ قَالُواْ لَا تَخَفۡۖ وَبَشَّرُوهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ عَلِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Then he conceived a fear of them (when they ate not). They said: "Fear not." And they gave him glad tidings of an intelligent son, having knowledge (about Allah and His religion of True Monotheism)

###### 51:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَقۡبَلَتِ ٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ فِي صَرَّةࣲ فَصَكَّتۡ وَجۡهَهَا وَقَالَتۡ عَجُوزٌ عَقِيمࣱ‏</div>

> Then his wife came forward with a loud voice, she smote her face, and said: "A barren old woman

###### 51:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡحَكِيمُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ</div>

> They said: "Even so says your Lord. Verily, He is the All-Wise, the All-Knower

###### 51:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞قَالَ فَمَا خَطۡبُكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ</div>

> Ibrahim (Abraham)] said: "Then for what purpose you have come, O Messengers

###### 51:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ أُرۡسِلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ قَوۡمࣲ مُّجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> They said: "We have been sent to a people who are Mujrimun (polytheists, sinners, criminals, disbelievers in Allah)

###### 51:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِنُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حِجَارَةࣰ مِّن طِينࣲ‏</div>

> To send down upon them stones of baked clay

###### 51:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُّسَوَّمَةً عِندَ رَبِّكَ لِلۡمُسۡرِفِينَ</div>

> Marked by your Lord for the Musrifun (polytheists, criminals, sinners those who trespass Allah's set limits in evil-doings by committing great sins)

###### 51:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مَن كَانَ فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> So We brought out from therein the believers

###### 51:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا وَجَدۡنَا فِيهَا غَيۡرَ بَيۡتࣲ مِّنَ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ</div>

> But We found not there any household of the Muslims except one [i.e. Lout (Lot) and his two daughters

###### 51:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَرَكۡنَا فِيهَآ ءَايَةࣰ لِّلَّذِينَ يَخَافُونَ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَلِيمَ</div>

> And We have left there a sign (i.e. the place of the Dead Sea, well-known in Palestine) for those who fear the painful torment

###### 51:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِي مُوسَىٰٓ إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَٰهُ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ بِسُلۡطَٰنࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> And in Musa (Moses) (too, there is a sign). When We sent him to Fir'aun (Pharaoh) with a manifest authority

###### 51:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَتَوَلَّىٰ بِرُكۡنِهِۦ وَقَالَ سَٰحِرٌ أَوۡ مَجۡنُونࣱ‏</div>

> But [Fir'aun (Pharaoh)] turned away (from Belief in might) along with his hosts, and said: "A sorcerer, or a madman

###### 51:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ وَجُنُودَهُۥ فَنَبَذۡنَٰهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡيَمِّ وَهُوَ مُلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> So We took him and his hosts, and dumped them into the sea, while he was to be blamed

###### 51:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِي عَادٍ إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلرِّيحَ ٱلۡعَقِيمَ</div>

> And in 'Ad (there is also a sign) when We sent against them the barren wind

###### 51:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا تَذَرُ مِن شَيۡءٍ أَتَتۡ عَلَيۡهِ إِلَّا جَعَلَتۡهُ كَٱلرَّمِيمِ</div>

> It spared nothing that it reached, but blew it into broken spreads of rotten ruins

###### 51:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِي ثَمُودَ إِذۡ قِيلَ لَهُمۡ تَمَتَّعُواْ حَتَّىٰ حِينࣲ‏</div>

> And in Thamud (there is also a sign), when they were told: "Enjoy yourselves for a while

###### 51:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَعَتَوۡاْ عَنۡ أَمۡرِ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَخَذَتۡهُمُ ٱلصَّٰعِقَةُ وَهُمۡ يَنظُرُونَ</div>

> But they insolently defied the Command of their Lord, so the Sa'iqah overtook them while they were looking

###### 51:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ مِن قِيَامࣲ وَمَا كَانُواْ مُنتَصِرِينَ</div>

> Then they were unable to rise up, nor could they help themselves

###### 51:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَوۡمَ نُوحࣲ مِّن قَبۡلُۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَوۡمࣰ ا فَٰسِقِينَ</div>

> (So were) the people of Nuh (Noah) before them. Verily, they were a people who were Fasiqun (rebellious, disobedient to Allah)

###### 51:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيۡنَٰهَا بِأَيۡيْدࣲ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ</div>

> With power did We construct the heaven. Verily, We are Able to extend the vastness of space thereof

###### 51:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَرَشۡنَٰهَا فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡمَٰهِدُونَ</div>

> And We have spread out the earth, how Excellent Spreader (thereof) are We

###### 51:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَا زَوۡجَيۡنِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَذَكَّرُونَ</div>

> And of everything We have created pairs, that you may remember (the Grace of Allah)

###### 51:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَفِرُّوٓاْ إِلَى ٱللَّهِۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> So flee to Allah (from His Torment to His Mercy Islamic Monotheism), verily, I (Muhammad SAW) am a plain warner to you from Him

###### 51:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا تَجۡعَلُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا ءَاخَرَۖ إِنِّي لَكُم مِّنۡهُ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> And set not up (or worship) any other ilahan (god) along with Allah [Glorified be He (Alone), Exalted above all that they associate as partners with Him]. Verily, I (Muhammad SAW) am a plain warner to you from Him

###### 51:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَ مَآ أَتَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا قَالُواْ سَاحِرٌ أَوۡ مَجۡنُونٌ</div>

> Likewise, no Messenger came to those before them, but they said: "A sorcerer or a madman

###### 51:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِهِۦۚ بَلۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ طَاغُونَ</div>

> Have they (the people of the past) transmitted this saying to these (Quraish pagans)? Nay, they are themselves a people transgressing beyond bounds (in disbelief)

###### 51:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡ فَمَآ أَنتَ بِمَلُومࣲ‏</div>

> So turn away (O Muhammad SAW) from them (Quraish pagans) you are not to be blamed (as you have conveyed Allah's Message)

###### 51:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَذَكِّرۡ فَإِنَّ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ تَنفَعُ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ</div>

> And remind (by preaching the Quran, O Muhammad SAW) for verily, the reminding profits the believers

###### 51:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا خَلَقۡتُ ٱلۡجِنَّ وَٱلۡإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعۡبُدُونِ</div>

> And I (Allah) created not the jinns and humans except they should worship Me (Alone)

###### 51:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَآ أُرِيدُ مِنۡهُم مِّن رِّزۡقࣲ وَمَآ أُرِيدُ أَن يُطۡعِمُونِ</div>

> I seek not any provision from them (i.e. provision for themselves or for My creatures) nor do I ask that they should feed Me (i.e. feed themselves or My creatures)

###### 51:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلۡقُوَّةِ ٱلۡمَتِينُ</div>

> Verily, Allah is the All-Provider, Owner of Power, the Most Strong

###### 51:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ ذَنُوبࣰ ا مِّثۡلَ ذَنُوبِ أَصۡحَٰبِهِمۡ فَلَا يَسۡتَعۡجِلُونِ</div>
