# 52. At-Tur — The Mount

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الطُّوْرِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 49 · **Juz':** 27

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 52:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلطُّورِ</div>

> By the Mount

###### 52:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكِتَٰبࣲ مَّسۡطُورࣲ‏</div>

> And by the Book Inscribed

###### 52:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي رَقࣲّ مَّنشُورࣲ‏</div>

> In parchment unrolled

###### 52:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡبَيۡتِ ٱلۡمَعۡمُورِ</div>

> And by the Bait-ul-Ma'mur (the house over the heavens parable to the Ka'bah at Makkah, continuously visited by the angels)

###### 52:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّقۡفِ ٱلۡمَرۡفُوعِ</div>

> And by the roof raised high (i.e. the heaven)

###### 52:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ ٱلۡمَسۡجُورِ</div>

> And by the sea kept filled (or it will be fire kindled on the Day of Resurrection)

###### 52:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّكَ لَوَٰقِعࣱ‏</div>

> Verily, the Torment of your Lord will surely come to pass

###### 52:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّا لَهُۥ مِن دَافِعࣲ‏</div>

> There is none that can avert it

###### 52:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ تَمُورُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ مَوۡرࣰ ا</div>

> On the Day when the heaven will shake with a dreadful shaking

###### 52:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَسِيرُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ سَيۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And the mountains will move away with a (horrible) movement

###### 52:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Then woe that Day to the beliers

###### 52:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ فِي خَوۡضࣲ يَلۡعَبُونَ</div>

> Who are playing in falsehood

###### 52:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يُدَعُّونَ إِلَىٰ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ دَعًّا</div>

> The Day when they will be pushed down by force to the Fire of Hell, with a horrible, forceful pushing

###### 52:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذِهِ ٱلنَّارُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُم بِهَا تُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> This is the Fire which you used to belie

###### 52:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَسِحۡرٌ هَٰذَآ أَمۡ أَنتُمۡ لَا تُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> Is this magic, or do you not see

###### 52:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱصۡلَوۡهَا فَٱصۡبِرُوٓاْ أَوۡ لَا تَصۡبِرُواْ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ إِنَّمَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ</div>

> Taste you therein its heat, and whether you are patient of it or impatient of it, it is all the same. You are only being requited for what you used to do

###### 52:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ وَنَعِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, the Muttaqun (pious - see V. 2:2) will be in Gardens (Paradise), and Delight

###### 52:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٰكِهِينَ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ وَوَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> Enjoying in that which their Lord has bestowed on them, and (the fact that) their Lord saved them from the torment of the blazing Fire

###### 52:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ</div>

> Eat and drink with happiness because of what you used to do

###### 52:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُتَّكِـِٔينَ عَلَىٰ سُرُرࣲ مَّصۡفُوفَةࣲۖ وَزَوَّجۡنَٰهُم بِحُورٍ عِينࣲ‏</div>

> They will recline (with ease) on thrones arranged in ranks. And We shall marry them to Houris (female, fair ones) with wide lovely eyes

###### 52:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَٱتَّبَعَتۡهُمۡ ذُرِّيَّتُهُم بِإِيمَٰنٍ أَلۡحَقۡنَا بِهِمۡ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ وَمَآ أَلَتۡنَٰهُم مِّنۡ عَمَلِهِم مِّن شَيۡءࣲۚ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيِٕۭ بِمَا كَسَبَ رَهِينࣱ‏</div>

> And those who believe and whose offspring follow them in Faith, to them shall We join their offspring, and We shall not decrease the reward of their deeds in anything. Every person is a pledge for that which he has earned

###### 52:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمۡدَدۡنَٰهُم بِفَٰكِهَةࣲ وَلَحۡمࣲ مِّمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ</div>

> And We shall provide them with fruit and meat, such as they desire

###### 52:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَتَنَٰزَعُونَ فِيهَا كَأۡسࣰ ا لَّا لَغۡوࣱ فِيهَا وَلَا تَأۡثِيمࣱ‏</div>

> There they shall pass from hand to hand a (wine) cup, free from any Laghw (dirty, false, evil vague talk between them), and free from sin (because it will be legal for them to drink)

###### 52:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غِلۡمَانࣱ لَّهُمۡ كَأَنَّهُمۡ لُؤۡلُؤࣱ مَّكۡنُونࣱ‏</div>

> And there will go round boy-servants of theirs, to serve them as if they were preserved pearls

###### 52:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضࣲ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ</div>

> And some of them draw near to others, questioning

###### 52:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا كُنَّا قَبۡلُ فِيٓ أَهۡلِنَا مُشۡفِقِينَ</div>

> Saying: "Aforetime, we were afraid with our families (from the punishment of Allah)

###### 52:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيۡنَا وَوَقَىٰنَا عَذَابَ ٱلسَّمُومِ</div>

> But Allah has been gracious to us, and has saved us from the torment of the Fire

###### 52:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا كُنَّا مِن قَبۡلُ نَدۡعُوهُۖ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلۡبَرُّ ٱلرَّحِيمُ</div>

> Verily, We used to invoke Him (Alone and none else) before. Verily, He is Al-Barr (the Most Subtle, Kind, Courteous, and Generous), the Most Merciful

###### 52:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَكِّرۡ فَمَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَتِ رَبِّكَ بِكَاهِنࣲ وَلَا مَجۡنُونٍ</div>

> Therefore, remind and preach (mankind, O Muhammad SAW of Islamic Monotheism). By the Grace of Allah, you are neither a soothsayer, nor a madman

###### 52:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ شَاعِرࣱ نَّتَرَبَّصُ بِهِۦ رَيۡبَ ٱلۡمَنُونِ</div>

> Or do they say: "(Muhammad SAW is) a poet! We await for him some calamity by time

###### 52:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ تَرَبَّصُواْ فَإِنِّي مَعَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡمُتَرَبِّصِينَ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW to them): "Wait! I am with you, among the waiters

###### 52:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ تَأۡمُرُهُمۡ أَحۡلَٰمُهُم بِهَٰذَآۚ أَمۡ هُمۡ قَوۡمࣱ طَاغُونَ</div>

> Do their minds command them this [i.e. to tell a lie against you (Muhammad SAW)] or are they people exceeding the bounds (i.e. from Belief in Allah to disbelief)

###### 52:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ تَقَوَّلَهُۥۚ بَل لَّا يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> Or do they say: "He (Muhammad SAW) has forged it (this Quran)?" Nay! They believe not

###### 52:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلۡيَأۡتُواْ بِحَدِيثࣲ مِّثۡلِهِۦٓ إِن كَانُواْ صَٰدِقِينَ</div>

> Let them then produce a recital like unto it (the Quran) if they are truthful

###### 52:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ خُلِقُواْ مِنۡ غَيۡرِ شَيۡءٍ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ</div>

> Were they created by nothing, or were they themselves the creators

###### 52:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ خَلَقُواْ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَۚ بَل لَّا يُوقِنُونَ</div>

> Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Nay, but they have no firm Belief

###### 52:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ عِندَهُمۡ خَزَآئِنُ رَبِّكَ أَمۡ هُمُ ٱلۡمُصَۜيۡطِرُونَ</div>

> Or are with them the treasures of your Lord? Or are they the tyrants with the authority to do as they like

###### 52:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَهُمۡ سُلَّمࣱ يَسۡتَمِعُونَ فِيهِۖ فَلۡيَأۡتِ مُسۡتَمِعُهُم بِسُلۡطَٰنࣲ مُّبِينٍ</div>

> Or have they a stairway (to heaven), by means of which they listen (to the talks of the angels)? Then let their listener produce some manifest proof

###### 52:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَهُ ٱلۡبَنَٰتُ وَلَكُمُ ٱلۡبَنُونَ</div>

> Or has He (Allah) only daughters and you have sons

###### 52:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ تَسۡـَٔلُهُمۡ أَجۡرࣰ ا فَهُم مِّن مَّغۡرَمࣲ مُّثۡقَلُونَ</div>

> Or is it that you (O Muhammad SAW) ask a wage from them (for your preaching of Islamic Monotheism) so that they are burdened with a load of debt

###### 52:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ عِندَهُمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبُ فَهُمۡ يَكۡتُبُونَ</div>

> Or that the Ghaib (unseen) is with them, and they write it down

###### 52:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ يُرِيدُونَ كَيۡدࣰ اۖ فَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ هُمُ ٱلۡمَكِيدُونَ</div>

> Or do they intend a plot (against you O Muhammad SAW)? But those who disbelieve (in the Oneness of Allah Islamic Monotheism) are themselves in a plot

###### 52:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَهُمۡ إِلَٰهٌ غَيۡرُ ٱللَّهِۚ سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشۡرِكُونَ</div>

> Or have they an ilah (a god) other than Allah? Glorified be Allah from all that they ascribe as partners (to Him)

###### 52:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ كِسۡفࣰ ا مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ سَاقِطࣰ ا يَقُولُواْ سَحَابࣱ مَّرۡكُومࣱ‏</div>

> And if they were to see a piece of the heaven falling down, they would say: "Clouds gathered in heaps

###### 52:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَرۡهُمۡ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي فِيهِ يُصۡعَقُونَ</div>

> So leave them alone till they meet their Day, in which they will sink into a fainting (with horror)

###### 52:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ لَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُمۡ كَيۡدُهُمۡ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا وَلَا هُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ</div>

> The Day when their plotting shall not avail them at all nor will they be helped (i.e. they will receive their torment in Hell)

###### 52:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ لِلَّذِينَ ظَلَمُواْ عَذَابࣰ ا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكۡثَرَهُمۡ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> And verily, for those who do wrong, there is another punishment (i.e. the torment in this world and in their graves) before this, but most of them know not. [Tafsir At-Tabari, Vol. 27, Page]

###### 52:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ فَإِنَّكَ بِأَعۡيُنِنَاۖ وَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ حِينَ تَقُومُ</div>

> So wait patiently (O Muhammad SAW) for the Decision of your Lord, for verily, you are under Our Eyes, and glorify the Praises of your Lord when you get up from sleep

###### 52:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَسَبِّحۡهُ وَإِدۡبَٰرَ ٱلنُّجُومِ</div>

> And in the night time, also glorify His Praises, and at the setting of the stars
