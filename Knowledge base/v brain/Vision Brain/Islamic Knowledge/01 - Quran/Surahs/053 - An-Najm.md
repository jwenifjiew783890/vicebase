# 53. An-Najm — The Star

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ النَّجْمِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 62 · **Juz':** 27 · **Sajda at:** 62

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 53:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّجۡمِ إِذَا هَوَىٰ</div>

> By the star when it goes down, (or vanishes)

###### 53:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا ضَلَّ صَاحِبُكُمۡ وَمَا غَوَىٰ</div>

> Your companion (Muhammad SAW) has neither gone astray nor has erred

###### 53:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يَنطِقُ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰٓ</div>

> Nor does he speak of (his own) desire

###### 53:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا وَحۡيࣱ يُوحَىٰ</div>

> It is only an Inspiration that is inspired

###### 53:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَّمَهُۥ شَدِيدُ ٱلۡقُوَىٰ</div>

> He has been taught (this Quran) by one mighty in power [Jibrael (Gabriel]

###### 53:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذُو مِرَّةࣲ فَٱسۡتَوَىٰ</div>

> Dhu Mirrah (free from any defect in body and mind), Fastawa [then he (Jibrael - Gabriel) rose and became stable]. [Tafsir At-Tabari]

###### 53:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُوَ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ</div>

> While he [Jibrael (Gabriel)] was in the highest part of the horizon

###### 53:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ دَنَا فَتَدَلَّىٰ</div>

> Then he [Jibrael (Gabriel)] approached and came closer

###### 53:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَانَ قَابَ قَوۡسَيۡنِ أَوۡ أَدۡنَىٰ</div>

> And was at a distance of two bows' length or (even) nearer

###### 53:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَوۡحَىٰٓ إِلَىٰ عَبۡدِهِۦ مَآ أَوۡحَىٰ</div>

> So did (Allah) convey the Inspiration to His slave [Muhammad SAW through Jibrael (Gabriel]

###### 53:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا كَذَبَ ٱلۡفُؤَادُ مَا رَأَىٰٓ</div>

> The (Prophet's) heart lied not (in seeing) what he (Muhammad SAW) saw

###### 53:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَتُمَٰرُونَهُۥ عَلَىٰ مَا يَرَىٰ</div>

> Will you then dispute with him (Muhammad SAW) about what he saw [during the Mi'raj: (Ascent of the Prophet SAW over the seven heavens]

###### 53:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ نَزۡلَةً أُخۡرَىٰ</div>

> And indeed he (Muhammad SAW) saw him [Jibrael (Gabriel)] at a second descent (i.e. another time)

###### 53:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عِندَ سِدۡرَةِ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ</div>

> Near Sidrat-ul-Muntaha [lote-tree of the utmost boundary (beyond which none can pass]

###### 53:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عِندَهَا جَنَّةُ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰٓ</div>

> Near it is the Paradise of Abode

###### 53:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ يَغۡشَى ٱلسِّدۡرَةَ مَا يَغۡشَىٰ</div>

> When that covered the lote-tree which did cover it

###### 53:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا زَاغَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ وَمَا طَغَىٰ</div>

> The sight (of Prophet Muhammad SAW) turned not aside (right or left), nor it transgressed beyond (the) limit (ordained for it)

###### 53:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَقَدۡ رَأَىٰ مِنۡ ءَايَٰتِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰٓ</div>

> Indeed he (Muhammad SAW) did see, of the Greatest Signs, of his Lord (Allah)

###### 53:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱللَّٰتَ وَٱلۡعُزَّىٰ</div>

> Have you then considered Al-Lat, and Al-'Uzza (two idols of the pagan Arabs)

###### 53:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَنَوٰةَ ٱلثَّالِثَةَ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰٓ</div>

> And Manat (another idol of the pagan Arabs), the other third

###### 53:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَكُمُ ٱلذَّكَرُ وَلَهُ ٱلۡأُنثَىٰ</div>

> Is it for you the males and for Him the females

###### 53:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تِلۡكَ إِذࣰ ا قِسۡمَةࣱ ضِيزَىٰٓ</div>

> That indeed is a division most unfair

###### 53:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هِيَ إِلَّآ أَسۡمَآءࣱ سَمَّيۡتُمُوهَآ أَنتُمۡ وَءَابَآؤُكُم مَّآ أَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَا مِن سُلۡطَٰنٍۚ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّ وَمَا تَهۡوَى ٱلۡأَنفُسُۖ وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّن رَّبِّهِمُ ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ</div>

> They are but names which you have named, you and your fathers, for which Allah has sent down no authority. They follow but a guess and that which they themselves desire, whereas there has surely come to them the Guidance from their Lord

###### 53:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ مَا تَمَنَّىٰ</div>

> Or shall man have what he wishes

###### 53:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلِلَّهِ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> But to Allah belongs the last (Hereafter) and the first (the world)

###### 53:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَكَم مِّن مَّلَكࣲ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ لَا تُغۡنِي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡـًٔا إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعۡدِ أَن يَأۡذَنَ ٱللَّهُ لِمَن يَشَآءُ وَيَرۡضَىٰٓ</div>

> And there are many angels in the heavens, whose intercession will avail nothing except after Allah has given leave for whom He wills and pleases

###### 53:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ بِٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ لَيُسَمُّونَ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةَ تَسۡمِيَةَ ٱلۡأُنثَىٰ</div>

> Verily, those who believe not in the Hereafter, name the angels with female names

###### 53:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا لَهُم بِهِۦ مِنۡ عِلۡمٍۖ إِن يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلَّا ٱلظَّنَّۖ وَإِنَّ ٱلظَّنَّ لَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱلۡحَقِّ شَيۡـࣰٔ ا</div>

> While they have no knowledge thereof. They follow but a guess, and verily, guess is no substitute for the truth

###### 53:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَعۡرِضۡ عَن مَّن تَوَلَّىٰ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَلَمۡ يُرِدۡ إِلَّا ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا</div>

> Therefore withdraw (O Muhammad SAW) from him who turns away from Our Reminder (this Quran) and desires nothing but the life of this world

###### 53:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذَٰلِكَ مَبۡلَغُهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱهۡتَدَىٰ</div>

> That is what they could reach of knowledge. Verily, your Lord it is He Who knows best him who goes astray from His Path, and He knows best him who receives guidance

###### 53:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلِلَّهِ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ لِيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسَٰٓـُٔواْ بِمَا عَمِلُواْ وَيَجۡزِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَحۡسَنُواْ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَى</div>

> And to Allah belongs all that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth, that He may requite those who do evil with that which they have done (i.e. punish them in Hell), and reward those who do good, with what is best (i.e. Paradise)

###### 53:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ يَجۡتَنِبُونَ كَبَٰٓئِرَ ٱلۡإِثۡمِ وَٱلۡفَوَٰحِشَ إِلَّا ٱللَّمَمَۚ إِنَّ رَبَّكَ وَٰسِعُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِۚ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِكُمۡ إِذۡ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِذۡ أَنتُمۡ أَجِنَّةࣱ فِي بُطُونِ أُمَّهَٰتِكُمۡۖ فَلَا تُزَكُّوٓاْ أَنفُسَكُمۡۖ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَنِ ٱتَّقَىٰٓ</div>

> Those who avoid great sins (see the Quran, Verses: 6:152, 153) and Al-Fawahish (illegal sexual intercourse, etc.) except the small faults, verily, your Lord is of vast forgiveness. He knows you well when He created you from the earth (Adam), and when you were fetuses in your mothers' wombs. So ascribe not purity to yourselves. He knows best him who fears Allah and keep his duty to Him [i.e. those who are Al-Muttaqun (pious - see V]

###### 53:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي تَوَلَّىٰ</div>

> Did you (O Muhammad SAW) observe him who turned away (from Islam)

###### 53:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَعۡطَىٰ قَلِيلࣰ ا وَأَكۡدَىٰٓ</div>

> And gave a little, then stopped (giving)

###### 53:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَعِندَهُۥ عِلۡمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَهُوَ يَرَىٰٓ</div>

> Is with him the knowledge of the unseen so that he sees

###### 53:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَمۡ يُنَبَّأۡ بِمَا فِي صُحُفِ مُوسَىٰ</div>

> Or is he not informed with what is in the Pages (Scripture) of Musa (Moses)

###### 53:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِبۡرَٰهِيمَ ٱلَّذِي وَفَّىٰٓ</div>

> And of Ibrahim (Abraham) who fulfilled (or conveyed) all that (what Allah ordered him to do or convey)

###### 53:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَّا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةࣱ وِزۡرَ أُخۡرَىٰ</div>

> That no burdened person (with sins) shall bear the burden (sins) of another

###### 53:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَن لَّيۡسَ لِلۡإِنسَٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ</div>

> And that man can have nothing but what he does (good or bad)

###### 53:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّ سَعۡيَهُۥ سَوۡفَ يُرَىٰ</div>

> And that his deeds will be seen

###### 53:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ يُجۡزَىٰهُ ٱلۡجَزَآءَ ٱلۡأَوۡفَىٰ</div>

> Then he will be recompensed with a full and the best recompense

###### 53:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡمُنتَهَىٰ</div>

> And that to your Lord (Allah) is the End (Return of everything)

###### 53:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَضۡحَكَ وَأَبۡكَىٰ</div>

> And that it is He (Allah) Who makes (whom He wills) laugh, and makes (whom He wills) weep

###### 53:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَمَاتَ وَأَحۡيَا</div>

> And that it is He (Allah) Who causes death and gives life

###### 53:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ خَلَقَ ٱلزَّوۡجَيۡنِ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰ</div>

> And that He (Allah) creates the pairs, male and female

###### 53:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ إِذَا تُمۡنَىٰ</div>

> From Nutfah (drops of semen male and female discharges) when it is emitted

###### 53:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّ عَلَيۡهِ ٱلنَّشۡأَةَ ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰ</div>

> And that upon Him (Allah) is another bringing forth (Resurrection)

###### 53:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ أَغۡنَىٰ وَأَقۡنَىٰ</div>

> And that it is He (Allah) Who gives much or a little (or gives wealth and contentment)

###### 53:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ هُوَ رَبُّ ٱلشِّعۡرَىٰ</div>

> And that He (Allah) is the Lord of Sirius (the star which the pagan Arabs used to worship)

###### 53:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥٓ أَهۡلَكَ عَادًا ٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> And that it is He (Allah) Who destroyed the former 'Ad (people)

###### 53:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَثَمُودَاْ فَمَآ أَبۡقَىٰ</div>

> And Thamud (people). He spared none of them

###### 53:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَوۡمَ نُوحࣲ مِّن قَبۡلُۖ إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ هُمۡ أَظۡلَمَ وَأَطۡغَىٰ</div>

> And the people of Nuh (Noah) aforetime, verily, they were more unjust and more rebellious and transgressing [in disobeying Allah and His Messenger Nuh (Noah]

###### 53:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَةَ أَهۡوَىٰ</div>

> And He destroyed the overthrown cities [of Sodom to which Prophet Lout (Lot) was sent

###### 53:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَغَشَّىٰهَا مَا غَشَّىٰ</div>

> So there covered them that which did cover (i.e. torment with stones)

###### 53:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَبِأَيِّ ءَالَآءِ رَبِّكَ تَتَمَارَىٰ</div>

> Then which of the Graces of your Lord (O man!) will you doubt

###### 53:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا نَذِيرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلنُّذُرِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰٓ</div>

> This (Muhammad SAW) is a warner (Messenger) of the (series of) warners (Messengers) of old

###### 53:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَزِفَتِ ٱلۡأٓزِفَةُ</div>

> The Day of Resurrection draws near

###### 53:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَيۡسَ لَهَا مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ كَاشِفَةٌ</div>

> None besides Allah can avert it, (or advance it, or delay it)

###### 53:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَمِنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِ تَعۡجَبُونَ</div>

> Do you then wonder at this recital (the Quran)

###### 53:60
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَضۡحَكُونَ وَلَا تَبۡكُونَ</div>

> And you laugh at it and weep not

###### 53:61
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنتُمۡ سَٰمِدُونَ</div>

> Wasting your (precious) lifetime in pastime and amusements (singing, etc)

###### 53:62 🕋
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱسۡجُدُواْۤ لِلَّهِۤ وَٱعۡبُدُواْ۩</div>

> So fall you down in prostration to Allah, and worship Him (Alone)
