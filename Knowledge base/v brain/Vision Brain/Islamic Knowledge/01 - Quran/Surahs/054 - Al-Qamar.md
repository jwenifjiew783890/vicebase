# 54. Al-Qamar — The Moon

###### 54:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱقۡتَرَبَتِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ وَٱنشَقَّ ٱلۡقَمَرُ</div>

> The Hour has drawn near, and the moon has been cleft asunder (the people of Makkah requested Prophet Muhammad SAW to show them a miracle, so he showed them the splitting of the moon)

###### 54:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن يَرَوۡاْ ءَايَةࣰ يُعۡرِضُواْ وَيَقُولُواْ سِحۡرࣱ مُّسۡتَمِرࣱّ‏</div>

> And if they see a sign, they turn away, and say: "This is continuous magic

###### 54:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَذَّبُواْ وَٱتَّبَعُوٓاْ أَهۡوَآءَهُمۡۚ وَكُلُّ أَمۡرࣲ مُّسۡتَقِرࣱّ‏</div>

> They belied (the Verses of Allah, this Quran), and followed their own lusts. And every matter will be settled [according to the kind of deeds (for the doer of good deeds, his deeds will take him to Paradise, and similarly evil deeds will take their doers to Hell]

###### 54:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَنۢبَآءِ مَا فِيهِ مُزۡدَجَرٌ</div>

> And indeed there has come to them news (in this Quran) wherein there is (enough warning) to check (them from evil)

###### 54:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حِكۡمَةُۢ بَٰلِغَةࣱۖ فَمَا تُغۡنِ ٱلنُّذُرُ</div>

> Perfect wisdom (this Quran), but (the preaching of) warners benefit them not

###### 54:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَتَوَلَّ عَنۡهُمۡۘ يَوۡمَ يَدۡعُ ٱلدَّاعِ إِلَىٰ شَيۡءࣲ نُّكُرٍ</div>

> So (O Muhammad SAW) withdraw from them. The Day that the caller will call (them) to a terrible thing

###### 54:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خُشَّعًا أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ يَخۡرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ كَأَنَّهُمۡ جَرَادࣱ مُّنتَشِرࣱ‏</div>

> They will come forth, with humbled eyes from (their) graves as if they were locusts spread abroad

###### 54:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُّهۡطِعِينَ إِلَى ٱلدَّاعِۖ يَقُولُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ هَٰذَا يَوۡمٌ عَسِرࣱ‏</div>

> Hastening towards the caller, the disbelievers will say: "This is a hard Day

###### 54:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞كَذَّبَتۡ قَبۡلَهُمۡ قَوۡمُ نُوحࣲ فَكَذَّبُواْ عَبۡدَنَا وَقَالُواْ مَجۡنُونࣱ وَٱزۡدُجِرَ</div>

> The people of Nuh (Noah) denied (their Messenger) before them, they rejected Our slave, and said: "A madman!" and he was insolently rebuked and threatened

###### 54:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَدَعَا رَبَّهُۥٓ أَنِّي مَغۡلُوبࣱ فَٱنتَصِرۡ</div>

> Then he invoked his Lord (saying): "I have been overcome, so help (me)

###### 54:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَفَتَحۡنَآ أَبۡوَٰبَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ بِمَآءࣲ مُّنۡهَمِرࣲ‏</div>

> So We opened the gates of heaven with water pouring forth

###### 54:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَجَّرۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ عُيُونࣰ ا فَٱلۡتَقَى ٱلۡمَآءُ عَلَىٰٓ أَمۡرࣲ قَدۡ قُدِرَ</div>

> And We caused the earth to gush forth with springs. So the waters (of the heaven and the earth) met for a matter predestined

###### 54:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحَمَلۡنَٰهُ عَلَىٰ ذَاتِ أَلۡوَٰحࣲ وَدُسُرࣲ‏</div>

> And We carried him on a (ship) made of planks and nails

###### 54:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَجۡرِي بِأَعۡيُنِنَا جَزَآءࣰ لِّمَن كَانَ كُفِرَ</div>

> Floating under Our Eyes, a reward for him who had been rejected

###### 54:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَد تَّرَكۡنَٰهَآ ءَايَةࣰ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed, We have left this as a sign, then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)

###### 54:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ</div>

> Then how (terrible) was My Torment and My Warnings

###### 54:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرࣲ‏</div>

> And We have indeed made the Quran easy to understand and remember, then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)

###### 54:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ عَادࣱ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ</div>

> Ad (people) belied (their Prophet, Hud), then how (terrible) was My Torment and My Warnings

###### 54:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ رِيحࣰ ا صَرۡصَرࣰ ا فِي يَوۡمِ نَحۡسࣲ مُّسۡتَمِرࣲّ‏</div>

> Verily, We sent against them a furious wind of harsh voice on a day of evil omen and continuous calamity

###### 54:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَنزِعُ ٱلنَّاسَ كَأَنَّهُمۡ أَعۡجَازُ نَخۡلࣲ مُّنقَعِرࣲ‏</div>

> Plucking out men as if they were uprooted stems of date-palms

###### 54:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ</div>

> Then, how (terrible) was My Torment and My Warnings

###### 54:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرࣲ‏</div>

> And We have indeed made the Quran easy to understand and remember, then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)

###### 54:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِٱلنُّذُرِ</div>

> Thamud (people also) belied the warnings

###### 54:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالُوٓاْ أَبَشَرࣰ ا مِّنَّا وَٰحِدࣰ ا نَّتَّبِعُهُۥٓ إِنَّآ إِذࣰ ا لَّفِي ضَلَٰلࣲ وَسُعُرٍ</div>

> For they said: "A man! Alone from among us, that we are to follow? Truly, then we should be in error and distress or madness

###### 54:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَءُلۡقِيَ ٱلذِّكۡرُ عَلَيۡهِ مِنۢ بَيۡنِنَا بَلۡ هُوَ كَذَّابٌ أَشِرࣱ‏</div>

> Is it that the Reminder is sent to him [Prophet Salih (Saleh)] alone from among us? Nay, he is an insolent liar

###### 54:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَيَعۡلَمُونَ غَدࣰ ا مَّنِ ٱلۡكَذَّابُ ٱلۡأَشِرُ</div>

> Tomorrow they will come to know, who is the liar, the insolent one

###### 54:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا مُرۡسِلُواْ ٱلنَّاقَةِ فِتۡنَةࣰ لَّهُمۡ فَٱرۡتَقِبۡهُمۡ وَٱصۡطَبِرۡ</div>

> Verily, We are sending the she-camel as a test for them. So watch them [O Salih (Saleh)], and be patient

###### 54:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَبِّئۡهُمۡ أَنَّ ٱلۡمَآءَ قِسۡمَةُۢ بَيۡنَهُمۡۖ كُلُّ شِرۡبࣲ مُّحۡتَضَرࣱ‏</div>

> And inform them that the water is to be shared between (her and) them. Each one's right to drink being established (by turns)

###### 54:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَنَادَوۡاْ صَاحِبَهُمۡ فَتَعَاطَىٰ فَعَقَرَ</div>

> But they called their comrade and he took (a sword) and killed (her)

###### 54:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ</div>

> Then, how (terrible) was My Torment and My Warnings

###### 54:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ صَيۡحَةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ فَكَانُواْ كَهَشِيمِ ٱلۡمُحۡتَظِرِ</div>

> Verily, We sent against them a single Saihah (torment - awful cry, etc.), and they became like the dry stubble of a fold-builder

###### 54:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed, We have made the Quran easy to understand and remember, then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)

###### 54:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ قَوۡمُ لُوطِۭ بِٱلنُّذُرِ</div>

> The people of Lout (Lot) belied the warnings

###### 54:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَاصِبًا إِلَّآ ءَالَ لُوطࣲۖ نَّجَّيۡنَٰهُم بِسَحَرࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, We sent against them a violent storm of stones (which destroyed them all), except the family of Lout (Lot), whom We saved in last hour of the night

###### 54:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نِّعۡمَةࣰ مِّنۡ عِندِنَاۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي مَن شَكَرَ</div>

> As a Favour from Us, thus do We reward him who gives thanks (by obeying Us)

###### 54:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ أَنذَرَهُم بَطۡشَتَنَا فَتَمَارَوۡاْ بِٱلنُّذُرِ</div>

> And he [Lout (Lot)] indeed had warned them of Our Grasp, but they did doubt the warnings

###### 54:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ رَٰوَدُوهُ عَن ضَيۡفِهِۦ فَطَمَسۡنَآ أَعۡيُنَهُمۡ فَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ</div>

> And they indeed sought to shame his guest (by asking to commit sodomy with them). So We blinded their eyes, "Then taste you My Torment and My Warnings

###### 54:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ صَبَّحَهُم بُكۡرَةً عَذَابࣱ مُّسۡتَقِرࣱّ‏</div>

> And verily, an abiding torment seized them early in the morning

###### 54:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذُوقُواْ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ</div>

> Then taste you My Torment and My Warnings

###### 54:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ يَسَّرۡنَا ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ لِلذِّكۡرِ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed, We have made the Quran easy to understand and remember, then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)

###### 54:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ جَآءَ ءَالَ فِرۡعَوۡنَ ٱلنُّذُرُ</div>

> And indeed, warnings came to the people of Fir'aun (Pharaoh) [through Musa (Moses) and Harun (Aaron)

###### 54:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا كُلِّهَا فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُمۡ أَخۡذَ عَزِيزࣲ مُّقۡتَدِرٍ</div>

> (They) belied all Our Signs, so We seized them with a Seizure of the All-Mighty, All-Capable to carry out what he Will (Omnipotent)

###### 54:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَكُفَّارُكُمۡ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡ أُوْلَٰٓئِكُمۡ أَمۡ لَكُم بَرَآءَةࣱ فِي ٱلزُّبُرِ</div>

> Are your disbelievers (O Quraish!) better than these [nations of Nuh (Noah), Lout (Lot), Salih (Saleh), and the people of Fir'aun (Pharaoh), etc., who were destroyed)? Or have you an immunity (against Our Torment) in the Divine Scriptures

###### 54:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ يَقُولُونَ نَحۡنُ جَمِيعࣱ مُّنتَصِرࣱ‏</div>

> Or do they say: "We are a great multitude, and we shall be victorious

###### 54:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَيُهۡزَمُ ٱلۡجَمۡعُ وَيُوَلُّونَ ٱلدُّبُرَ</div>

> Their multitude will be put to flight, and they will show their backs

###### 54:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلِ ٱلسَّاعَةُ مَوۡعِدُهُمۡ وَٱلسَّاعَةُ أَدۡهَىٰ وَأَمَرُّ</div>

> Nay, but the Hour is their appointed time (for their full recompense), and the Hour will be more grievous and more bitter

###### 54:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ وَسُعُرࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, the Mujrimun (polytheists, disbelievers, sinners, criminals, etc.) are in error (in this world) and will burn (in the Hell-fire in the Hereafter)

###### 54:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يُسۡحَبُونَ فِي ٱلنَّارِ عَلَىٰ وُجُوهِهِمۡ ذُوقُواْ مَسَّ سَقَرَ</div>

> The Day they will be dragged in the Fire on their faces (it will be said to them): "Taste you the touch of Hell

###### 54:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقۡنَٰهُ بِقَدَرࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, We have created all things with Qadar (Divine Preordainments of all things before their creation, as written in the Book of Decrees Al-Lauh Al-Mahfuz)

###### 54:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَمۡرُنَآ إِلَّا وَٰحِدَةࣱ كَلَمۡحِۭ بِٱلۡبَصَرِ</div>

> And Our Commandment is but one, as the twinkling of an eye

###### 54:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَآ أَشۡيَاعَكُمۡ فَهَلۡ مِن مُّدَّكِرࣲ‏</div>

> And indeed, We have destroyed your likes, then is there any that will remember (or receive admonition)

###### 54:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكُلُّ شَيۡءࣲ فَعَلُوهُ فِي ٱلزُّبُرِ</div>

> And each and everything they have done is noted in (their) Records (of deeds)

###### 54:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكُلُّ صَغِيرࣲ وَكَبِيرࣲ مُّسۡتَطَرٌ</div>

> And everything, small and big is written (in Al-Lauh Al-Mahfuz already beforehand i.e. before it befalls, or is done by its doer) (See the Quran V)

###### 54:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ وَنَهَرࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, The Muttaqun (pious), will be in the midst of Gardens and Rivers (Paradise)

###### 54:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي مَقۡعَدِ صِدۡقٍ عِندَ مَلِيكࣲ مُّقۡتَدِرِۭ</div>

> In a seat of truth (i.e. Paradise), near the Omnipotent King (Allah, the All-Blessed, the Most High, the Owner of Majesty and Honour)
