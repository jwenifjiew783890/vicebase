# 56. Al-Waaqia — The Inevitable

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْوَاقِعَةِ</div>

**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 56:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ</div>

> When the Event (i.e. the Day of Resurrection) befalls

###### 56:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَيۡسَ لِوَقۡعَتِهَا كَاذِبَةٌ</div>

> And there can be no denying of its befalling

###### 56:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خَافِضَةࣱ رَّافِعَةٌ</div>

> It will bring low (some); (and others) it will exalt

###### 56:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا رُجَّتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ رَجࣰّ ا</div>

> When the earth will be shaken with a terrible shake

###### 56:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبُسَّتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ بَسࣰّ ا</div>

> And the mountains will be powdered to dust

###### 56:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَانَتۡ هَبَآءࣰ مُّنۢبَثࣰّ ا</div>

> So that they will become floating dust particles

###### 56:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجࣰ ا ثَلَٰثَةࣰ‏</div>

> And you (all) will be in three kinds (i.e. separate groups)

###### 56:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ</div>

> So those on the Right Hand (i.e. those who will be given their Records in their right hands), Who will be those on the Right Hand? (As a respect for them, because they will enter Paradise)

###### 56:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ</div>

> And those on the Left Hand (i.e. those who will be given their Record in their left hands), Who will be those on the Left Hand? (As a disgrace for them, because they will enter Hell)

###### 56:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ</div>

> And those foremost [(in Islamic Faith of Monotheism and in performing righteous deeds) in the life of this world on the very first call for to embrace Islam,] will be foremost (in Paradise)

###### 56:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ</div>

> These will be those nearest to Allah

###### 56:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ</div>

> In the Gardens of delight (Paradise)

###### 56:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُلَّةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> A multitude of those (foremost) will be from the first generations (who embraced Islam)

###### 56:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ</div>

> And a few of those (foremost) will be from the later time (generations)

###### 56:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَىٰ سُرُرࣲ مَّوۡضُونَةࣲ‏</div>

> (They will be) on thrones woven with gold and precious stones

###### 56:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ عَلَيۡهَا مُتَقَٰبِلِينَ</div>

> Reclining thereon, face to face

###### 56:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وِلۡدَٰنࣱ مُّخَلَّدُونَ</div>

> They will be served by immortal boys

###### 56:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بِأَكۡوَابࣲ وَأَبَارِيقَ وَكَأۡسࣲ مِّن مَّعِينࣲ‏</div>

> With cups, and jugs, and a glass from the flowing wine

###### 56:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا يُصَدَّعُونَ عَنۡهَا وَلَا يُنزِفُونَ</div>

> Wherefrom they will get neither any aching of the head, nor any intoxication

###### 56:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَٰكِهَةࣲ مِّمَّا يَتَخَيَّرُونَ</div>

> And fruit; that they may choose

###### 56:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَحۡمِ طَيۡرࣲ مِّمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ</div>

> And the flesh of fowls that they desire

###### 56:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحُورٌ عِينࣱ‏</div>

> And (there will be) Houris (fair females) with wide, lovely eyes (as wives for the pious)

###### 56:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَأَمۡثَٰلِ ٱللُّؤۡلُوِٕ ٱلۡمَكۡنُونِ</div>

> Like unto preserved pearls

###### 56:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">جَزَآءَۢ بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ</div>

> A reward for what they used to do

###### 56:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوࣰ ا وَلَا تَأۡثِيمًا</div>

> No Laghw (dirty, false, evil vain talk) will they hear therein, nor any sinful speech (like backbiting, etc)

###### 56:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا قِيلࣰ ا سَلَٰمࣰ ا سَلَٰمࣰ ا</div>

> But only the saying of: Salam!, Salam! (greetings with peace)

###### 56:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡيَمِينِ</div>

> And those on the Right Hand, Who will be those on the Right Hand

###### 56:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي سِدۡرࣲ مَّخۡضُودࣲ‏</div>

> (They will be) among thornless lote-trees

###### 56:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَطَلۡحࣲ مَّنضُودࣲ‏</div>

> Among Talh (banana-trees) with fruits piled one above another

###### 56:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَظِلࣲّ مَّمۡدُودࣲ‏</div>

> In shade long-extended

###### 56:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآءࣲ مَّسۡكُوبࣲ‏</div>

> By water flowing constantly

###### 56:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَٰكِهَةࣲ كَثِيرَةࣲ‏</div>

> And fruit in plenty

###### 56:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا مَقۡطُوعَةࣲ وَلَا مَمۡنُوعَةࣲ‏</div>

> Whose season is not limited, and their supply will not be cut off

###### 56:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفُرُشࣲ مَّرۡفُوعَةٍ</div>

> And on couches or thrones, raised high

###### 56:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَنشَأۡنَٰهُنَّ إِنشَآءࣰ‏</div>

> Verily, We have created them (maidens) of special creation

###### 56:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُنَّ أَبۡكَارًا</div>

> And made them virgins

###### 56:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عُرُبًا أَتۡرَابࣰ ا</div>

> Loving (their husbands only), equal in age

###### 56:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ</div>

> For those on the Right Hand

###### 56:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُلَّةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> A multitude of those (on the Right Hand) will be from the first generation (who embraced Islam)

###### 56:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَثُلَّةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ</div>

> And a multitude of those (on the Right Hand) will be from the later times (generations)

###### 56:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلشِّمَالِ</div>

> And those on the Left Hand Who will be those on the Left Hand

###### 56:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي سَمُومࣲ وَحَمِيمࣲ‏</div>

> In fierce hot wind and boiling water

###### 56:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَظِلࣲّ مِّن يَحۡمُومࣲ‏</div>

> And shadow of black smoke

###### 56:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا بَارِدࣲ وَلَا كَرِيمٍ</div>

> (That shadow) neither cool, nor (even) good

###### 56:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ قَبۡلَ ذَٰلِكَ مُتۡرَفِينَ</div>

> Verily, before that, they indulged in luxury

###### 56:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَانُواْ يُصِرُّونَ عَلَى ٱلۡحِنثِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ</div>

> And were persisting in great sin (joining partners in worship along with Allah, committing murders and other crimes, etc)

###### 56:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَانُواْ يَقُولُونَ أَئِذَا مِتۡنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابࣰ ا وَعِظَٰمًا أَءِنَّا لَمَبۡعُوثُونَ</div>

> And they used to say: "When we die and become dust and bones, shall we then indeed be resurrected

###### 56:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَءَابَآؤُنَا ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ</div>

> And also our forefathers

###### 56:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ وَٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "(Yes) verily, those of old, and those of later times

###### 56:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَمَجۡمُوعُونَ إِلَىٰ مِيقَٰتِ يَوۡمࣲ مَّعۡلُومࣲ‏</div>

> All will surely be gathered together for appointed Meeting of a known Day

###### 56:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنَّكُمۡ أَيُّهَا ٱلضَّآلُّونَ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> Then moreover, verily, you the erring-ones, the deniers (of Resurrection)

###### 56:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَأٓكِلُونَ مِن شَجَرࣲ مِّن زَقُّومࣲ‏</div>

> You verily will eat of the trees of Zaqqum

###### 56:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَالِـُٔونَ مِنۡهَا ٱلۡبُطُونَ</div>

> Then you will fill your bellies therewith

###### 56:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَشَٰرِبُونَ عَلَيۡهِ مِنَ ٱلۡحَمِيمِ</div>

> And drink boiling water on top of it

###### 56:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَشَٰرِبُونَ شُرۡبَ ٱلۡهِيمِ</div>

> So you will drink (that) like thirsty camels

###### 56:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا نُزُلُهُمۡ يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> That will be their entertainment on the Day of Recompense

###### 56:57
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَحۡنُ خَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ فَلَوۡلَا تُصَدِّقُونَ</div>

> We created you, then why do you believe not

###### 56:58
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تُمۡنُونَ</div>

> Then tell Me (about) the human semen that you emit

###### 56:59
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَنتُمۡ تَخۡلُقُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡخَٰلِقُونَ</div>

> Is it you who create it (i.e. make this semen into a perfect human being), or are We the Creator

###### 56:60
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَحۡنُ قَدَّرۡنَا بَيۡنَكُمُ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَسۡبُوقِينَ</div>

> We have decreed death to you all, and We are not unable

###### 56:61
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ أَمۡثَٰلَكُمۡ وَنُنشِئَكُمۡ فِي مَا لَا تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> To transfigure you and create you in (forms) that you know not

###### 56:62
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ عَلِمۡتُمُ ٱلنَّشۡأَةَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ فَلَوۡلَا تَذَكَّرُونَ</div>

> And indeed, you have already known the first form of creation (i.e. the creation of Adam), why then do you not remember or take heed

###### 56:63
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَرَءَيۡتُم مَّا تَحۡرُثُونَ</div>

> Tell Me! The seed that you sow in the ground

###### 56:64
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَنتُمۡ تَزۡرَعُونَهُۥٓ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلزَّٰرِعُونَ</div>

> Is it you that make it grow, or are We the Grower

###### 56:65
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ حُطَٰمࣰ ا فَظَلۡتُمۡ تَفَكَّهُونَ</div>

> Were it Our Will, We could crumble it to dry pieces, and you would be regretful (or left in wonderment)

###### 56:66
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا لَمُغۡرَمُونَ</div>

> (Saying): "We are indeed Mughramun (i.e. ruined or lost the money without any profit, or punished by the loss of all that we spend for cultivation, etc.)! [See Tafsir Al-Qurtubi, Vol. 17, Page]

###### 56:67
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ نَحۡنُ مَحۡرُومُونَ</div>

> Nay, but we are deprived

###### 56:68
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَآءَ ٱلَّذِي تَشۡرَبُونَ</div>

> Tell Me! The water that you drink

###### 56:69
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنزَلۡتُمُوهُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُزۡنِ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنزِلُونَ</div>

> Is it you who cause it from the rainclouds to come down, or are We the Causer of it to come down

###### 56:70
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَوۡ نَشَآءُ جَعَلۡنَٰهُ أُجَاجࣰ ا فَلَوۡلَا تَشۡكُرُونَ</div>

> If We willed, We verily could make it salt (and undrinkable), why then do you not give thanks (to Allah)

###### 56:71
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَرَءَيۡتُمُ ٱلنَّارَ ٱلَّتِي تُورُونَ</div>

> Tell Me! The fire which you kindle

###### 56:72
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَنتُمۡ أَنشَأۡتُمۡ شَجَرَتَهَآ أَمۡ نَحۡنُ ٱلۡمُنشِـُٔونَ</div>

> Is it you who made the tree thereof to grow, or are We the Grower

###### 56:73
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَحۡنُ جَعَلۡنَٰهَا تَذۡكِرَةࣰ وَمَتَٰعࣰ ا لِّلۡمُقۡوِينَ</div>

> We have made it a Reminder (for the Hell-fire, in the Hereafter); and an article of use for the travellers (and all the others, in this world)

###### 56:74
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ</div>

> Then glorify with praises the Name of your Lord, the Most Great

###### 56:75
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَوَٰقِعِ ٱلنُّجُومِ</div>

> So I swear by Mawaqi (setting or the mansions, etc.) of the stars (they traverse)

###### 56:76
