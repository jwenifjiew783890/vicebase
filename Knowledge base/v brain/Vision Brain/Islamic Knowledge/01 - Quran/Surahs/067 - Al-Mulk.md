# 67. Al-Mulk — The Sovereignty

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْمُلْكِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 30 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 67:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَبَٰرَكَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِ ٱلۡمُلۡكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ قَدِيرٌ</div>

> Blessed is He in Whose Hand is the dominion, and He is Able to do all things

###### 67:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ وَٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ لِيَبۡلُوَكُمۡ أَيُّكُمۡ أَحۡسَنُ عَمَلࣰ اۚ وَهُوَ ٱلۡعَزِيزُ ٱلۡغَفُورُ</div>

> Who has created death and life, that He may test you which of you is best in deed. And He is the All-Mighty, the Oft-Forgiving

###### 67:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتࣲ طِبَاقࣰ اۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِن تَفَٰوُتࣲۖ فَٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ هَلۡ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورࣲ‏</div>

> Who has created the seven heavens one above another, you can see no fault in the creations of the Most Beneficent. Then look again: "Can you see any rifts

###### 67:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ ٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيۡنِ يَنقَلِبۡ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ خَاسِئࣰ ا وَهُوَ حَسِيرࣱ‏</div>

> Then look again and yet again, your sight will return to you in a state of humiliation and worn out

###### 67:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ زَيَّنَّا ٱلسَّمَآءَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا بِمَصَٰبِيحَ وَجَعَلۡنَٰهَا رُجُومࣰ ا لِّلشَّيَٰطِينِۖ وَأَعۡتَدۡنَا لَهُمۡ عَذَابَ ٱلسَّعِيرِ</div>

> And indeed We have adorned the nearest heaven with lamps, and We have made such lamps (as) missiles to drive away the Shayatin (devils), and have prepared for them the torment of the blazing Fire

###### 67:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِرَبِّهِمۡ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَۖ وَبِئۡسَ ٱلۡمَصِيرُ</div>

> And for those who disbelieve in their Lord (Allah) is the torment of Hell, and worst indeed is that destination

###### 67:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَآ أُلۡقُواْ فِيهَا سَمِعُواْ لَهَا شَهِيقࣰ ا وَهِيَ تَفُورُ</div>

> When they are cast therein, they will hear the (terrible) drawing in of its breath as it blazes forth

###### 67:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَكَادُ تَمَيَّزُ مِنَ ٱلۡغَيۡظِۖ كُلَّمَآ أُلۡقِيَ فِيهَا فَوۡجࣱ سَأَلَهُمۡ خَزَنَتُهَآ أَلَمۡ يَأۡتِكُمۡ نَذِيرࣱ‏</div>

> It almost bursts up with fury. Every time a group is cast therein, its keeper will ask: "Did no warner come to you

###### 67:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ قَدۡ جَآءَنَا نَذِيرࣱ فَكَذَّبۡنَا وَقُلۡنَا مَا نَزَّلَ ٱللَّهُ مِن شَيۡءٍ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ كَبِيرࣲ‏</div>

> They will say: "Yes indeed; a warner did come to us, but we belied him and said: 'Allah never sent down anything (of revelation), you are only in great error

###### 67:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ لَوۡ كُنَّا نَسۡمَعُ أَوۡ نَعۡقِلُ مَا كُنَّا فِيٓ أَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ</div>

> And they will say: "Had we but listened or used our intelligence, we would not have been among the dwellers of the blazing Fire

###### 67:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱعۡتَرَفُواْ بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسُحۡقࣰ ا لِّأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلسَّعِيرِ</div>

> Then they will confess their sin. So, away with the dwellers of the blazing Fire

###### 67:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَخۡشَوۡنَ رَبَّهُم بِٱلۡغَيۡبِ لَهُم مَّغۡفِرَةࣱ وَأَجۡرࣱ كَبِيرࣱ‏</div>

> Verily! Those who fear their Lord unseen (i.e. they do not see Him, nor His Punishment in the Hereafter, etc.), theirs will be forgiveness and a great reward (i.e. Paradise)

###### 67:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَسِرُّواْ قَوۡلَكُمۡ أَوِ ٱجۡهَرُواْ بِهِۦٓۖ إِنَّهُۥ عَلِيمُۢ بِذَاتِ ٱلصُّدُورِ</div>

> And whether you keep your talk secret or disclose it, verily, He is the All-Knower of what is in the breasts (of men)

###### 67:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَا يَعۡلَمُ مَنۡ خَلَقَ وَهُوَ ٱللَّطِيفُ ٱلۡخَبِيرُ</div>

> Should not He Who has created know? And He is the Most Kind and Courteous (to His slaves) All-Aware (of everything)

###### 67:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هُوَ ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ ذَلُولࣰ ا فَٱمۡشُواْ فِي مَنَاكِبِهَا وَكُلُواْ مِن رِّزۡقِهِۦۖ وَإِلَيۡهِ ٱلنُّشُورُ</div>

> He it is, Who has made the earth subservient to you (i.e. easy for you to walk, to live and to do agriculture on it, etc.), so walk in the path thereof and eat of His provision, and to Him will be the Resurrection

###### 67:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَمِنتُم مَّن فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يَخۡسِفَ بِكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَمُورُ</div>

> Do you feel secure that He, Who is over the heaven (Allah), will not cause the earth to sink with you, then behold it shakes (as in an earthquake)

###### 67:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ أَمِنتُم مَّن فِي ٱلسَّمَآءِ أَن يُرۡسِلَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ حَاصِبࣰ اۖ فَسَتَعۡلَمُونَ كَيۡفَ نَذِيرِ</div>

> Or do you feel secure that He, Who is over the heaven (Allah), will not send against you a violent whirlwind? Then you shall know how (terrible) has been My Warning

###### 67:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ كَذَّبَ ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِهِمۡ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ نَكِيرِ</div>

> And indeed those before them belied (the Messengers of Allah), then how terrible was My denial (punishment)

###### 67:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ إِلَى ٱلطَّيۡرِ فَوۡقَهُمۡ صَٰٓفَّٰتࣲ وَيَقۡبِضۡنَۚ مَا يُمۡسِكُهُنَّ إِلَّا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُۚ إِنَّهُۥ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءِۭ بَصِيرٌ</div>

> Do they not see the birds above them, spreading out their wings and folding them in? None upholds them except the Most Beneficent (Allah). Verily, He is the All-Seer of everything

###### 67:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمَّنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي هُوَ جُندࣱ لَّكُمۡ يَنصُرُكُم مِّن دُونِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۚ إِنِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ إِلَّا فِي غُرُورٍ</div>

> Who is he besides the Most Beneficent that can be an army to you to help you? The disbelievers are in nothing but delusion

###### 67:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمَّنۡ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي يَرۡزُقُكُمۡ إِنۡ أَمۡسَكَ رِزۡقَهُۥۚ بَل لَّجُّواْ فِي عُتُوࣲّ وَنُفُورٍ</div>

> Who is he that can provide for you if He should withhold His provision? Nay, but they continue to be in pride, and (they) flee (from the truth)

###### 67:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَمَن يَمۡشِي مُكِبًّا عَلَىٰ وَجۡهِهِۦٓ أَهۡدَىٰٓ أَمَّن يَمۡشِي سَوِيًّا عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطࣲ مُّسۡتَقِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Is he who walks without seeing on his face, more rightly guided, or he who (sees and) walks on a Straight Way (i.e. Islamic Monotheism)

###### 67:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَكُمۡ وَجَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلسَّمۡعَ وَٱلۡأَبۡصَٰرَ وَٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةَۚ قَلِيلࣰ ا مَّا تَشۡكُرُونَ</div>

> Say it is He Who has created you, and endowed you with hearing (ears), seeing (eyes), and hearts. Little thanks you give

###### 67:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ</div>

> Say: "It is He Who has created you from the earth, and to Him shall you be gathered (in the Hereafter)

###### 67:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ</div>

> They say: "When will this promise (i.e. the Day of Resurrection) come to pass? if you are telling the truth

###### 67:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنَّمَا ٱلۡعِلۡمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَإِنَّمَآ أَنَا۠ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينࣱ‏</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "The knowledge (of its exact time) is with Allah only, and I am only a plain warner

###### 67:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهُ زُلۡفَةࣰ سِيٓـَٔتۡ وُجُوهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ وَقِيلَ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تَدَّعُونَ</div>

> But when they will see it (the torment on the Day of Resurrection) approaching, the faces of those who disbelieve will be different (black, sad, and in grieve), and it will be said (to them): "This is (the promise) which you were calling for

###### 67:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَهۡلَكَنِيَ ٱللَّهُ وَمَن مَّعِيَ أَوۡ رَحِمَنَا فَمَن يُجِيرُ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ مِنۡ عَذَابٍ أَلِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "Tell me! If Allah destroys me, and those with me, or He bestows His Mercy on us, - who can save the disbelievers from a painful torment

###### 67:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦ وَعَلَيۡهِ تَوَكَّلۡنَاۖ فَسَتَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ هُوَ فِي ضَلَٰلࣲ مُّبِينࣲ‏</div>

> Say: "He is the Most Beneficent (Allah), in Him we believe, and in Him we put our trust. So you will come to know who is it that is in manifest error

###### 67:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ أَرَءَيۡتُمۡ إِنۡ أَصۡبَحَ مَآؤُكُمۡ غَوۡرࣰ ا فَمَن يَأۡتِيكُم بِمَآءࣲ مَّعِينِۭ</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "Tell me! If (all) your water were to be sunk away, who then can supply you with flowing (spring) water
