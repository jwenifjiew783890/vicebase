# 68. Al-Qalam — The Pen

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْقَلَمِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 52 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 68:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نٓۚ وَٱلۡقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسۡطُرُونَ</div>

> Nun. [These letters (Nun, etc.) are one of the miracles of the Quran, and none but Allah (Alone) knows their meanings]. By the pen and what the (angels) write (in the Records of men)

###### 68:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَآ أَنتَ بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ بِمَجۡنُونࣲ‏</div>

> You (O Muhammad SAW) are not, by the Grace of your Lord, a madman

###### 68:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ لَكَ لَأَجۡرًا غَيۡرَ مَمۡنُونࣲ‏</div>

> And verily, for you (O Muhammad SAW) will be an endless reward

###### 68:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمࣲ‏</div>

> And verily, you (O Muhammad SAW) are on an exalted standard of character

###### 68:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَتُبۡصِرُ وَيُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> You will see, and they will see

###### 68:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بِأَييِّكُمُ ٱلۡمَفۡتُونُ</div>

> Which of you is afflicted with madness

###### 68:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ</div>

> Verily, your Lord knows better, who (among men) has gone astray from His Path, and He knows better those who are guided

###### 68:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَا تُطِعِ ٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> So (O Muhammad SAW) obey not the deniers [(of Islamic Monotheism those who belie the Verses of Allah), the Oneness of Allah, and the Messenger of Allah (Muhammad SAW), etc

###### 68:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَدُّواْ لَوۡ تُدۡهِنُ فَيُدۡهِنُونَ</div>

> They wish that you should compromise (in religion out of courtesy) with them, so they (too) would compromise with you

###### 68:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا تُطِعۡ كُلَّ حَلَّافࣲ مَّهِينٍ</div>

> And obey not everyone who swears much, and is considered worthless

###### 68:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَمَّازࣲ مَّشَّآءِۭ بِنَمِيمࣲ‏</div>

> A slanderer, going about with calumnies

###### 68:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّنَّاعࣲ لِّلۡخَيۡرِ مُعۡتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ</div>

> Hinderer of the good, transgressor, sinful

###### 68:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عُتُلِّۭ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ زَنِيمٍ</div>

> Cruel, after all that base-born (of illegitimate birth)

###### 68:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَن كَانَ ذَا مَالࣲ وَبَنِينَ</div>

> (He was so) because he had wealth and children

###### 68:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> When Our Verses (of the Quran) are recited to him, he says: "Tales of the men of old

###### 68:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَنَسِمُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلۡخُرۡطُومِ</div>

> We shall brand him over the nose

###### 68:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا بَلَوۡنَٰهُمۡ كَمَا بَلَوۡنَآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ إِذۡ أَقۡسَمُواْ لَيَصۡرِمُنَّهَا مُصۡبِحِينَ</div>

> Verily, We have tried them as We tried the people of the garden, when they swore to pluck the fruits of the (garden) in the morning

###### 68:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يَسۡتَثۡنُونَ</div>

> Without saying: Insha' Allah (If Allah will)

###### 68:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَطَافَ عَلَيۡهَا طَآئِفࣱ مِّن رَّبِّكَ وَهُمۡ نَآئِمُونَ</div>

> Then there passed by on the (garden) something (fire) from your Lord at night and burnt it while they were asleep

###### 68:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَصۡبَحَتۡ كَٱلصَّرِيمِ</div>

> So the (garden) became black by the morning, like a pitch dark night (in complete ruins)

###### 68:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَتَنَادَوۡاْ مُصۡبِحِينَ</div>

> Then they called out one to another as soon as the morning broke

###### 68:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنِ ٱغۡدُواْ عَلَىٰ حَرۡثِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰرِمِينَ</div>

> Saying: "Go to your tilth in the morning, if you would pluck the fruits

###### 68:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱنطَلَقُواْ وَهُمۡ يَتَخَٰفَتُونَ</div>

> So they departed, conversing in secret low tones (saying)

###### 68:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَن لَّا يَدۡخُلَنَّهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّسۡكِينࣱ‏</div>

> No Miskin (poor man) shall enter upon you into it today

###### 68:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَغَدَوۡاْ عَلَىٰ حَرۡدࣲ قَٰدِرِينَ</div>

> And they went in the morning with strong intention, thinking that they have power (to prevent the poor taking anything of the fruits therefrom)

###### 68:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَمَّا رَأَوۡهَا قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا لَضَآلُّونَ</div>

> But when they saw the (garden), they said: "Verily, we have gone astray

###### 68:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ نَحۡنُ مَحۡرُومُونَ</div>

> (Then they said): "Nay! Indeed we are deprived of (the fruits)

###### 68:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ أَوۡسَطُهُمۡ أَلَمۡ أَقُل لَّكُمۡ لَوۡلَا تُسَبِّحُونَ</div>

> The best among them said: "Did I not tell you: why do you not say: Insha' Allah (If Allah will)

###### 68:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ سُبۡحَٰنَ رَبِّنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا ظَٰلِمِينَ</div>

> They said: "Glory to Our Lord! Verily, we have been Zalimun (wrong-doers, etc)

###### 68:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَقۡبَلَ بَعۡضُهُمۡ عَلَىٰ بَعۡضࣲ يَتَلَٰوَمُونَ</div>

> Then they turned, one against another, in blaming

###### 68:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَآ إِنَّا كُنَّا طَٰغِينَ</div>

> They said: "Woe to us! Verily, we were Taghun (transgressors and disobedient, etc)

###### 68:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَسَىٰ رَبُّنَآ أَن يُبۡدِلَنَا خَيۡرࣰ ا مِّنۡهَآ إِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا رَٰغِبُونَ</div>

> We hope that our Lord will give us in exchange a better (garden) than this. Truly, we turn to our Lord (wishing for good that He may forgive our sins, and reward us in the Hereafter)

###### 68:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡعَذَابُۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ أَكۡبَرُۚ لَوۡ كَانُواْ يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Such is the punishment (in this life), but truly, the punishment of the Hereafter is greater, if they but knew

###### 68:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ</div>

> Verily, for the Muttaqun (pious and righteous persons - see V. 2:2) are Gardens of delight (Paradise) with their Lord

###### 68:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَنَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡمُسۡلِمِينَ كَٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> Shall We then treat the (submitting) Muslims like the Mujrimun (criminals, polytheists and disbelievers, etc)

###### 68:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا لَكُمۡ كَيۡفَ تَحۡكُمُونَ</div>

> What is the matter with you? How judge you

###### 68:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَكُمۡ كِتَٰبࣱ فِيهِ تَدۡرُسُونَ</div>

> Or have you a Book through which you learn

###### 68:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ لَكُمۡ فِيهِ لَمَا تَخَيَّرُونَ</div>

> That you shall have all that you choose

###### 68:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَكُمۡ أَيۡمَٰنٌ عَلَيۡنَا بَٰلِغَةٌ إِلَىٰ يَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ إِنَّ لَكُمۡ لَمَا تَحۡكُمُونَ</div>

> Or you have oaths from Us, reaching to the Day of Resurrection that yours will be what you judge

###### 68:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَلۡهُمۡ أَيُّهُم بِذَٰلِكَ زَعِيمٌ</div>

> Ask them, which of them will stand surety for that

###### 68:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ لَهُمۡ شُرَكَآءُ فَلۡيَأۡتُواْ بِشُرَكَآئِهِمۡ إِن كَانُواْ صَٰدِقِينَ</div>

> Or have they "partners"? Then let them bring their "partners" if they are truthful

###### 68:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يُكۡشَفُ عَن سَاقࣲ وَيُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ</div>

> (Remember) the Day when the Shin shall be laid bare (i.e. the Day of Resurrection) and they shall be called to prostrate (to Allah), but they (hypocrites) shall not be able to do so

###### 68:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خَٰشِعَةً أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةࣱۖ وَقَدۡ كَانُواْ يُدۡعَوۡنَ إِلَى ٱلسُّجُودِ وَهُمۡ سَٰلِمُونَ</div>

> Their eyes will be cast down, ignominy will cover them; they used to be called to prostrate (offer prayers), while they were healthy and good (in the life of the world, but they did not)

###### 68:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَرۡنِي وَمَن يُكَذِّبُ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡحَدِيثِۖ سَنَسۡتَدۡرِجُهُم مِّنۡ حَيۡثُ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Then leave Me Alone with such as belie this Quran. We shall punish them gradually from directions they perceive not

###### 68:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأُمۡلِي لَهُمۡۚ إِنَّ كَيۡدِي مَتِينٌ</div>

> And I will grant them a respite. Verily, My Plan is strong

###### 68:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ تَسۡـَٔلُهُمۡ أَجۡرࣰ ا فَهُم مِّن مَّغۡرَمࣲ مُّثۡقَلُونَ</div>

> Or is it that you (O Muhammad SAW) ask them a wage, so that they are heavily burdened with debt

###### 68:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمۡ عِندَهُمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبُ فَهُمۡ يَكۡتُبُونَ</div>

> Or that the Ghaib (unseen here in this Verse it means Al-Lauh Al-Mahfuz) is in their hands, so that they can write it down

###### 68:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تَكُن كَصَاحِبِ ٱلۡحُوتِ إِذۡ نَادَىٰ وَهُوَ مَكۡظُومࣱ‏</div>

> So wait with patience for the Decision of your Lord, and be not like the Companion of the Fish, when he cried out (to Us) while he was in deep sorrow. (See the Quran, Verse)

###### 68:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّوۡلَآ أَن تَدَٰرَكَهُۥ نِعۡمَةࣱ مِّن رَّبِّهِۦ لَنُبِذَ بِٱلۡعَرَآءِ وَهُوَ مَذۡمُومࣱ‏</div>

> Had not a Grace from his Lord reached him, he would indeed have been (left in the stomach of the fish, but We forgave him), so he was cast off on the naked shore, while he was to be blamed

###### 68:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱجۡتَبَٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَجَعَلَهُۥ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ</div>

> But his Lord chose him and made him of the righteous

###### 68:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِن يَكَادُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لَيُزۡلِقُونَكَ بِأَبۡصَٰرِهِمۡ لَمَّا سَمِعُواْ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَيَقُولُونَ إِنَّهُۥ لَمَجۡنُونࣱ‏</div>

> And verily, those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes through hatredness when they hear the Reminder (the Quran), and they say: "Verily, he (Muhammad SAW) is a madman

###### 68:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> But it is nothing else than a Reminder to all the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)
