# 69. Al-Haaqqa — The Reality

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْحَآقَّةِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 52 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 69:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ</div>

> The Reality (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 69:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ</div>

> What is the Reality

###### 69:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحَآقَّةُ</div>

> And what will make you know what the Reality is

###### 69:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ وَعَادُۢ بِٱلۡقَارِعَةِ</div>

> Thamud and 'Ad people denied the Qari'ah [the striking Hour (of Judgement]

###### 69:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهۡلِكُواْ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ</div>

> As for Thamud, they were destroyed by the awful cry

###### 69:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا عَادࣱ فَأُهۡلِكُواْ بِرِيحࣲ صَرۡصَرٍ عَاتِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> And as for 'Ad, they were destroyed by a furious violent wind

###### 69:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَبۡعَ لَيَالࣲ وَثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومࣰ اۖ فَتَرَى ٱلۡقَوۡمَ فِيهَا صَرۡعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمۡ أَعۡجَازُ نَخۡلٍ خَاوِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> Which Allah imposed on them for seven nights and eight days in succession, so that you could see men lying overthrown (destroyed), as if they were hollow trunks of date-palms

###### 69:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَهَلۡ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> Do you see any remnants of them

###### 69:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَآءَ فِرۡعَوۡنُ وَمَن قَبۡلَهُۥ وَٱلۡمُؤۡتَفِكَٰتُ بِٱلۡخَاطِئَةِ</div>

> And Fir'aun (Pharaoh), and those before him, and the cities overthrown [the towns of the people of [Lout (Lot)] committed sin

###### 69:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَعَصَوۡاْ رَسُولَ رَبِّهِمۡ فَأَخَذَهُمۡ أَخۡذَةࣰ رَّابِيَةً</div>

> And they disobeyed their Lord's Messenger, so He punished them with a strong punishment

###### 69:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا لَمَّا طَغَا ٱلۡمَآءُ حَمَلۡنَٰكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡجَارِيَةِ</div>

> Verily! When the water rose beyond its limits [Nuh's (Noah) Flood], We carried you (mankind) in the floating [ship that was constructed by Nuh (Noah]

###### 69:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِنَجۡعَلَهَا لَكُمۡ تَذۡكِرَةࣰ وَتَعِيَهَآ أُذُنࣱ وَٰعِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> That We might make it a remembrance for you, and the keen ear (person) may (hear and) understand it

###### 69:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ نَفۡخَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ‏</div>

> Then when the Trumpet will be blown with one blowing (the first one)

###### 69:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحُمِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ فَدُكَّتَا دَكَّةࣰ وَٰحِدَةࣰ‏</div>

> And the earth and the mountains shall be removed from their places, and crushed with a single crushing

###### 69:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَيَوۡمَئِذࣲ وَقَعَتِ ٱلۡوَاقِعَةُ</div>

> Then on that Day shall the (Great) Event befall

###### 69:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱنشَقَّتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَهِيَ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ وَاهِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> And the heaven will split asunder, for that Day it (the heaven will be frail (weak), and torn up

###### 69:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡمَلَكُ عَلَىٰٓ أَرۡجَآئِهَاۚ وَيَحۡمِلُ عَرۡشَ رَبِّكَ فَوۡقَهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ ثَمَٰنِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> And the angels will be on its sides, and eight angels will, that Day, bear the Throne of your Lord above them

###### 69:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَئِذࣲ تُعۡرَضُونَ لَا تَخۡفَىٰ مِنكُمۡ خَافِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> That Day shall you be brought to Judgement, not a secret of you will be hidden

###### 69:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ هَآؤُمُ ٱقۡرَءُواْ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ</div>

> Then as for him who will be given his Record in his right hand will say: "Take, read my Record

###### 69:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنِّي ظَنَنتُ أَنِّي مُلَٰقٍ حِسَابِيَهۡ</div>

> Surely, I did believe that I shall meet my Account

###### 69:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةࣲ رَّاضِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> So he shall be in a life, well-pleasing

###### 69:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> In a lofty Paradise

###### 69:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُطُوفُهَا دَانِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> The fruits in bunches whereof will be low and near at hand

###### 69:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَآ أَسۡلَفۡتُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَيَّامِ ٱلۡخَالِيَةِ</div>

> Eat and drink at ease for that which you have sent on before you in days past

###### 69:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِشِمَالِهِۦ فَيَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي لَمۡ أُوتَ كِتَٰبِيَهۡ</div>

> But as for him who will be given his Record in his left hand, will say: "I wish that I had not been given my Record

###### 69:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَمۡ أَدۡرِ مَا حِسَابِيَهۡ</div>

> And that I had never known, how my Account is

###### 69:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰلَيۡتَهَا كَانَتِ ٱلۡقَاضِيَةَ</div>

> I wish, would that it had been my end (death)

###### 69:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنِّي مَالِيَهۡۜ</div>

> My wealth has not availed me

###### 69:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلَكَ عَنِّي سُلۡطَٰنِيَهۡ</div>

> My power and arguments (to defend myself) have gone from me

###### 69:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خُذُوهُ فَغُلُّوهُ</div>

> (It will be said): "Seize him and fetter him

###### 69:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ صَلُّوهُ</div>

> Then throw him in the blazing Fire

###### 69:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ فِي سِلۡسِلَةࣲ ذَرۡعُهَا سَبۡعُونَ ذِرَاعࣰ ا فَٱسۡلُكُوهُ</div>

> Then fasten him with a chain whereof the length is seventy cubits

###### 69:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لَا يُؤۡمِنُ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ</div>

> Verily, He used not to believe in Allah, the Most Great

###### 69:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ</div>

> And urged not on the feeding of Al-Miskin (the poor)

###### 69:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَيۡسَ لَهُ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ هَٰهُنَا حَمِيمࣱ‏</div>

> So no friend has he here this Day

###### 69:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِنۡ غِسۡلِينࣲ‏</div>

> Nor any food except filth from the washing of wounds

###### 69:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا يَأۡكُلُهُۥٓ إِلَّا ٱلۡخَٰطِـُٔونَ</div>

> None will eat except the Khati'un (sinners, disbelievers, polytheists, etc)

###### 69:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِمَا تُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> So I swear by whatsoever you see

###### 69:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا لَا تُبۡصِرُونَ</div>

> And by whatsoever you see not

###### 69:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولࣲ كَرِيمࣲ‏</div>

> That this is verily the word of an honoured Messenger [i.e. Jibrael (Gabriel) or Muhammad SAW which he has brought from Allah

###### 69:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَاعِرࣲۚ قَلِيلࣰ ا مَّا تُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> It is not the word of a poet, little is that you believe

###### 69:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا بِقَوۡلِ كَاهِنࣲۚ قَلِيلࣰ ا مَّا تَذَكَّرُونَ</div>

> Nor is it the word of a soothsayer (or a foreteller), little is that you remember

###### 69:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَنزِيلࣱ مِّن رَّبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> This is the Revelation sent down from the Lord of the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)

###### 69:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡ تَقَوَّلَ عَلَيۡنَا بَعۡضَ ٱلۡأَقَاوِيلِ</div>

> And if he (Muhammad SAW) had forged a false saying concerning Us (Allah)

###### 69:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَأَخَذۡنَا مِنۡهُ بِٱلۡيَمِينِ</div>

> We surely should have seized him by his right hand (or with power and might)

###### 69:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ لَقَطَعۡنَا مِنۡهُ ٱلۡوَتِينَ</div>

> And then certainly should have cut off his life artery (Aorta)

###### 69:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا مِنكُم مِّنۡ أَحَدٍ عَنۡهُ حَٰجِزِينَ</div>

> And none of you could withhold Us from (punishing) him

###### 69:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُۥ لَتَذۡكِرَةࣱ لِّلۡمُتَّقِينَ</div>

> And verily, this Quran is a Reminder for the Muttaqun (pious - see V)

###### 69:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّا لَنَعۡلَمُ أَنَّ مِنكُم مُّكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> And verily, We know that there are some among you that belie (this Quran). [Tafsir At-Tabari, Vol. 29, Page]

###### 69:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَسۡرَةٌ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ</div>

> And indeed it (this Quran) will be an anguish for the disbelievers (on the Day of Resurrection)

###### 69:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُۥ لَحَقُّ ٱلۡيَقِينِ</div>

> And Verily, it (this Quran) is an absolute truth with certainty

###### 69:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَبِّحۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ</div>

> So glorify the Name of your Lord, the Most Great
