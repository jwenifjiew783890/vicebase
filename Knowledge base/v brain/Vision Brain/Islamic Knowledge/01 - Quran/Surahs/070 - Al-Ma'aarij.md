# 70. Al-Ma'aarij — The Ascending Stairways

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْمَعَارِجِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 44 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

---

###### 70:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَأَلَ سَآئِلُۢ بِعَذَابࣲ وَاقِعࣲ‏</div>

> A questioner asked concerning a torment about to befall

###### 70:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّلۡكَٰفِرِينَ لَيۡسَ لَهُۥ دَافِعࣱ‏</div>

> Upon the disbelievers, which none can avert

###### 70:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ ذِي ٱلۡمَعَارِجِ</div>

> From Allah, the Lord of the ways of ascent

###### 70:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَعۡرُجُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ إِلَيۡهِ فِي يَوۡمࣲ كَانَ مِقۡدَارُهُۥ خَمۡسِينَ أَلۡفَ سَنَةࣲ‏</div>

> The angels and the Ruh [Jibrael (Gabriel)] ascend to Him in a Day the measure whereof is fifty thousand years

###### 70:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱصۡبِرۡ صَبۡرࣰ ا جَمِيلًا</div>

> So be patient (O Muhammad SAW), with a good patience

###### 70:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُمۡ يَرَوۡنَهُۥ بَعِيدࣰ ا</div>

> Verily! They see it (the torment) afar off

###### 70:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَرَىٰهُ قَرِيبࣰ ا</div>

> But We see it (quite) near

###### 70:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ تَكُونُ ٱلسَّمَآءُ كَٱلۡمُهۡلِ</div>

> The Day that the sky will be like the boiling filth of oil, (or molten copper or silver or lead, etc)

###### 70:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ</div>

> And the mountains will be like flakes of wool

###### 70:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يَسۡـَٔلُ حَمِيمٌ حَمِيمࣰ ا</div>

> And no friend will ask of a friend

###### 70:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُبَصَّرُونَهُمۡۚ يَوَدُّ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُ لَوۡ يَفۡتَدِي مِنۡ عَذَابِ يَوۡمِئِذِۭ بِبَنِيهِ</div>

> Though they shall be made to see one another [(i.e. on the Day of Resurrection), there will be none but see his father, children and relatives, but he will neither speak to them nor will ask them for any help)], - the Mujrim, (criminal, sinner, disbeliever, etc.) would desire to ransom himself from the punishment of that Day by his children

###### 70:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَأَخِيهِ</div>

> And his wife and his brother

###### 70:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَصِيلَتِهِ ٱلَّتِي تُـٔۡوِيهِ</div>

> And his kindred who sheltered him

###### 70:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ جَمِيعࣰ ا ثُمَّ يُنجِيهِ</div>

> And all that are in the earth, so that it might save him

###### 70:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآۖ إِنَّهَا لَظَىٰ</div>

> By no means! Verily, it will be the Fire of Hell

###### 70:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَزَّاعَةࣰ لِّلشَّوَىٰ</div>

> Taking away (burning completely) the head skin

###### 70:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَدۡعُواْ مَنۡ أَدۡبَرَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ</div>

> Calling: "[O Kafir (O disbeliever in Allah, His angels, His Book, His Messengers, Day of Resurrection and in Al-Qadar (Divine Preordainments), O Mushrik (O polytheist, disbeliever in the Oneness of Allah)] (all) such as turn their backs and turn away their faces (from Faith) [picking and swallowing them up from that great gathering of mankind (on the Day of Resurrection) just as a bird picks up a food-grain from the earth with its beak and swallows it up] [Tafsir Al-Qurtubi, Vol. 18, Page]

###### 70:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَمَعَ فَأَوۡعَىٰٓ</div>

> And collect (wealth) and hide it (from spending it in the Cause of Allah)

###### 70:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ خُلِقَ هَلُوعًا</div>

> Verily, man (disbeliever) was created very impatient

###### 70:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلشَّرُّ جَزُوعࣰ ا</div>

> Irritable (discontented) when evil touches him

###### 70:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا مَسَّهُ ٱلۡخَيۡرُ مَنُوعًا</div>

> And niggardly when good touches him

###### 70:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ</div>

> Except those devoted to Salat (prayers)

###### 70:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ دَآئِمُونَ</div>

> Those who remain constant in their Salat (prayers)

###### 70:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ فِيٓ أَمۡوَٰلِهِمۡ حَقࣱّ مَّعۡلُومࣱ‏</div>

> And those in whose wealth there is a known right

###### 70:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَٱلۡمَحۡرُومِ</div>

> For the beggar who asks, and for the unlucky who has lost his property and wealth, (and his means of living has been straitened)

###### 70:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ يُصَدِّقُونَ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> And those who believe in the Day of Recompense

###### 70:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنۡ عَذَابِ رَبِّهِم مُّشۡفِقُونَ</div>

> And those who fear the torment of their Lord

###### 70:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ عَذَابَ رَبِّهِمۡ غَيۡرُ مَأۡمُونࣲ‏</div>

> Verily! The torment of their Lord is that before which none can feel secure

###### 70:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِفُرُوجِهِمۡ حَٰفِظُونَ</div>

> And those who guard their chastity (i.e. private parts from illegal sexual acts)

###### 70:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا عَلَىٰٓ أَزۡوَٰجِهِمۡ أَوۡ مَا مَلَكَتۡ أَيۡمَٰنُهُمۡ فَإِنَّهُمۡ غَيۡرُ مَلُومِينَ</div>

> Except with their wives and the (women slaves and captives) whom their right hands possess, for (then) they are not to be blamed

###### 70:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَنِ ٱبۡتَغَىٰ وَرَآءَ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡعَادُونَ</div>

> But whosoever seeks beyond that, then it is those who are trespassers

###### 70:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ لِأَمَٰنَٰتِهِمۡ وَعَهۡدِهِمۡ رَٰعُونَ</div>

> And those who keep their trusts and covenants

###### 70:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِشَهَٰدَٰتِهِمۡ قَآئِمُونَ</div>

> And those who stand firm in their testimonies

###### 70:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَلَىٰ صَلَاتِهِمۡ يُحَافِظُونَ</div>

> And those who guard their Salat (prayers) well

###### 70:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ مُّكۡرَمُونَ</div>

> Such shall dwell in the Gardens (i.e. Paradise) honoured

###### 70:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَالِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ قِبَلَكَ مُهۡطِعِينَ</div>

> So what is the matter with those who disbelieve that they hasten to listen from you (O Muhammad SAW), in order to belie you and to mock at you, and at Allah's Book (this Quran)

###### 70:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَنِ ٱلۡيَمِينِ وَعَنِ ٱلشِّمَالِ عِزِينَ</div>

> (Sitting) in groups on the right and on the left (of you, O Muhammad SAW)

###### 70:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَطۡمَعُ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيࣲٕ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَن يُدۡخَلَ جَنَّةَ نَعِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Does every man of them hope to enter the Paradise of delight

###### 70:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآۖ إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُم مِّمَّا يَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> No, that is not like that! Verily, We have created them out of that which they know

###### 70:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡمَشَٰرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغَٰرِبِ إِنَّا لَقَٰدِرُونَ</div>

> So I swear by the Lord of all [the three hundred and sixty (360)] points of sunrise and sunset in the east and the west that surely We are Able

###### 70:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّبَدِّلَ خَيۡرࣰ ا مِّنۡهُمۡ وَمَا نَحۡنُ بِمَسۡبُوقِينَ</div>

> To replace them by (others) better than them; and We are not to be outrun

###### 70:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَرۡهُمۡ يَخُوضُواْ وَيَلۡعَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ</div>

> So leave them to plunge in vain talk and play about, until they meet their Day which they are promised

###### 70:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَخۡرُجُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ سِرَاعࣰ ا كَأَنَّهُمۡ إِلَىٰ نُصُبࣲ يُوفِضُونَ</div>

> The Day when they will come out of the graves quickly as racing to a goal

###### 70:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خَٰشِعَةً أَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ تَرۡهَقُهُمۡ ذِلَّةࣱۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلَّذِي كَانُواْ يُوعَدُونَ</div>

> With their eyes lowered in fear and humility, ignominy covering them (all over)! That is the Day which they were promised
