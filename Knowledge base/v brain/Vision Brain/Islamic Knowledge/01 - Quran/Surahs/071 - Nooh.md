# 71. Nooh — Noah

###### 71:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَا نُوحًا إِلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦٓ أَنۡ أَنذِرۡ قَوۡمَكَ مِن قَبۡلِ أَن يَأۡتِيَهُمۡ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمࣱ‏</div>

> Verily, We sent Nuh (Noah) to his people (Saying): "Warn your people before there comes to them a painful torment

###### 71:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ إِنِّي لَكُمۡ نَذِيرࣱ مُّبِينٌ</div>

> He said: "O my people! Verily, I am a plain warner to you

###### 71:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنِ ٱعۡبُدُواْ ٱللَّهَ وَٱتَّقُوهُ وَأَطِيعُونِ</div>

> That you should worship Allah (Alone), be dutiful to Him, and obey me

###### 71:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَغۡفِرۡ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمۡ وَيُؤَخِّرۡكُمۡ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلࣲ مُّسَمًّىۚ إِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا جَآءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُۚ لَوۡ كُنتُمۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> He (Allah) will forgive you of your sins and respite you to an appointed term. Verily, the term of Allah when it comes, cannot be delayed, if you but knew

###### 71:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي دَعَوۡتُ قَوۡمِي لَيۡلࣰ ا وَنَهَارࣰ ا</div>

> He said: "O my Lord! Verily, I have called my people night and day (i.e. secretly and openly to accept the doctrine of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 71:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَمۡ يَزِدۡهُمۡ دُعَآءِيٓ إِلَّا فِرَارࣰ ا</div>

> But all my calling added nothing but to (their) flight (from the truth)

###### 71:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنِّي كُلَّمَا دَعَوۡتُهُمۡ لِتَغۡفِرَ لَهُمۡ جَعَلُوٓاْ أَصَٰبِعَهُمۡ فِيٓ ءَاذَانِهِمۡ وَٱسۡتَغۡشَوۡاْ ثِيَابَهُمۡ وَأَصَرُّواْ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرُواْ ٱسۡتِكۡبَارࣰ ا</div>

> And verily! Every time I called unto them that You might forgive them, they thrust their fingers into their ears, covered themselves up with their garments, and persisted (in their refusal), and magnified themselves in pride

###### 71:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنِّي دَعَوۡتُهُمۡ جِهَارࣰ ا</div>

> Then verily, I called to them openly (aloud)

###### 71:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنِّيٓ أَعۡلَنتُ لَهُمۡ وَأَسۡرَرۡتُ لَهُمۡ إِسۡرَارࣰ ا</div>

> Then verily, I proclaimed to them in public, and I have appealed to them in private

###### 71:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقُلۡتُ ٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ رَبَّكُمۡ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ غَفَّارࣰ ا</div>

> I said (to them): 'Ask forgiveness from your Lord; Verily, He is Oft-Forgiving

###### 71:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُرۡسِلِ ٱلسَّمَآءَ عَلَيۡكُم مِّدۡرَارࣰ ا</div>

> He will send rain to you in abundance

###### 71:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيُمۡدِدۡكُم بِأَمۡوَٰلࣲ وَبَنِينَ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ جَنَّٰتࣲ وَيَجۡعَل لَّكُمۡ أَنۡهَٰرࣰ ا</div>

> And give you increase in wealth and children, and bestow on you gardens and bestow on you rivers

###### 71:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّا لَكُمۡ لَا تَرۡجُونَ لِلَّهِ وَقَارࣰ ا</div>

> What is the matter with you, [that you fear not Allah (His punishment), and] you hope not for reward (from Allah or you believe not in His Oneness)

###### 71:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَدۡ خَلَقَكُمۡ أَطۡوَارًا</div>

> While He has created you in (different) stages [i.e. first Nutfah, then 'Alaqah and then Mudghah, see (VV. 23:13, 14) the Quran

###### 71:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ تَرَوۡاْ كَيۡفَ خَلَقَ ٱللَّهُ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتࣲ طِبَاقࣰ ا</div>

> See you not how Allah has created the seven heavens one above another

###### 71:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ فِيهِنَّ نُورࣰ ا وَجَعَلَ ٱلشَّمۡسَ سِرَاجࣰ ا</div>

> And has made the moon a light therein, and made the sun a lamp

###### 71:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱللَّهُ أَنۢبَتَكُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَرۡضِ نَبَاتࣰ ا</div>

> And Allah has brought you forth from the (dust of) earth. [Tafsir At-Tabari, Vol. 29, Page]

###### 71:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ يُعِيدُكُمۡ فِيهَا وَيُخۡرِجُكُمۡ إِخۡرَاجࣰ ا</div>

> Afterwards He will return you into it (the earth), and bring you forth (again on the Day of Resurrection)

###### 71:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱللَّهُ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِسَاطࣰ ا</div>

> And Allah has made for you the earth wide spread (an expanse)

###### 71:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّتَسۡلُكُواْ مِنۡهَا سُبُلࣰ ا فِجَاجࣰ ا</div>

> That you may go about therein in broad roads

###### 71:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالَ نُوحࣱ رَّبِّ إِنَّهُمۡ عَصَوۡنِي وَٱتَّبَعُواْ مَن لَّمۡ يَزِدۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَوَلَدُهُۥٓ إِلَّا خَسَارࣰ ا</div>

> Nuh (Noah) said: "My Lord! They have disobeyed me, and followed one whose wealth and children give him no increase but only loss

###### 71:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَكَرُواْ مَكۡرࣰ ا كُبَّارࣰ ا</div>

> And they have plotted a mighty plot

###### 71:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالُواْ لَا تَذَرُنَّ ءَالِهَتَكُمۡ وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدࣰّ ا وَلَا سُوَاعࣰ ا وَلَا يَغُوثَ وَيَعُوقَ وَنَسۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And they have said: 'You shall not leave your gods, nor shall you leave Wadd, nor Suwa', nor Yaghuth, nor Ya'uq, nor Nasr (names of the idols)

###### 71:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَدۡ أَضَلُّواْ كَثِيرࣰ اۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا ضَلَٰلࣰ ا</div>

> And indeed they have led many astray. And (O Allah): 'Grant no increase to the Zalimun (polytheists, wrong-doers, and disbelievers, etc.) save error

###### 71:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِّمَّا خَطِيٓـَٰٔتِهِمۡ أُغۡرِقُواْ فَأُدۡخِلُواْ نَارࣰ ا فَلَمۡ يَجِدُواْ لَهُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَنصَارࣰ ا</div>

> Because of their sins they were drowned, then were made to enter the Fire, and they found none to help them instead of Allah

###### 71:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالَ نُوحࣱ رَّبِّ لَا تَذَرۡ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ مِنَ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ دَيَّارًا</div>

> And Nuh (Noah) said: "My Lord! Leave not one of the disbelievers on the earth

###### 71:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّكَ إِن تَذَرۡهُمۡ يُضِلُّواْ عِبَادَكَ وَلَا يَلِدُوٓاْ إِلَّا فَاجِرࣰ ا كَفَّارࣰ ا</div>

> If You leave them, they will mislead Your slaves, and they will beget none but wicked disbelievers

###### 71:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَّبِّ ٱغۡفِرۡ لِي وَلِوَٰلِدَيَّ وَلِمَن دَخَلَ بَيۡتِيَ مُؤۡمِنࣰ ا وَلِلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِۖ وَلَا تَزِدِ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ إِلَّا تَبَارَۢا</div>

> My Lord! Forgive me, and my parents, and him who enters my home as a believer, and all the believing men and women. And to the Zalimun (polytheists, wrong-doers, and disbelievers, etc.) grant You no increase but destruction
