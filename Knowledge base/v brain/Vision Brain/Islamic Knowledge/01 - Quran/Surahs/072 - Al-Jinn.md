# 72. Al-Jinn — The Jinn

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْجِنِّ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 28 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 72:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ أُوحِيَ إِلَيَّ أَنَّهُ ٱسۡتَمَعَ نَفَرࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا سَمِعۡنَا قُرۡءَانًا عَجَبࣰ ا</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "It has been revealed to me that a group (from three to ten in number) of jinns listened (to this Quran). They said: 'Verily! We have heard a wonderful Recital (this Quran)

###### 72:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَهۡدِيٓ إِلَى ٱلرُّشۡدِ فَـَٔامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ وَلَن نُّشۡرِكَ بِرَبِّنَآ أَحَدࣰ ا</div>

> It guides to the Right Path, and we have believed therein, and we shall never join (in worship) anything with our Lord (Allah)

###### 72:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ تَعَٰلَىٰ جَدُّ رَبِّنَا مَا ٱتَّخَذَ صَٰحِبَةࣰ وَلَا وَلَدࣰ ا</div>

> And exalted be the Majesty of our Lord, He has taken neither a wife, nor a son (or offspring or children)

###### 72:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ يَقُولُ سَفِيهُنَا عَلَى ٱللَّهِ شَطَطࣰ ا</div>

> And that the foolish among us [i.e. Iblis (Satan) or the polytheists amongst the jinns] used to utter against Allah that which was wrong and not right

###### 72:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن تَقُولَ ٱلۡإِنسُ وَٱلۡجِنُّ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ كَذِبࣰ ا</div>

> And verily, we thought that men and jinns would not utter a lie against Allah

###### 72:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ كَانَ رِجَالࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡإِنسِ يَعُوذُونَ بِرِجَالࣲ مِّنَ ٱلۡجِنِّ فَزَادُوهُمۡ رَهَقࣰ ا</div>

> And verily, there were men among mankind who took shelter with the masculine among the jinns, but they (jinns) increased them (mankind) in sin and disbelief

###### 72:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُمۡ ظَنُّواْ كَمَا ظَنَنتُمۡ أَن لَّن يَبۡعَثَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدࣰ ا</div>

> And they thought as you thought, that Allah will not send any Messenger (to mankind or jinns)

###### 72:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا لَمَسۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ فَوَجَدۡنَٰهَا مُلِئَتۡ حَرَسࣰ ا شَدِيدࣰ ا وَشُهُبࣰ ا</div>

> And we have sought to reach the heaven; but found it filled with stern guards and flaming fires

###### 72:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا كُنَّا نَقۡعُدُ مِنۡهَا مَقَٰعِدَ لِلسَّمۡعِۖ فَمَن يَسۡتَمِعِ ٱلۡأٓنَ يَجِدۡ لَهُۥ شِهَابࣰ ا رَّصَدࣰ ا</div>

> And verily, we used to sit there in stations, to (steal) a hearing, but any who listens now will find a flaming fire watching him in ambush

###### 72:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا لَا نَدۡرِيٓ أَشَرٌّ أُرِيدَ بِمَن فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ أَمۡ أَرَادَ بِهِمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ رَشَدࣰ ا</div>

> And we know not whether evil is intended for those on earth, or whether their Lord intends for them a Right Path

###### 72:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلصَّٰلِحُونَ وَمِنَّا دُونَ ذَٰلِكَۖ كُنَّا طَرَآئِقَ قِدَدࣰ ا</div>

> There are among us some that are righteous, and some the contrary; we are groups each having a different way (religious sect, etc)

###### 72:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّآ أَن لَّن نُّعۡجِزَ ٱللَّهَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَلَن نُّعۡجِزَهُۥ هَرَبࣰ ا</div>

> And we think that we cannot escape (from the punishment of) Allah in the earth, nor can we escape (from the punishment) by flight

###### 72:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا لَمَّا سَمِعۡنَا ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ ءَامَنَّا بِهِۦۖ فَمَن يُؤۡمِنۢ بِرَبِّهِۦ فَلَا يَخَافُ بَخۡسࣰ ا وَلَا رَهَقࣰ ا</div>

> And indeed when we heard the Guidance (this Quran), we believed therein (Islamic Monotheism), and whosoever believes in his Lord shall have no fear, either of a decrease in the reward of his good deeds or an increase in punishment for his sins

###### 72:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّا مِنَّا ٱلۡمُسۡلِمُونَ وَمِنَّا ٱلۡقَٰسِطُونَۖ فَمَنۡ أَسۡلَمَ فَأُوْلَٰٓئِكَ تَحَرَّوۡاْ رَشَدࣰ ا</div>

> And of us some are Muslims (who have submitted to Allah, after listening to this Quran), and of us some are Al-Qasitun (disbelievers those who have deviated from the Right Path)'. And whosoever has embraced Islam (i.e. has become a Muslim by submitting to Allah), then such have sought the Right Path

###### 72:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا ٱلۡقَٰسِطُونَ فَكَانُواْ لِجَهَنَّمَ حَطَبࣰ ا</div>

> And as for the Qasitun (disbelievers who deviated from the Right Path), they shall be firewood for Hell

###### 72:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَلَّوِ ٱسۡتَقَٰمُواْ عَلَى ٱلطَّرِيقَةِ لَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰهُم مَّآءً غَدَقࣰ ا</div>

> If they (non-Muslims) had believed in Allah, and went on the Right Way (i.e. Islam) We should surely have bestowed on them water (rain) in abundance

###### 72:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّنَفۡتِنَهُمۡ فِيهِۚ وَمَن يُعۡرِضۡ عَن ذِكۡرِ رَبِّهِۦ يَسۡلُكۡهُ عَذَابࣰ ا صَعَدࣰ ا</div>

> That We might try them thereby. And whosoever turns away from the Reminder of his Lord (i.e. this Quran, and practice not its laws and orders), He will cause him to enter in a severe torment (i.e. Hell)

###### 72:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّ ٱلۡمَسَٰجِدَ لِلَّهِ فَلَا تَدۡعُواْ مَعَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدࣰ ا</div>

> And the mosques are for Allah (Alone), so invoke not anyone along with Allah

###### 72:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنَّهُۥ لَمَّا قَامَ عَبۡدُ ٱللَّهِ يَدۡعُوهُ كَادُواْ يَكُونُونَ عَلَيۡهِ لِبَدࣰ ا</div>

> (It has been revealed to me that) When the slave of Allah (Muhammad SAW) stood up invoking (his Lord Allah) in prayer to Him they (the jinns) just made round him a dense crowd as if sticking one over the other (in order to listen to the Prophet's recitation)

###### 72:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنَّمَآ أَدۡعُواْ رَبِّي وَلَآ أُشۡرِكُ بِهِۦٓ أَحَدࣰ ا</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "I invoke only my Lord (Allah Alone), and I associate none as partners along with Him

###### 72:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنِّي لَآ أَمۡلِكُ لَكُمۡ ضَرࣰّ ا وَلَا رَشَدࣰ ا</div>

> Say: "It is not in my power to cause you harm, or to bring you to the Right Path

###### 72:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنِّي لَن يُجِيرَنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهِ أَحَدࣱ وَلَنۡ أَجِدَ مِن دُونِهِۦ مُلۡتَحَدًا</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "None can protect me from Allah's punishment (if I were to disobey Him), nor should I find refuge except in Him

###### 72:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا بَلَٰغࣰ ا مِّنَ ٱللَّهِ وَرِسَٰلَٰتِهِۦۚ وَمَن يَعۡصِ ٱللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُۥ فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ نَارَ جَهَنَّمَ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدًا</div>

> (Mine is) but conveyance (of the truth) from Allah and His Messages (of Islamic Monotheism), and whosoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger, then verily, for him is the Fire of Hell, he shall dwell therein forever

###### 72:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا رَأَوۡاْ مَا يُوعَدُونَ فَسَيَعۡلَمُونَ مَنۡ أَضۡعَفُ نَاصِرࣰ ا وَأَقَلُّ عَدَدࣰ ا</div>

> Till, when they see that which they are promised, then they will know who it is that is weaker concerning helpers and less important concerning numbers

###### 72:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ إِنۡ أَدۡرِيٓ أَقَرِيبࣱ مَّا تُوعَدُونَ أَمۡ يَجۡعَلُ لَهُۥ رَبِّيٓ أَمَدًا</div>

> Say (O Muhammad SAW): "I know not whether (the punishment) which you are promised is near or whether my Lord will appoint for it a distant term

###### 72:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَٰلِمُ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ فَلَا يُظۡهِرُ عَلَىٰ غَيۡبِهِۦٓ أَحَدًا</div>

> (He Alone) the All-Knower of the Gha'ib (unseen), and He reveals to none His Gha'ib (unseen)

###### 72:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَنِ ٱرۡتَضَىٰ مِن رَّسُولࣲ فَإِنَّهُۥ يَسۡلُكُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ يَدَيۡهِ وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِۦ رَصَدࣰ ا</div>

> Except to a Messenger (from mankind) whom He has chosen (He informs him of unseen as much as He likes), and then He makes a band of watching guards (angels) to march before him and behind him

###### 72:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّيَعۡلَمَ أَن قَدۡ أَبۡلَغُواْ رِسَٰلَٰتِ رَبِّهِمۡ وَأَحَاطَ بِمَا لَدَيۡهِمۡ وَأَحۡصَىٰ كُلَّ شَيۡءٍ عَدَدَۢا</div>

> He (Allah) protects them (the Messengers)], till He sees that they (the Messengers) have conveyed the Messages of their Lord (Allah). And He (Allah) surrounds all that which is with them, and He (Allah) keeps count of all things (i.e. He knows the exact number of everything)
