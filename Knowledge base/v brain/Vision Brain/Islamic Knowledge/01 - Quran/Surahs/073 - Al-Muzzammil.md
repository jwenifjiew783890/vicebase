# 73. Al-Muzzammil — The Enshrouded One

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 20 · **Juz':** 29

**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 73:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُزَّمِّلُ</div>

> O you wrapped in garments (i.e. Prophet Muhammad SAW)

###### 73:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُمِ ٱلَّيۡلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلࣰ ا</div>

> Stand (to pray) all night, except a little

###### 73:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نِّصۡفَهُۥٓ أَوِ ٱنقُصۡ مِنۡهُ قَلِيلًا</div>

> Half of it, or a little less than that

###### 73:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوۡ زِدۡ عَلَيۡهِ وَرَتِّلِ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَرۡتِيلًا</div>

> Or a little more; and recite the Quran (aloud) in a slow, (pleasant tone and) style

###### 73:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا سَنُلۡقِي عَلَيۡكَ قَوۡلࣰ ا ثَقِيلًا</div>

> Verily, We shall send down to you a weighty Word (i.e. obligations, legal laws, etc)

###### 73:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ ٱلَّيۡلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطۡـࣰٔ ا وَأَقۡوَمُ قِيلًا</div>

> Verily, the rising by night (for Tahajjud prayer) is very hard and most potent and good for governing (the soul), and most suitable for (understanding) the Word (of Allah)

###### 73:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ لَكَ فِي ٱلنَّهَارِ سَبۡحࣰ ا طَوِيلࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, there is for you by day prolonged occupation with ordinary duties

###### 73:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ وَتَبَتَّلۡ إِلَيۡهِ تَبۡتِيلࣰ ا</div>

> And remember the Name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with a complete devotion

###### 73:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَّبُّ ٱلۡمَشۡرِقِ وَٱلۡمَغۡرِبِ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱتَّخِذۡهُ وَكِيلࣰ ا</div>

> (He Alone is) the Lord of the east and the west, La ilaha illa Huwa (none has the right to be worshipped but He). So take Him Alone as Wakil (Disposer of your affairs)

###### 73:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱصۡبِرۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَٱهۡجُرۡهُمۡ هَجۡرࣰ ا جَمِيلࣰ ا</div>

> And be patient (O Muhammad SAW) with what they say, and keep away from them in a good way

###### 73:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَذَرۡنِي وَٱلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ أُوْلِي ٱلنَّعۡمَةِ وَمَهِّلۡهُمۡ قَلِيلًا</div>

> And leave Me Alone to deal with the beliers (those who deny My Verses, etc.), and those who are in possession of good things of life. And give them respite for a little while

###### 73:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ لَدَيۡنَآ أَنكَالࣰ ا وَجَحِيمࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, with Us are fetters (to bind them), and a raging Fire

###### 73:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَطَعَامࣰ ا ذَا غُصَّةࣲ وَعَذَابًا أَلِيمࣰ ا</div>

> And a food that chokes, and a painful torment

###### 73:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ تَرۡجُفُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَٱلۡجِبَالُ وَكَانَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَثِيبࣰ ا مَّهِيلًا</div>

> On the Day when the earth and the mountains will be in violent shake, and the mountains will be a heap of sand poured out and flowing down

###### 73:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ رَسُولࣰ ا شَٰهِدًا عَلَيۡكُمۡ كَمَآ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ رَسُولࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, We have sent to you (O men) a Messenger (Muhammad SAW) to be a witness over you, as We did send a Messenger [Musa (Moses)] to Fir'aun (Pharaoh)

###### 73:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَعَصَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنُ ٱلرَّسُولَ فَأَخَذۡنَٰهُ أَخۡذࣰ ا وَبِيلࣰ ا</div>

> But Fir'aun (Pharaoh) disobeyed the Messenger [Musa (Moses)], so We seized him with a severe punishment

###### 73:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَيۡفَ تَتَّقُونَ إِن كَفَرۡتُمۡ يَوۡمࣰ ا يَجۡعَلُ ٱلۡوِلۡدَٰنَ شِيبًا</div>

> Then how can you avoid the punishment, if you disbelieve, on a Day that will make the children grey-headed (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 73:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلسَّمَآءُ مُنفَطِرُۢ بِهِۦۚ كَانَ وَعۡدُهُۥ مَفۡعُولًا</div>

> Whereon the heaven will be cleft asunder? His Promise is certainly to be accomplished

###### 73:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةࣱۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا</div>

> Verily, this is an admonition, therefore whosoever will, let him take a Path to His Lord

###### 73:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞إِنَّ رَبَّكَ يَعۡلَمُ أَنَّكَ تَقُومُ أَدۡنَىٰ مِن ثُلُثَيِ ٱلَّيۡلِ وَنِصۡفَهُۥ وَثُلُثَهُۥ وَطَآئِفَةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلَّذِينَ مَعَكَۚ وَٱللَّهُ يُقَدِّرُ ٱلَّيۡلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَۚ عَلِمَ أَن لَّن تُحۡصُوهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيۡكُمۡۖ فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانِۚ عَلِمَ أَن سَيَكُونُ مِنكُم مَّرۡضَىٰ وَءَاخَرُونَ يَضۡرِبُونَ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ يَبۡتَغُونَ مِن فَضۡلِ ٱللَّهِ وَءَاخَرُونَ يُقَٰتِلُونَ فِي سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِۖ فَٱقۡرَءُواْ مَا تَيَسَّرَ مِنۡهُۚ وَأَقِيمُواْ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ وَءَاتُواْ ٱلزَّكَوٰةَ وَأَقۡرِضُواْ ٱللَّهَ قَرۡضًا حَسَنࣰ اۚ وَمَا تُقَدِّمُواْ لِأَنفُسِكُم مِّنۡ خَيۡرࣲ تَجِدُوهُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ هُوَ خَيۡرࣰ ا وَأَعۡظَمَ أَجۡرࣰ اۚ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَفُورࣱ رَّحِيمُۢ</div>

> Verily, your Lord knows that you do stand (to pray at night) a little less than two-thirds of the night, or half the night, or a third of the night, and so do a party of those with you, And Allah measures the night and the day. He knows that you are unable to pray the whole night, so He has turned to you (in mercy). So, recite you of the Quran as much as may be easy for you. He knows that there will be some among you sick, others travelling through the land, seeking of Allah's Bounty; yet others fighting in Allah's Cause. So recite as much of the Quran as may be easy (for you), and perform As-Salat (Iqamat-as-Salat) and give Zakat, and lend to Allah a goodly loan, and whatever good you send before you for yourselves, (i.e. Nawafil non-obligatory acts of worship: prayers, charity, fasting, Hajj and 'Umrah, etc.), you will certainly find it with Allah, better and greater in reward. And seek Forgiveness of Allah. Verily, Allah is Oft-Forgiving, Most-Merciful
