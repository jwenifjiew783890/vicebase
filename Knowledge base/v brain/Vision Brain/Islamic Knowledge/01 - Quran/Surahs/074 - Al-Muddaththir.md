# 74. Al-Muddaththir — The Cloaked One

**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 74:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُدَّثِّرُ</div>

> O you (Muhammad SAW) enveloped (in garments)

###### 74:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُمۡ فَأَنذِرۡ</div>

> Arise and warn

###### 74:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَرَبَّكَ فَكَبِّرۡ</div>

> And your Lord (Allah) magnify

###### 74:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَثِيَابَكَ فَطَهِّرۡ</div>

> And your garments purify

###### 74:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلرُّجۡزَ فَٱهۡجُرۡ</div>

> And keep away from Ar-Rujz (the idols)

###### 74:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا تَمۡنُن تَسۡتَكۡثِرُ</div>

> And give not a thing in order to have more (or consider not your deeds of Allah's obedience as a favour to Allah)

###### 74:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلِرَبِّكَ فَٱصۡبِرۡ</div>

> And be patient for the sake of your Lord (i.e. perform your duty to Allah)

###### 74:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا نُقِرَ فِي ٱلنَّاقُورِ</div>

> Then, when the Trumpet is sounded (i.e. its second blowing)

###### 74:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَٰلِكَ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ يَوۡمٌ عَسِيرٌ</div>

> Truly, that Day will be a Hard Day

###### 74:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ غَيۡرُ يَسِيرࣲ‏</div>

> Far from easy for the disbelievers

###### 74:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذَرۡنِي وَمَنۡ خَلَقۡتُ وَحِيدࣰ ا</div>

> Leave Me Alone (to deal) with whom I created Alone (without any means, i.e. Al-Walid bin Al-Mughirah Al-Makhzumi)

###### 74:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡتُ لَهُۥ مَالࣰ ا مَّمۡدُودࣰ ا</div>

> And then granted him resources in abundance

###### 74:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبَنِينَ شُهُودࣰ ا</div>

> And children to be by his side

###### 74:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَهَّدتُّ لَهُۥ تَمۡهِيدࣰ ا</div>

> And made life smooth and comfortable for him

###### 74:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ يَطۡمَعُ أَنۡ أَزِيدَ</div>

> After all that he desires that I should give more

###### 74:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآۖ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ لِأٓيَٰتِنَا عَنِيدࣰ ا</div>

> Nay! Verily, he has been stubborn and opposing Our Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc)

###### 74:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَأُرۡهِقُهُۥ صَعُودًا</div>

> I shall oblige him to (climb a slippery mountain in the Hell-fire called As-Sa'ud, or to) face a severe torment

###### 74:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ فَكَّرَ وَقَدَّرَ</div>

> Verily, he thought and plotted

###### 74:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقُتِلَ كَيۡفَ قَدَّرَ</div>

> So let him be cursed! How he plotted

###### 74:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ قُتِلَ كَيۡفَ قَدَّرَ</div>

> And once more let him be cursed, how he plotted

###### 74:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ نَظَرَ</div>

> Then he thought

###### 74:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ عَبَسَ وَبَسَرَ</div>

> Then he frowned and he looked in a bad tempered way

###### 74:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَدۡبَرَ وَٱسۡتَكۡبَرَ</div>

> Then he turned back and was proud

###### 74:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالَ إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا سِحۡرࣱ يُؤۡثَرُ</div>

> Then he said: "This is nothing but magic from that of old

###### 74:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هَٰذَآ إِلَّا قَوۡلُ ٱلۡبَشَرِ</div>

> This is nothing but the word of a human being

###### 74:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَأُصۡلِيهِ سَقَرَ</div>

> I will cast him into Hell-fire

###### 74:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا سَقَرُ</div>

> And what will make you know exactly what Hell-fire is

###### 74:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا تُبۡقِي وَلَا تَذَرُ</div>

> It spares not (any sinner), nor does it leave (anything unburnt)

###### 74:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَوَّاحَةࣱ لِّلۡبَشَرِ</div>

> Burning the skins

###### 74:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَيۡهَا تِسۡعَةَ عَشَرَ</div>

> Over it are nineteen (angels as guardians and keepers of Hell)

###### 74:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا جَعَلۡنَآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلنَّارِ إِلَّا مَلَٰٓئِكَةࣰۖ وَمَا جَعَلۡنَا عِدَّتَهُمۡ إِلَّا فِتۡنَةࣰ لِّلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِيَسۡتَيۡقِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَيَزۡدَادَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِيمَٰنࣰ ا وَلَا يَرۡتَابَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُواْ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنُونَ وَلِيَقُولَ ٱلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِم مَّرَضࣱ وَٱلۡكَٰفِرُونَ مَاذَآ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ بِهَٰذَا مَثَلࣰ اۚ كَذَٰلِكَ يُضِلُّ ٱللَّهُ مَن يَشَآءُ وَيَهۡدِي مَن يَشَآءُۚ وَمَا يَعۡلَمُ جُنُودَ رَبِّكَ إِلَّا هُوَۚ وَمَا هِيَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرَىٰ لِلۡبَشَرِ</div>

> And We have set none but angels as guardians of the Fire, and We have fixed their number (19) only as a trial for the disbelievers, in order that the people of the Scripture (Jews and Christians) may arrive at a certainty [that this Quran is the truth as it agrees with their Books i.e. their number (19) is written in the Taurat (Torah) and the Injeel (Gospel)] and the believers may increase in Faith (as this Quran is the truth) and that no doubts may be left for the people of the Scripture and the believers, and that those in whose hearts is a disease (of hypocrisy) and the disbelievers may say: "What Allah intends by this (curious) example?" Thus Allah leads astray whom He wills and guides whom He wills. And none can know the hosts of your Lord but He. And this (Hell) is nothing else than a (warning) reminder to mankind

###### 74:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا وَٱلۡقَمَرِ</div>

> Nay, and by the moon

###### 74:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذۡ أَدۡبَرَ</div>

> And by the night when it withdraws

###### 74:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلصُّبۡحِ إِذَآ أَسۡفَرَ</div>

> And by the dawn when it brightens

###### 74:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهَا لَإِحۡدَى ٱلۡكُبَرِ</div>

> Verily, it (Hell, or their denial of the Prophet Muhammad SAW, or the Day of Resurrection) is but one of the greatest calamities

###### 74:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَذِيرࣰ ا لِّلۡبَشَرِ</div>

> A warning to mankind

###### 74:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَتَقَدَّمَ أَوۡ يَتَأَخَّرَ</div>

> To any of you that chooses to go forward (by working righteous deeds), or to remain behind (by commiting sins)

###### 74:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كُلُّ نَفۡسِۭ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ رَهِينَةٌ</div>

> Every person is a pledge for what he has earned

###### 74:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّآ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡيَمِينِ</div>

> Except those on the Right, (i.e. the pious true believers of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 74:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جَنَّٰتࣲ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ</div>

> In Gardens (Paradise) they will ask one another

###### 74:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَنِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> About Al-Mujrimun (polytheists, criminals, disbelievers, etc.), (And they will say to them)

###### 74:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا سَلَكَكُمۡ فِي سَقَرَ</div>

> What has caused you to enter Hell

###### 74:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ لَمۡ نَكُ مِنَ ٱلۡمُصَلِّينَ</div>

> They will say: "We were not of those who used to offer their Salat (prayers)

###### 74:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَمۡ نَكُ نُطۡعِمُ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ</div>

> Nor we used to feed Al-Miskin (the poor)

###### 74:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكُنَّا نَخُوضُ مَعَ ٱلۡخَآئِضِينَ</div>

> And we used to talk falsehood (all that which Allah hated) with vain talkers

###### 74:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكُنَّا نُكَذِّبُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> And we used to belie the Day of Recompense

###### 74:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَتَّىٰٓ أَتَىٰنَا ٱلۡيَقِينُ</div>

> Until there came to us (the death) that is certain

###### 74:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا تَنفَعُهُمۡ شَفَٰعَةُ ٱلشَّٰفِعِينَ</div>

> So no intercession of intercessors will be of any use to them

###### 74:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا لَهُمۡ عَنِ ٱلتَّذۡكِرَةِ مُعۡرِضِينَ</div>

> Then what is wrong with them (i.e. the disbelievers) that they turn away from (receiving) admonition

###### 74:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَأَنَّهُمۡ حُمُرࣱ مُّسۡتَنفِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> As if they were frightened (wild) donkeys

###### 74:51
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَرَّتۡ مِن قَسۡوَرَةِۭ</div>

> Fleeing from a hunter, or a lion, or a beast of prey

###### 74:52
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ يُرِيدُ كُلُّ ٱمۡرِيࣲٕ مِّنۡهُمۡ أَن يُؤۡتَىٰ صُحُفࣰ ا مُّنَشَّرَةࣰ‏</div>

> Nay, everyone of them desires that he should be given pages spread out (coming from Allah with a writing that Islam is the right religion, and Muhammad SAW has come with the truth from Allah the Lord of the heavens and earth, etc)

###### 74:53
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّاۖ بَل لَّا يَخَافُونَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ</div>

> Nay! But they fear not the Hereafter (from Allah's punishment)

###### 74:54
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِنَّهُۥ تَذۡكِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Nay, verily, this (Quran) is an admonition

###### 74:55
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ</div>

> So whosoever will (let him read it), and receive admonition (from it)

###### 74:56
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يَذۡكُرُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ هُوَ أَهۡلُ ٱلتَّقۡوَىٰ وَأَهۡلُ ٱلۡمَغۡفِرَةِ</div>

> And they will not receive admonition unless Allah wills; He (Allah) is the One, deserving that mankind should be afraid of, and should be dutiful to Him, and should not take any Ilah (God) along with Him, and He is the One Who forgives (sins)
