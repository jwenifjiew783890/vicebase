# 75. Al-Qiyaama — The Resurrection

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْقِيَامَةِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 40 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

---

###### 75:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ</div>

> I swear by the Day of Resurrection

###### 75:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلنَّفۡسِ ٱللَّوَّامَةِ</div>

> And I swear by the self-reproaching person (a believer)

###### 75:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَحۡسَبُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَلَّن نَّجۡمَعَ عِظَامَهُۥ</div>

> Does man (a disbeliever) think that We shall not assemble his bones

###### 75:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلَىٰ قَٰدِرِينَ عَلَىٰٓ أَن نُّسَوِّيَ بَنَانَهُۥ</div>

> Yes, We are Able to put together in perfect order the tips of his fingers

###### 75:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ يُرِيدُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ لِيَفۡجُرَ أَمَامَهُۥ</div>

> Nay! (Man denies Resurrection and Reckoning. So) he desires to continue committing sins

###### 75:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَسۡـَٔلُ أَيَّانَ يَوۡمُ ٱلۡقِيَٰمَةِ</div>

> He asks: "When will be this Day of Resurrection

###### 75:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا بَرِقَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ</div>

> So, when the sight shall be dazed

###### 75:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَخَسَفَ ٱلۡقَمَرُ</div>

> And the moon will be eclipsed

###### 75:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجُمِعَ ٱلشَّمۡسُ وَٱلۡقَمَرُ</div>

> And the sun and moon will be joined together (by going one into the other or folded up or deprived of their light, etc)

###### 75:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَقُولُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ يَوۡمَئِذٍ أَيۡنَ ٱلۡمَفَرُّ</div>

> On that Day man will say: "Where (is the refuge) to flee

###### 75:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا لَا وَزَرَ</div>

> No! There is no refuge

###### 75:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقَرُّ</div>

> Unto your Lord (Alone) will be the place of rest that Day

###### 75:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُنَبَّؤُاْ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بِمَا قَدَّمَ وَأَخَّرَ</div>

> On that Day man will be informed of what he sent forward (of his evil or good deeds), and what he left behind (of his good or evil traditions)

###### 75:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ عَلَىٰ نَفۡسِهِۦ بَصِيرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Nay! Man will be a witness against himself [as his body parts (skin, hands, legs, etc.) will speak about his deeds

###### 75:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَوۡ أَلۡقَىٰ مَعَاذِيرَهُۥ</div>

> Though he may put forth his excuses (to cover his evil deeds)

###### 75:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا تُحَرِّكۡ بِهِۦ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعۡجَلَ بِهِۦٓ</div>

> Move not your tongue concerning (the Quran, O Muhammad SAW) to make haste therewith

###### 75:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا جَمۡعَهُۥ وَقُرۡءَانَهُۥ</div>

> It is for Us to collect it and to give you (O Muhammad SAW) the ability to recite it (the Quran)

###### 75:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا قَرَأۡنَٰهُ فَٱتَّبِعۡ قُرۡءَانَهُۥ</div>

> And when We have recited it to you [O Muhammad SAW through Jibrael (Gabriel)], then follow you its (the Quran's) recital

###### 75:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا بَيَانَهُۥ</div>

> Then it is for Us (Allah) to make it clear to you

###### 75:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا بَلۡ تُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ</div>

> Not [as you think, that you (mankind) will not be resurrected and recompensed for your deeds], but (you men) love the present life of this world

###### 75:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَذَرُونَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ</div>

> And leave (neglect) the Hereafter

###### 75:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ نَّاضِرَةٌ</div>

> Some faces that Day shall be Nadirah (shining and radiant)

###### 75:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ رَبِّهَا نَاظِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Looking at their Lord (Allah)

###### 75:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بَاسِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> And some faces, that Day, will be Basirah (dark, gloomy, frowning, and sad)

###### 75:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَظُنُّ أَن يُفۡعَلَ بِهَا فَاقِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Thinking that some calamity was about to fall on them

###### 75:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِذَا بَلَغَتِ ٱلتَّرَاقِيَ</div>

> Nay, when (the soul) reaches to the collar bone (i.e. up to the throat in its exit)

###### 75:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقِيلَ مَنۡۜ رَاقࣲ‏</div>

> And it will be said: "Who can cure him and save him from death

###### 75:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَظَنَّ أَنَّهُ ٱلۡفِرَاقُ</div>

> And he (the dying person) will conclude that it was (the time) of departing (death)

###### 75:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡتَفَّتِ ٱلسَّاقُ بِٱلسَّاقِ</div>

> And leg will be joined with another leg (shrouded)

###### 75:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ يَوۡمَئِذٍ ٱلۡمَسَاقُ</div>

> The drive will be, on that Day, to your Lord (Allah)

###### 75:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَا صَدَّقَ وَلَا صَلَّىٰ</div>

> So he (the disbeliever) neither believed (in this Quran, in the Message of Muhammad SAW) nor prayed

###### 75:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَٰكِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ</div>

> But on the contrary, he belied (this Quran and the Message of Muhammad SAW) and turned away

###### 75:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ ذَهَبَ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ يَتَمَطَّىٰٓ</div>

> Then he walked in full pride to his family admiring himself

###### 75:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوۡلَىٰ لَكَ فَأَوۡلَىٰ</div>

> Woe to you [O man (disbeliever)]! And then (again) woe to you

###### 75:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَوۡلَىٰ لَكَ فَأَوۡلَىٰٓ</div>

> Again, woe to you [O man (disbeliever)]! And then (again) woe to you

###### 75:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَحۡسَبُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَن يُتۡرَكَ سُدًى</div>

> Does man think that he will be left Suda [neglected without being punished or rewarded for the obligatory duties enjoined by his Lord (Allah) on him

###### 75:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ يَكُ نُطۡفَةࣰ مِّن مَّنِيࣲّ يُمۡنَىٰ</div>

> Was he not a Nutfah (mixed male and female discharge of semen) poured forth

###### 75:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ كَانَ عَلَقَةࣰ فَخَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ</div>

> Then he became an 'Alaqa (a clot); then (Allah) shaped and fashioned (him) in due proportion

###### 75:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَجَعَلَ مِنۡهُ ٱلزَّوۡجَيۡنِ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ</div>

> And made him in two sexes, male and female

###### 75:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَيۡسَ ذَٰلِكَ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يُحۡـِۧيَ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ</div>

> Is not He (Allah Who does that), Able to give life to the dead? (Yes! He is Able to do all things)
