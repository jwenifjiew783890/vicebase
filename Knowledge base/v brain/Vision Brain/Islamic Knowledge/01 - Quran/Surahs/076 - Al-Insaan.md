# 76. Al-Insaan — Man

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الدَّهْرِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Madina · **Verses:** 31 · **Juz':** 29

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 76:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ أَتَىٰ عَلَى ٱلۡإِنسَٰنِ حِينࣱ مِّنَ ٱلدَّهۡرِ لَمۡ يَكُن شَيۡـࣰٔ ا مَّذۡكُورًا</div>

> Has there not been over man a period of time, when he was nothing to be mentioned

###### 76:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ أَمۡشَاجࣲ نَّبۡتَلِيهِ فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ سَمِيعَۢا بَصِيرًا</div>

> Verily, We have created man from Nutfah drops of mixed semen (discharge of man and woman), in order to try him, so We made him hearer, seer

###### 76:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا هَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلسَّبِيلَ إِمَّا شَاكِرࣰ ا وَإِمَّا كَفُورًا</div>

> Verily, We showed him the way, whether he be grateful or ungrateful

###### 76:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَعۡتَدۡنَا لِلۡكَٰفِرِينَ سَلَٰسِلَاْ وَأَغۡلَٰلࣰ ا وَسَعِيرًا</div>

> Verily, We have prepared for the disbelievers iron chains, iron collars, and a blazing Fire

###### 76:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ يَشۡرَبُونَ مِن كَأۡسࣲ كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُورًا</div>

> Verily, the Abrar (pious, who fear Allah and avoid evil), shall drink a cup (of wine) mixed with water from a spring in Paradise called Kafur

###### 76:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَيۡنࣰ ا يَشۡرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ ٱللَّهِ يُفَجِّرُونَهَا تَفۡجِيرࣰ ا</div>

> A spring wherefrom the slaves of Allah will drink, causing it to gush forth abundantly

###### 76:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُوفُونَ بِٱلنَّذۡرِ وَيَخَافُونَ يَوۡمࣰ ا كَانَ شَرُّهُۥ مُسۡتَطِيرࣰ ا</div>

> They (are those who) fulfill (their) vows, and they fear a Day whose evil will be wide-spreading

###### 76:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيُطۡعِمُونَ ٱلطَّعَامَ عَلَىٰ حُبِّهِۦ مِسۡكِينࣰ ا وَيَتِيمࣰ ا وَأَسِيرًا</div>

> And they give food, inspite of their love for it (or for the love of Him), to Miskin (poor), the orphan, and the captive

###### 76:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَا نُطۡعِمُكُمۡ لِوَجۡهِ ٱللَّهِ لَا نُرِيدُ مِنكُمۡ جَزَآءࣰ وَلَا شُكُورًا</div>

> (Saying): "We feed you seeking Allah's Countenance only. We wish for no reward, nor thanks from you

###### 76:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَخَافُ مِن رَّبِّنَا يَوۡمًا عَبُوسࣰ ا قَمۡطَرِيرࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, We fear from our Lord a Day, hard and distressful, that will make the faces look horrible (from extreme dislikeness to it)

###### 76:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَقَىٰهُمُ ٱللَّهُ شَرَّ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمِ وَلَقَّىٰهُمۡ نَضۡرَةࣰ وَسُرُورࣰ ا</div>

> So Allah saved them from the evil of that Day, and gave them Nadratan (a light of beauty) and joy

###### 76:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَزَىٰهُم بِمَا صَبَرُواْ جَنَّةࣰ وَحَرِيرࣰ ا</div>

> And their recompense shall be Paradise, and silken garments, because they were patient

###### 76:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُّتَّكِـِٔينَ فِيهَا عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِۖ لَا يَرَوۡنَ فِيهَا شَمۡسࣰ ا وَلَا زَمۡهَرِيرࣰ ا</div>

> Reclining therein on raised thrones, they will see there neither the excessive heat of the sun, nor the excessive bitter cold, (as in Paradise there is no sun and no moon)

###### 76:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَدَانِيَةً عَلَيۡهِمۡ ظِلَٰلُهَا وَذُلِّلَتۡ قُطُوفُهَا تَذۡلِيلࣰ ا</div>

> And the shade thereof is close upon them, and the bunches of fruit thereof will hang low within their reach

###### 76:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيُطَافُ عَلَيۡهِم بِـَٔانِيَةࣲ مِّن فِضَّةࣲ وَأَكۡوَابࣲ كَانَتۡ قَوَارِيرَا۠</div>

> And amongst them will be passed round vessels of silver and cups of crystal

###### 76:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَوَارِيرَاْ مِن فِضَّةࣲ قَدَّرُوهَا تَقۡدِيرࣰ ا</div>

> Crystal-clear, made of silver. They will determine the measure thereof according to their wishes

###### 76:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيُسۡقَوۡنَ فِيهَا كَأۡسࣰ ا كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنجَبِيلًا</div>

> And they will be given to drink there a cup (of wine) mixed with Zanjabil (ginger, etc)

###### 76:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَيۡنࣰ ا فِيهَا تُسَمَّىٰ سَلۡسَبِيلࣰ ا</div>

> A spring there, called Salsabil

###### 76:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞وَيَطُوفُ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وِلۡدَٰنࣱ مُّخَلَّدُونَ إِذَا رَأَيۡتَهُمۡ حَسِبۡتَهُمۡ لُؤۡلُؤࣰ ا مَّنثُورࣰ ا</div>

> And round about them will (serve) boys of everlasting youth. If you see them, you would think them scattered pearls

###### 76:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا رَأَيۡتَ ثَمَّ رَأَيۡتَ نَعِيمࣰ ا وَمُلۡكࣰ ا كَبِيرًا</div>

> And when you look there (in Paradise), you will see a delight (that cannot be imagined), and a great dominion

###### 76:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَٰلِيَهُمۡ ثِيَابُ سُندُسٍ خُضۡرࣱ وَإِسۡتَبۡرَقࣱۖ وَحُلُّوٓاْ أَسَاوِرَ مِن فِضَّةࣲ وَسَقَىٰهُمۡ رَبُّهُمۡ شَرَابࣰ ا طَهُورًا</div>

> Their garments will be of fine green silk, and gold embroidery. They will be adorned with bracelets of silver, and their Lord will give them a pure drink

###### 76:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذَا كَانَ لَكُمۡ جَزَآءࣰ وَكَانَ سَعۡيُكُم مَّشۡكُورًا</div>

> (And it will be said to them): "Verily, this is a reward for you, and your endeavour has been accepted

###### 76:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نَزَّلۡنَا عَلَيۡكَ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانَ تَنزِيلࣰ ا</div>

> Verily! It is We Who have sent down the Quran to you (O Muhammad SAW) by stages

###### 76:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱصۡبِرۡ لِحُكۡمِ رَبِّكَ وَلَا تُطِعۡ مِنۡهُمۡ ءَاثِمًا أَوۡ كَفُورࣰ ا</div>

> Therefore be patient (O Muhammad SAW) and submit to the Command of your Lord (Allah, by doing your duty to Him and by conveying His Message to mankind), and obey neither a sinner nor a disbeliever among them

###### 76:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱذۡكُرِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ بُكۡرَةࣰ وَأَصِيلࣰ ا</div>

> And remember the Name of your Lord every morning and afternoon [i.e. offering of the Morning (Fajr), Zuhr, and 'Asr prayers

###### 76:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِنَ ٱلَّيۡلِ فَٱسۡجُدۡ لَهُۥ وَسَبِّحۡهُ لَيۡلࣰ ا طَوِيلًا</div>

> And during night, prostrate yourself to Him (i.e. the offering of Maghrib and 'Isha' prayers), and glorify Him a long night through (i.e. Tahajjud prayer)

###### 76:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ يُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡعَاجِلَةَ وَيَذَرُونَ وَرَآءَهُمۡ يَوۡمࣰ ا ثَقِيلࣰ ا</div>

> Verily! These (disbelievers) love the present life of this world, and put behind them a heavy Day (that will be hard)

###### 76:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَّحۡنُ خَلَقۡنَٰهُمۡ وَشَدَدۡنَآ أَسۡرَهُمۡۖ وَإِذَا شِئۡنَا بَدَّلۡنَآ أَمۡثَٰلَهُمۡ تَبۡدِيلًا</div>

> It is We Who created them, and We have made them of strong built. And when We will, We can replace them with others like them with a complete replacement

###### 76:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةࣱۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلࣰ ا</div>

> Verily! This (Verses of the Quran) is an admonition, so whosoever wills, let him take a Path to his Lord (Allah)

###### 76:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ عَلِيمًا حَكِيمࣰ ا</div>

> But you cannot will, unless Allah wills. Verily, Allah is Ever All-Knowing, All-Wise

###### 76:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُدۡخِلُ مَن يَشَآءُ فِي رَحۡمَتِهِۦۚ وَٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ أَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ عَذَابًا أَلِيمَۢا</div>

> He will admit to His Mercy whom He will and as for the Zalimun, (polytheists, wrong-doers, etc.) He has prepared a painful torment
