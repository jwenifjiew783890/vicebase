# 77. Al-Mursalaat — The Emissaries

###### 77:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡمُرۡسَلَٰتِ عُرۡفࣰ ا</div>

> By the winds (or angels or the Messengers of Allah) sent forth one after another

###### 77:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡعَٰصِفَٰتِ عَصۡفࣰ ا</div>

> And by the winds that blow violently

###### 77:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّٰشِرَٰتِ نَشۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And by the winds that scatter clouds and rain

###### 77:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡفَٰرِقَٰتِ فَرۡقࣰ ا</div>

> And by the Verses (of the Quran) that separate the right from the wrong

###### 77:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡمُلۡقِيَٰتِ ذِكۡرًا</div>

> And by the angels that bring the revelations to the Messengers

###### 77:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عُذۡرًا أَوۡ نُذۡرًا</div>

> To cut off all excuses or to warn

###### 77:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَا تُوعَدُونَ لَوَٰقِعࣱ‏</div>

> Surely, what you are promised must come to pass

###### 77:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ طُمِسَتۡ</div>

> Then when the stars lose their lights

###### 77:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ فُرِجَتۡ</div>

> And when the heaven is cleft asunder

###### 77:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ نُسِفَتۡ</div>

> And when the mountains are blown away

###### 77:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلرُّسُلُ أُقِّتَتۡ</div>

> And when the Messengers are gathered to their time appointed

###### 77:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِأَيِّ يَوۡمٍ أُجِّلَتۡ</div>

> For what Day are these signs postponed

###### 77:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِيَوۡمِ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ</div>

> For the Day of sorting out (the men of Paradise from the men destined for Hell)

###### 77:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ</div>

> And what will explain to you what is the Day of sorting out

###### 77:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ نُهۡلِكِ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> Did We not destroy the ancients

###### 77:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ نُتۡبِعُهُمُ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ</div>

> So shall We make later generations to follow them

###### 77:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَٰلِكَ نَفۡعَلُ بِٱلۡمُجۡرِمِينَ</div>

> Thus do We deal with the Mujrimun (polytheists, disbelievers, sinners, criminals, etc)

###### 77:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ نَخۡلُقكُّم مِّن مَّآءࣲ مَّهِينࣲ‏</div>

> Did We not create you from a worthless water (semen, etc)

###### 77:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَجَعَلۡنَٰهُ فِي قَرَارࣲ مَّكِينٍ</div>

> Then We placed it in a place of safety (womb)

###### 77:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ قَدَرࣲ مَّعۡلُومࣲ‏</div>

> For a known period (determined by gestation)

###### 77:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَدَرۡنَا فَنِعۡمَ ٱلۡقَٰدِرُونَ</div>

> So We did measure, and We are the Best to measure (the things)

###### 77:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ كِفَاتًا</div>

> Have We not made the earth a receptacle

###### 77:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَحۡيَآءࣰ وَأَمۡوَٰتࣰ ا</div>

> For the living and the dead

###### 77:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ شَٰمِخَٰتࣲ وَأَسۡقَيۡنَٰكُم مَّآءࣰ فُرَاتࣰ ا</div>

> And have placed therein firm, and tall mountains; and have given you to drink sweet water

###### 77:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱنطَلِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ مَا كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> (It will be said to the disbelievers): "Depart you to that which you used to deny

###### 77:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱنطَلِقُوٓاْ إِلَىٰ ظِلࣲّ ذِي ثَلَٰثِ شُعَبࣲ‏</div>

> Depart you to a shadow (of Hell-fire smoke ascending) in three columns

###### 77:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا ظَلِيلࣲ وَلَا يُغۡنِي مِنَ ٱللَّهَبِ</div>

> Neither shading, nor of any use against the fierce flame of the Fire

###### 77:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهَا تَرۡمِي بِشَرَرࣲ كَٱلۡقَصۡرِ</div>

> Verily! It (Hell) throws sparks (huge) as Al-Qasr [a fort or a Qasr (huge log of wood]

###### 77:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَأَنَّهُۥ جِمَٰلَتࣱ صُفۡرࣱ‏</div>

> As if they were Jimalatun Sufr (yellow camels or bundles of ropes)

###### 77:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ لَا يَنطِقُونَ</div>

> That will be a Day when they shall not speak (during some part of it)

###### 77:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يُؤۡذَنُ لَهُمۡ فَيَعۡتَذِرُونَ</div>

> And they will not be permitted to put forth any excuse

###### 77:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَٰذَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِۖ جَمَعۡنَٰكُمۡ وَٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> That will be a Day of Decision! We have brought you and the men of old together

###### 77:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِن كَانَ لَكُمۡ كَيۡدࣱ فَكِيدُونِ</div>

> So if you have a plot, use it against Me (Allah)

###### 77:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي ظِلَٰلࣲ وَعُيُونࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, the Muttaqun (pious - see V. 2:2) shall be amidst shades and springs

###### 77:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَوَٰكِهَ مِمَّا يَشۡتَهُونَ</div>

> And fruits, such as they desire

###### 77:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كُلُواْ وَٱشۡرَبُواْ هَنِيٓـَٔۢا بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ</div>

> Eat and drink comfortably for that which you used to do

###### 77:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجۡزِي ٱلۡمُحۡسِنِينَ</div>

> Verily, thus We reward the Muhsinun (good-doers)

###### 77:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كُلُواْ وَتَمَتَّعُواْ قَلِيلًا إِنَّكُم مُّجۡرِمُونَ</div>

> (O you disbelievers)! Eat and enjoy yourselves (in this worldly life) for a little while. Verily, you are the Mujrimun (polytheists, disbelievers, sinners, criminals, etc)

###### 77:47
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:48
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱرۡكَعُواْ لَا يَرۡكَعُونَ</div>

> And when it is said to them: "Bow down yourself (in prayer)!" They bow not down (offer not their prayers)

###### 77:49
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe that Day to the deniers (of the Day of Resurrection)

###### 77:50
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَبِأَيِّ حَدِيثِۭ بَعۡدَهُۥ يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> Then in what statement after this (the Quran) will they believe
