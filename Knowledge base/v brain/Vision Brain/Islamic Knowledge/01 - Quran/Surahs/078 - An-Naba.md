# 78. An-Naba — The Announcement

###### 78:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ</div>

> What are they asking (one another)

###### 78:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ</div>

> About the great news, (i.e. Islamic Monotheism, the Quran, which Prophet Muhammad (Peace be upon him) brought and the Day of Resurrection, etc

###### 78:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي هُمۡ فِيهِ مُخۡتَلِفُونَ</div>

> About which they are in disagreement

###### 78:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Nay, they will come to know

###### 78:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Nay, again, they will come to know

###### 78:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِهَٰدࣰ ا</div>

> Have We not made the earth as a bed

###### 78:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَوۡتَادࣰ ا</div>

> And the mountains as pegs

###### 78:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَخَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجࣰ ا</div>

> And We have created you in pairs (male and female, tall and short, good and bad, etc)

###### 78:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا نَوۡمَكُمۡ سُبَاتࣰ ا</div>

> And have made your sleep as a thing for rest

###### 78:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلَّيۡلَ لِبَاسࣰ ا</div>

> And have made the night as a covering (through its darkness)

###### 78:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلنَّهَارَ مَعَاشࣰ ا</div>

> And have made the day for livelihood

###### 78:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبَنَيۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعࣰ ا شِدَادࣰ ا</div>

> And We have built above you seven strong (heavens)

###### 78:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَعَلۡنَا سِرَاجࣰ ا وَهَّاجࣰ ا</div>

> And have made (therein) a shinning lamp (sun)

###### 78:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُعۡصِرَٰتِ مَآءࣰ ثَجَّاجࣰ ا</div>

> And have sent down from the rainy clouds abundant water

###### 78:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّنُخۡرِجَ بِهِۦ حَبࣰّ ا وَنَبَاتࣰ ا</div>

> That We may produce therewith corn and vegetations

###### 78:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَنَّٰتٍ أَلۡفَافًا</div>

> And gardens of thick growth

###### 78:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ كَانَ مِيقَٰتࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, the Day of Decision is a fixed time

###### 78:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَتَأۡتُونَ أَفۡوَاجࣰ ا</div>

> The Day when the Trumpet will be blown, and you shall come forth in crowds (groups)

###### 78:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفُتِحَتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبࣰ ا</div>

> And the heaven shall be opened, and it will become as gates

###### 78:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَسُيِّرَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ فَكَانَتۡ سَرَابًا</div>

> And the mountains shall be moved away from their places and they will be as if they were a mirage

###### 78:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتۡ مِرۡصَادࣰ ا</div>

> Truly, Hell is a place of ambush

###### 78:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّلطَّٰغِينَ مَـَٔابࣰ ا</div>

> A dwelling place for the Taghun (those who transgress the boundry limits set by Allah like polytheists, disbelievers in the Oneness of Allah, hyprocrites, sinners, criminals, etc)

###### 78:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّٰبِثِينَ فِيهَآ أَحۡقَابࣰ ا</div>

> They will abide therein for ages

###### 78:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا بَرۡدࣰ ا وَلَا شَرَابًا</div>

> Nothing cool shall they taste therein, nor any drink

###### 78:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا حَمِيمࣰ ا وَغَسَّاقࣰ ا</div>

> Except boiling water, and dirty wound discharges

###### 78:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">جَزَآءࣰ وِفَاقًا</div>

> An exact recompense (according to their evil crimes)

###### 78:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَرۡجُونَ حِسَابࣰ ا</div>

> For verily, they used not to look for a reckoning

###### 78:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا كِذَّابࣰ ا</div>

> But they belied Our Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, and that which Our Prophet (Peace be upon him) brought) completely

###### 78:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ كِتَٰبࣰ ا</div>

> And all things We have recorded in a Book

###### 78:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذُوقُواْ فَلَن نَّزِيدَكُمۡ إِلَّا عَذَابًا</div>

> So taste you (the results of your evil actions); no increase shall We give you, except in torment

###### 78:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ مَفَازًا</div>

> Verily, for the Muttaqun, there will be a success (Paradise)

###### 78:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَدَآئِقَ وَأَعۡنَٰبࣰ ا</div>

> Gardens and grapeyards

###### 78:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَوَاعِبَ أَتۡرَابࣰ ا</div>

> And young full-breasted (mature) maidens of equal age

###### 78:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَأۡسࣰ ا دِهَاقࣰ ا</div>

> And a full cup (of wine)

###### 78:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوࣰ ا وَلَا كِذَّٰبࣰ ا</div>

> No Laghw (dirty, false, evil talk) shall they hear therein, nor lying

###### 78:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">جَزَآءࣰ مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَآءً حِسَابࣰ ا</div>

> A reward from your Lord, an ample calculated gift (according to the best of their good deeds)

###### 78:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَّبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۖ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِنۡهُ خِطَابࣰ ا</div>

> (From) the Lord of the heavens and the earth, and whatsoever is in between them, the Most Beneficent, none can dare to speak with Him (on the Day of Resurrection except after His Leave)

###### 78:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ صَفࣰّ اۖ لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابࣰ ا</div>

> The Day that Ar-Ruh [Jibrael (Gabriel) or another angel] and the angels will stand forth in rows, none shall speak except him whom the Most Beneficent (Allah) allows, and he will speak what is right

###### 78:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا</div>

> That is without doubt the True Day, so, whosoever wills, let him seek a place with (or a way to) His Lord (by obeying Him in this worldly life)

###### 78:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّآ أَنذَرۡنَٰكُمۡ عَذَابࣰ ا قَرِيبࣰ ا يَوۡمَ يَنظُرُ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَٰبَۢا</div>

> Verily, We have warned you of a near torment, the Day when man will see that (the deeds) which his hands have sent forth, and the disbeliever will say: "Woe to me! Would that I were dust
