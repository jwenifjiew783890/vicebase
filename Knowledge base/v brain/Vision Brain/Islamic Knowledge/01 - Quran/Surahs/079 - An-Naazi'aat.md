# 79. An-Naazi'aat — Those who drag forth

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ النَّازِعَاتِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 46 · **Juz':** 30

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 79:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّٰزِعَٰتِ غَرۡقࣰ ا</div>

> By those (angels) who pull out (the souls of the disbelievers and the wicked) with great violence

###### 79:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّٰشِطَٰتِ نَشۡطࣰ ا</div>

> By those (angels) who gently take out (the souls of the believers)

###### 79:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّٰبِحَٰتِ سَبۡحࣰ ا</div>

> And by those that swim along (i.e. angels or planets in their orbits, etc)

###### 79:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلسَّٰبِقَٰتِ سَبۡقࣰ ا</div>

> And by those that press forward as in a race (i.e. the angels or stars or the horses, etc)

###### 79:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡمُدَبِّرَٰتِ أَمۡرࣰ ا</div>

> And by those angels who arrange to do the Commands of their Lord, (so verily, you disbelievers will be called to account)

###### 79:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ تَرۡجُفُ ٱلرَّاجِفَةُ</div>

> On the Day (when the first blowing of the Trumpet is blown), the earth and the mountains will shake violently (and everybody will die)

###### 79:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَتۡبَعُهَا ٱلرَّادِفَةُ</div>

> The second blowing of the Trumpet follows it (and everybody will be raised up)

###### 79:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلُوبࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ وَاجِفَةٌ</div>

> (Some) hearts that Day will shake with fear and anxiety

###### 79:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَبۡصَٰرُهَا خَٰشِعَةࣱ‏</div>

> Their eyes cast down

###### 79:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَقُولُونَ أَءِنَّا لَمَرۡدُودُونَ فِي ٱلۡحَافِرَةِ</div>

> They say: "Shall we indeed be returned to (our) former state of life

###### 79:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَءِذَا كُنَّا عِظَٰمࣰ ا نَّخِرَةࣰ‏</div>

> Even after we are crumbled bones

###### 79:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَالُواْ تِلۡكَ إِذࣰ ا كَرَّةٌ خَاسِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> They say: "It would in that case, be a return with loss

###### 79:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ زَجۡرَةࣱ وَٰحِدَةࣱ‏</div>

> But only, it will be a single Zajrah [shout (i.e., the second blowing of the Trumpet)]. (See Verse)

###### 79:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا هُم بِٱلسَّاهِرَةِ</div>

> When, behold, they find themselves over the earth alive after their death

###### 79:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ</div>

> Has there come to you the story of Musa (Moses)

###### 79:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ نَادَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوًى</div>

> When his Lord called him in the sacred valley of Tuwa

###### 79:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱذۡهَبۡ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ</div>

> Go to Fir'aun (Pharaoh), verily, he has transgressed all bounds (in crimes, sins, polytheism, disbelief, etc)

###### 79:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقُلۡ هَل لَّكَ إِلَىٰٓ أَن تَزَكَّىٰ</div>

> And say to him: "Would you purify yourself (from the sin of disbelief by becoming a believer)

###### 79:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَهۡدِيَكَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَتَخۡشَىٰ</div>

> And that I guide you to your Lord, so you should fear Him

###### 79:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَرَىٰهُ ٱلۡأٓيَةَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ</div>

> Then [Musa (Moses)] showed him the great sign (miracles)

###### 79:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَذَّبَ وَعَصَىٰ</div>

> But [Fir'aun (Pharaoh)] belied and disobeyed

###### 79:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَدۡبَرَ يَسۡعَىٰ</div>

> Then he turned his back, striving hard (against Allah)

###### 79:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَحَشَرَ فَنَادَىٰ</div>

> Then he gathered his people and cried aloud

###### 79:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالَ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ</div>

> Saying: "I am your lord, most high

###### 79:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَخَذَهُ ٱللَّهُ نَكَالَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰٓ</div>

> So Allah, seized him with punishment for his last [i.e. his saying: "I am your lord, most high") (see Verse 79:24)] and first [(i.e. his saying, "O chiefs! I know not that you have a god other than I" (see Verse 28:38)] transgression

###### 79:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَعِبۡرَةࣰ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰٓ</div>

> Verily, in this is an instructive admonition for whosoever fears Allah

###### 79:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ءَأَنتُمۡ أَشَدُّ خَلۡقًا أَمِ ٱلسَّمَآءُۚ بَنَىٰهَا</div>

> Are you more difficult to create, or is the heaven that He constructed

###### 79:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">رَفَعَ سَمۡكَهَا فَسَوَّىٰهَا</div>

> He raised its height, and He has equally ordered it

###### 79:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَغۡطَشَ لَيۡلَهَا وَأَخۡرَجَ ضُحَىٰهَا</div>

> Its night He covers with darkness, and its forenoon He brings out (with light)

###### 79:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ دَحَىٰهَآ</div>

> And after that He spread the earth

###### 79:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَخۡرَجَ مِنۡهَا مَآءَهَا وَمَرۡعَىٰهَا</div>

> And brought forth therefrom its water and its pasture

###### 79:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَرۡسَىٰهَا</div>

> And the mountains He has fixed firmly

###### 79:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَتَٰعࣰ ا لَّكُمۡ وَلِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ</div>

> (To be) a provision and benefit for you and your cattle

###### 79:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلطَّآمَّةُ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ</div>

> But when there comes the greatest catastrophe (i.e. the Day of Recompense, etc)

###### 79:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا سَعَىٰ</div>

> The Day when man shall remember what he strove for

###### 79:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَبُرِّزَتِ ٱلۡجَحِيمُ لِمَن يَرَىٰ</div>

> And Hell-fire shall be made apparent in full view for (every) one who sees

###### 79:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ</div>

> Then, for him who Tagha (transgressed all bounds, in disbelief, oppression and evil deeds of disobedience to Allah)

###### 79:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَءَاثَرَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا</div>

> And preferred the life of this world (by following his evil desires and lusts)

###### 79:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ</div>

> Verily, his abode will be Hell-fire

###### 79:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفۡسَ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ</div>

> But as for him who feared standing before his Lord, and restrained himself from impure evil desires, and lusts

###### 79:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ</div>

> Verily, Paradise will be his abode

###### 79:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَسۡـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِ أَيَّانَ مُرۡسَىٰهَا</div>

> They ask you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) about the Hour, - when will be its appointed time

###### 79:43
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِيمَ أَنتَ مِن ذِكۡرَىٰهَآ</div>

> You have no knowledge to say anything about it

###### 79:44
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ مُنتَهَىٰهَآ</div>

> To your Lord belongs (the knowledge of) the term thereof

###### 79:45
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرُ مَن يَخۡشَىٰهَا</div>

> You (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) are only a warner for those who fear it

###### 79:46
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَأَنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَهَا لَمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا عَشِيَّةً أَوۡ ضُحَىٰهَا</div>

> The Day they see it, (it will be) as if they had not tarried (in this world) except an afternoon or a morning
