###### 80:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ</div>

> The Prophet (Peace be upon him)) frowned and turned away

###### 80:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ</div>

> Because there came to him the blind man (i.e. 'Abdullah bin Umm-Maktum, who came to the Prophet (Peace be upon him) while he was preaching to one or some of the Quraish chiefs

###### 80:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ</div>

> But what could tell you that per chance he might become pure (from sins)

###### 80:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوۡ يَذَّكَّرُ فَتَنفَعَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰٓ</div>

> Or that he might receive admonition, and that the admonition might profit him

###### 80:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَمَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ</div>

> As for him who thinks himself self-sufficient

###### 80:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَنتَ لَهُۥ تَصَدَّىٰ</div>

> To him you attend

###### 80:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا عَلَيۡكَ أَلَّا يَزَّكَّىٰ</div>

> What does it matter to you if he will not become pure (from disbelief, you are only a Messenger, your duty is to convey the Message of Allah)

###### 80:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا مَن جَآءَكَ يَسۡعَىٰ</div>

> But as to him who came to you running

###### 80:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُوَ يَخۡشَىٰ</div>

> And is afraid (of Allah and His Punishment)

###### 80:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَنتَ عَنۡهُ تَلَهَّىٰ</div>

> Of him you are neglectful and divert your attention to another

###### 80:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِنَّهَا تَذۡكِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Nay, (do not do like this), indeed it (these Verses of this Quran) are an admonition

###### 80:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ</div>

> So whoever wills, let him pay attention to it

###### 80:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي صُحُفࣲ مُّكَرَّمَةࣲ‏</div>

> (It is) in Records held (greatly) in honour (Al-Lauh Al-Mahfuz)

###### 80:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّرۡفُوعَةࣲ مُّطَهَّرَةِۭ</div>

> Exalted (in dignity), purified

###### 80:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بِأَيۡدِي سَفَرَةࣲ‏</div>

> In the hands of scribes (angels)

###### 80:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كِرَامِۭ بَرَرَةࣲ‏</div>

> Honourable and obedient

###### 80:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُتِلَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَآ أَكۡفَرَهُۥ</div>

> Be cursed (the disbelieving) man! How ungrateful he is

###### 80:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِنۡ أَيِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥ</div>

> From what thing did He create him

###### 80:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ خَلَقَهُۥ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ</div>

> From Nutfah (male and female semen drops) He created him, and then set him in due proportion

###### 80:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ ٱلسَّبِيلَ يَسَّرَهُۥ</div>

> Then He makes the Path easy for him

###### 80:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ أَمَاتَهُۥ فَأَقۡبَرَهُۥ</div>

> Then He causes him to die, and puts him in his grave

###### 80:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِذَا شَآءَ أَنشَرَهُۥ</div>

> Then, when it is His Will, He will resurrect him (again)

###### 80:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا لَمَّا يَقۡضِ مَآ أَمَرَهُۥ</div>

> Nay, but (man) has not done what He commanded him

###### 80:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِۦٓ</div>

> Then let man look at his food

###### 80:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَنَّا صَبَبۡنَا ٱلۡمَآءَ صَبࣰّ ا</div>

> That We pour forth water in abundance

###### 80:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ شَقَقۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ شَقࣰّ ا</div>

> And We split the earth in clefts

###### 80:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا حَبࣰّ ا</div>

> And We cause therein the grain to grow

###### 80:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَعِنَبࣰ ا وَقَضۡبࣰ ا</div>

> And grapes and clover plants (i.e. green fodder for the cattle)

###### 80:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَزَيۡتُونࣰ ا وَنَخۡلࣰ ا</div>

> And olives and date-palms

###### 80:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحَدَآئِقَ غُلۡبࣰ ا</div>

> And gardens, dense with many trees

###### 80:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفَٰكِهَةࣰ وَأَبࣰّ ا</div>

> And fruits and Abba (herbage, etc)

###### 80:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَّتَٰعࣰ ا لَّكُمۡ وَلِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ</div>

> (To be) a provision and benefit for you and your cattle

###### 80:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلصَّآخَّةُ</div>

> Then, when there comes As-Sakhkhah (the Day of Resurrection's second blowing of Trumpet)

###### 80:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ</div>

> That Day shall a man flee from his brother

###### 80:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ</div>

> And from his mother and his father

###### 80:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ</div>

> And from his wife and his children

###### 80:37
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيࣲٕ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ شَأۡنࣱ يُغۡنِيهِ</div>

> Everyman, that Day, will have enough to make him careless of others

###### 80:38
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ مُّسۡفِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Some faces that Day, will be bright (true believers of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 80:39
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ضَاحِكَةࣱ مُّسۡتَبۡشِرَةࣱ‏</div>

> Laughing, rejoicing at good news (of Paradise)

###### 80:40
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَلَيۡهَا غَبَرَةࣱ‏</div>

> And other faces, that Day, will be dust-stained

###### 80:41
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَرۡهَقُهَا قَتَرَةٌ</div>

> Darkness will cover them

###### 80:42
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَفَرَةُ ٱلۡفَجَرَةُ</div>

> Such will be the Kafarah (disbelievers in Allah, in His Oneness, and in His Messenger Muhammad (Peace be upon him), etc.), the Fajarah (wicked evil doers)
