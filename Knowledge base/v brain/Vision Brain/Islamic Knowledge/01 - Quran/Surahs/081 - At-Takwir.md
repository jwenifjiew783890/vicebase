# 81. At-Takwir — The Overthrowing

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ التَّكْوِيْرِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 29 · **Juz':** 30

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 81:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ</div>

> When the sun Kuwwirat (wound round and lost its light and is overthrown)

###### 81:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتۡ</div>

> And when the stars shall fall

###### 81:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتۡ</div>

> And when the mountains shall made to pass away

###### 81:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتۡ</div>

> And when the pregnant she-camels shall be neglected

###### 81:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتۡ</div>

> And when the wild beasts shall be gathered together

###### 81:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتۡ</div>

> And when the seas shall become as blazing Fire or shall overflow

###### 81:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتۡ</div>

> And when the souls shall be joined with their bodies

###### 81:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ</div>

> And when the female (infant) buried alive (as the pagan Arabs used to do) shall be questioned

###### 81:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبࣲ قُتِلَتۡ</div>

> For what sin she was killed

###### 81:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتۡ</div>

> And when the written pages of deeds (good and bad) of every person shall be laid open

###### 81:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ كُشِطَتۡ</div>

> And when the heaven shall be stripped off and taken away from its place

###### 81:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتۡ</div>

> And when Hell-fire shall be kindled to fierce ablaze

###### 81:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَنَّةُ أُزۡلِفَتۡ</div>

> And when Paradise shall be brought near

###### 81:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّآ أَحۡضَرَتۡ</div>

> (Then) every person will know what he has brought (of good and evil)

###### 81:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلۡخُنَّسِ</div>

> So verily, I swear by the planets that recede (i.e. disappear during the day and appear during the night)

###### 81:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلۡجَوَارِ ٱلۡكُنَّسِ</div>

> And by the planets that move swiftly and hide themselves

###### 81:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا عَسۡعَسَ</div>

> And by the night as it departs

###### 81:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلصُّبۡحِ إِذَا تَنَفَّسَ</div>

> And by the dawn as it brightens

###### 81:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولࣲ كَرِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, this is the Word (this Quran brought by) a most honourable messenger [Jibrael (Gabriel), from Allah to the Prophet Muhammad (Peace be upon him)

###### 81:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذِي قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِي ٱلۡعَرۡشِ مَكِينࣲ‏</div>

> Owner of power, and high rank with (Allah) the Lord of the Throne

###### 81:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مُّطَاعࣲ ثَمَّ أَمِينࣲ‏</div>

> Obeyed (by the angels), trustworthy there (in the heavens)

###### 81:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجۡنُونࣲ‏</div>

> And (O people) your companion (Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) is not a madman

###### 81:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ</div>

> And indeed he (Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) saw him [Jibrael (Gabriel)] in the clear horizon (towards the east)

###### 81:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلۡغَيۡبِ بِضَنِينࣲ‏</div>

> And he (Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) withholds not a knowledge of the unseen

###### 81:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَيۡطَٰنࣲ رَّجِيمࣲ‏</div>

> And it (the Quran) is not the word of the outcast Shaitan (Satan)

###### 81:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَيۡنَ تَذۡهَبُونَ</div>

> Then where are you going

###### 81:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرࣱ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> Verily, this (the Quran) is no less than a Reminder to (all) the 'Alamin (mankind and jinns)

###### 81:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَسۡتَقِيمَ</div>

> To whomsoever among you who wills to walk straight

###### 81:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> And you will not, unless (it be) that Allah wills, the Lord of the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)
