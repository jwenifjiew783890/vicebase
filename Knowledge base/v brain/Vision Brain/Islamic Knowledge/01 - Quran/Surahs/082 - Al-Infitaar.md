# 82. Al-Infitaar — The Cleaving

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 19 · **Juz':** 30

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 82:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ</div>

> When the heaven is cleft asunder

###### 82:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ</div>

> And when the stars have fallen and scattered

###### 82:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ</div>

> And when the seas are burst forth (got dried up)

###### 82:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ</div>

> And when the graves are turned upside down (and they bring out their contents)

###### 82:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسࣱ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ</div>

> (Then) a person will know what he has sent forward and (what he has) left behind (of good or bad deeds)

###### 82:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ</div>

> O man! What has made you careless concerning your Lord, the Most Generous

###### 82:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ</div>

> Who created you, fashioned you perfectly, and gave you due proportion

###### 82:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةࣲ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ</div>

> In whatever form He willed, He put you together

###### 82:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا بَلۡ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ</div>

> Nay! But you deny the Recompense (reward for good deeds and punishment for evil deeds)

###### 82:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ</div>

> But verily, over you (are appointed angels in charge of mankind) to watch you

###### 82:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كِرَامࣰ ا كَٰتِبِينَ</div>

> Kiraman (honourable) Katibin writing down (your deeds)

###### 82:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ</div>

> They know all that you do

###### 82:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمࣲ‏</div>

> Verily, the Abrar (pious and righteous) will be in delight (Paradise)

###### 82:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ ٱلۡفُجَّارَ لَفِي جَحِيمࣲ‏</div>

> And verily, the Fujjar (the wicked, disbelievers, sinners and evil-doers) will be in the blazing Fire (Hell)

###### 82:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> In which they will enter, and taste its burning flame on the Day of Recompense

###### 82:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ</div>

> And they (Al-Fujjar) will not be absent therefrom (i.e. will not go out from the Hell)

###### 82:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> And what will make you know what the Day of Recompense is

###### 82:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ مَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> Again, what will make you know what the Day of Recompense is

###### 82:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ لَا تَمۡلِكُ نَفۡسࣱ لِّنَفۡسࣲ شَيۡـࣰٔ اۖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلَّهِ</div>

> (It will be) the Day when no person shall have power (to do) anything for another, and the Decision, that Day, will be (wholly) with Allah
