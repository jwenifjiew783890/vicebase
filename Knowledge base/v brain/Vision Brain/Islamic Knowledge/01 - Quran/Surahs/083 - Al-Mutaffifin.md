# 83. Al-Mutaffifin — Defrauding

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ المُطَفِّفِيْنَ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 36 · **Juz':** 30

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

###### 83:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُطَفِّفِينَ</div>

> Woe to Al-Mutaffifin [those who give less in measure and weight (decrease the rights of others]

###### 83:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ إِذَا ٱكۡتَالُواْ عَلَى ٱلنَّاسِ يَسۡتَوۡفُونَ</div>

> Those who, when they have to receive by measure from men, demand full measure

###### 83:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا كَالُوهُمۡ أَو وَّزَنُوهُمۡ يُخۡسِرُونَ</div>

> And when they have to give by measure or weight to men, give less than due

###### 83:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَا يَظُنُّ أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَنَّهُم مَّبۡعُوثُونَ</div>

> Think they not that they will be resurrected (for reckoning)

###### 83:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِيَوۡمٍ عَظِيمࣲ‏</div>

> On a Great Day

###### 83:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلنَّاسُ لِرَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ</div>

> The Day when (all) mankind will stand before the Lord of the 'Alamin (mankind, jinns and all that exists)

###### 83:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَٰبَ ٱلۡفُجَّارِ لَفِي سِجِّينࣲ‏</div>

> Nay! Truly, the Record (writing of the deeds) of the Fujjar (disbelievers, sinners, evil-doers and wicked) is (preserved) in Sijjin

###### 83:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا سِجِّينࣱ‏</div>

> And what will make you know what Sijjin is

###### 83:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كِتَٰبࣱ مَّرۡقُومࣱ‏</div>

> A Register inscribed

###### 83:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لِّلۡمُكَذِّبِينَ</div>

> Woe, that Day, to those who deny [(Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Day of Resurrection, and Al-Qadar (Divine Preordainments]

###### 83:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ يُكَذِّبُونَ بِيَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ</div>

> Those who deny the Day of Recompense

###### 83:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يُكَذِّبُ بِهِۦٓ إِلَّا كُلُّ مُعۡتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ</div>

> And none can deny it except every transgressor beyond bounds, (in disbelief, oppression and disobedience of Allah, the sinner)

###### 83:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا تُتۡلَىٰ عَلَيۡهِ ءَايَٰتُنَا قَالَ أَسَٰطِيرُ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ</div>

> When Our Verses (of the Quran) are recited to him he says: "Tales of the ancients

###### 83:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ</div>

> Nay! But on their hearts is the Ran (covering of sins and evil deeds) which they used to earn

###### 83:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِنَّهُمۡ عَن رَّبِّهِمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لَّمَحۡجُوبُونَ</div>

> Nay! Surely, they (evil-doers) will be veiled from seeing their Lord that Day

###### 83:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنَّهُمۡ لَصَالُواْ ٱلۡجَحِيمِ</div>

> Then, verily they will indeed enter and taste the burning flame of Hell

###### 83:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ يُقَالُ هَٰذَا ٱلَّذِي كُنتُم بِهِۦ تُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> Then, it will be said to them: "This is what you used to deny

###### 83:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِنَّ كِتَٰبَ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارِ لَفِي عِلِّيِّينَ</div>

> Nay! Verily, the Record (writing of the deeds) of Al-Abrar (the pious who fear Allah and avoid evil), is (preserved) in 'Illiyyun

###### 83:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا عِلِّيُّونَ</div>

> And what will make you know what 'Illiyyun is

###### 83:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كِتَٰبࣱ مَّرۡقُومࣱ‏</div>

> A Register inscribed

###### 83:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَشۡهَدُهُ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ</div>

> To which bear witness those nearest (to Allah, i.e. the angels)

###### 83:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٍ</div>

> Verily, Al-Abrar (the pious who fear Allah and avoid evil) will be in delight (Paradise)

###### 83:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ</div>

> On thrones, looking (at all things)

###### 83:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَعۡرِفُ فِي وُجُوهِهِمۡ نَضۡرَةَ ٱلنَّعِيمِ</div>

> You will recognise in their faces the brightness of delight

###### 83:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يُسۡقَوۡنَ مِن رَّحِيقࣲ مَّخۡتُومٍ</div>

> They will be given to drink pure sealed wine

###### 83:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خِتَٰمُهُۥ مِسۡكࣱۚ وَفِي ذَٰلِكَ فَلۡيَتَنَافَسِ ٱلۡمُتَنَٰفِسُونَ</div>

> The last thereof (that wine) will be the smell of musk, and for this let (all) those strive who want to strive (i.e. hasten earnestly to the obedience of Allah)

###### 83:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِزَاجُهُۥ مِن تَسۡنِيمٍ</div>

> It (that wine) will be mixed with Tasnim

###### 83:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَيۡنࣰ ا يَشۡرَبُ بِهَا ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ</div>

> A spring whereof drink those nearest to Allah

###### 83:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ أَجۡرَمُواْ كَانُواْ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ يَضۡحَكُونَ</div>

> Verily! (During the worldly life) those who committed crimes used to laugh at those who believed

###### 83:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا مَرُّواْ بِهِمۡ يَتَغَامَزُونَ</div>

> And whenever they passed by them, used to wink one to another (in mockery)

###### 83:31
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱنقَلَبُوٓاْ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمُ ٱنقَلَبُواْ فَكِهِينَ</div>

> And when they returned to their own people, they would return jesting

###### 83:32
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا رَأَوۡهُمۡ قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّ هَٰٓؤُلَآءِ لَضَآلُّونَ</div>

> And when they saw them, they said: "Verily! These have indeed gone astray

###### 83:33
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أُرۡسِلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ حَٰفِظِينَ</div>

> But they (disbelievers, sinners) had not been sent as watchers over them (the believers)

###### 83:34
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ مِنَ ٱلۡكُفَّارِ يَضۡحَكُونَ</div>

> But this Day (the Day of Resurrection) those who believe will laugh at the disbelievers

###### 83:35
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ يَنظُرُونَ</div>

> On (high) thrones, looking (at all things)

###### 83:36
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ ثُوِّبَ ٱلۡكُفَّارُ مَا كَانُواْ يَفۡعَلُونَ</div>

> Are not the disbelievers paid (fully) for what they used to do
