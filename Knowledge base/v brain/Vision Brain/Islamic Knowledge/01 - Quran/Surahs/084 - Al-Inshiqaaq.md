# 84. Al-Inshiqaaq — The Splitting Open

###### 84:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنشَقَّتۡ</div>

> When the heaven is split asunder

###### 84:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ</div>

> And listens and obeys its Lord, and it must do so

###### 84:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مُدَّتۡ</div>

> And when the earth is stretched forth

###### 84:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَلۡقَتۡ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتۡ</div>

> And has cast out all that was in it and became empty

###### 84:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ</div>

> And listens and obeys its Lord, and it must do so

###### 84:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحࣰ ا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ</div>

> O man! Verily, you are returning towards your Lord with your deeds and actions (good or bad), a sure returning, so you will meet (i.e. the results of your deeds which you did)

###### 84:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ</div>

> Then, as for him who will be given his Record in his right hand

###### 84:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَوۡفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابࣰ ا يَسِيرࣰ ا</div>

> He surely will receive an easy reckoning

###### 84:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورࣰ ا</div>

> And will return to his family in joy

###### 84:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ وَرَآءَ ظَهۡرِهِۦ</div>

> But whosoever is given his Record behind his back

###### 84:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَوۡفَ يَدۡعُواْ ثُبُورࣰ ا</div>

> He will invoke (his) destruction

###### 84:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَصۡلَىٰ سَعِيرًا</div>

> And shall enter a blazing Fire, and made to taste its burning

###### 84:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فِيٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورًا</div>

> Verily, he was among his people in joy

###### 84:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ</div>

> Verily, he thought that he would never come back (to Us)

###### 84:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلَىٰٓۚ إِنَّ رَبَّهُۥ كَانَ بِهِۦ بَصِيرࣰ ا</div>

> Yes! Verily, his Lord has been ever beholding him

###### 84:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلشَّفَقِ</div>

> So I swear by the afterglow of sunset

###### 84:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ</div>

> And by the night and whatever it gathers in its darkness

###### 84:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا ٱتَّسَقَ</div>

> And by the moon when it is at the full

###### 84:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَتَرۡكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقࣲ‏</div>

> You shall certainly travel from stage to stage (in this life and in the Hereafter)

###### 84:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا لَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ</div>

> What is the matter with them, that they believe not

###### 84:21 🕋
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لَا يَسۡجُدُونَۤ۩</div>

> And when the Quran is recited to them, they fall not prostrate

###### 84:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُكَذِّبُونَ</div>

> Nay, (on the contrary), those who disbelieve, belie (Prophet Muhammad (Peace be upon him) and whatever he brought, i.e. this Quran and Islamic Monotheism, etc

###### 84:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يُوعُونَ</div>

> And Allah knows best what they gather (of good and bad deeds)

###### 84:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ</div>

> So announce to them a painful torment

###### 84:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونِۭ</div>

> Save those who believe and do righteous good deeds, for them is a reward that will never come to an end (i.e. Paradise)
