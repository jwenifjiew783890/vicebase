# 85. Al-Burooj — The Constellations

###### 85:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡبُرُوجِ</div>

> By the heaven, holding the big stars

###### 85:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡمَوۡعُودِ</div>

> And by the Promised Day (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 85:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَشَاهِدࣲ وَمَشۡهُودࣲ‏</div>

> And by the witnessing day (i.e. Friday), and by the witnessed day [i.e. the day of 'Arafat (Hajj) the ninth of Dhul-Hijjah

###### 85:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُتِلَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأُخۡدُودِ</div>

> Cursed were the people of the ditch (the story of the Boy and the King)

###### 85:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡوَقُودِ</div>

> Fire supplied (abundantly) with fuel

###### 85:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذۡ هُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا قُعُودࣱ‏</div>

> When they sat by it (fire)

###### 85:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ شُهُودࣱ‏</div>

> And they witnessed what they were doing against the believers (i.e. burning them)

###### 85:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا نَقَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ</div>

> They had nothing against them, except that they believed in Allah, the All-Mighty, Worthy of all Praise

###### 85:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءࣲ شَهِيدٌ</div>

> Who, to Whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth! And Allah is Witness over everything

###### 85:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَتَنُواْ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَتُوبُواْ فَلَهُمۡ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابُ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ</div>

> Verily, those who put into trial the believing men and believing women (by torturing them and burning them), and then do not turn in repentance, (to Allah), will have the torment of Hell, and they will have the punishment of the burning Fire

###### 85:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتࣱ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ</div>

> Verily, those who believe and do righteous good deeds, for them will be Gardens under which rivers flow (Paradise). That is the great success

###### 85:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ بَطۡشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ</div>

> Verily, (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) the Grip (Punishment) of your Lord is severe

###### 85:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ يُبۡدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ</div>

> Verily, He it is Who begins (punishment) and repeats (punishment in the Hereafter) (or originates the creation of everything, and then repeats it on the Day of Resurrection)

###### 85:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلۡوَدُودُ</div>

> And He is Oft-Forgiving, full of love (towards the pious who are real true believers of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 85:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡمَجِيدُ</div>

> Owner of the throne, the Glorious

###### 85:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَعَّالࣱ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ</div>

> He does what He intends (or wills)

###### 85:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡجُنُودِ</div>

> Has the story reached you of the hosts

###### 85:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَثَمُودَ</div>

> Of Fir'aun (Pharaoh) and Thamud

###### 85:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي تَكۡذِيبࣲ‏</div>

> Nay! The disbelievers (persisted) in denying (Prophet Muhammad (Peace be upon him) and his Message of Islamic Monotheism

###### 85:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱللَّهُ مِن وَرَآئِهِم مُّحِيطُۢ</div>

> And Allah encompasses them from behind! (i.e. all their deeds are within His Knowledge, and He will requite them for their deeds)

###### 85:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ هُوَ قُرۡءَانࣱ مَّجِيدࣱ‏</div>

> Nay! This is a Glorious Quran

###### 85:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي لَوۡحࣲ مَّحۡفُوظِۭ</div>

> (Inscribed) in Al-Lauh Al-Mahfuz (The Preserved Tablet)
