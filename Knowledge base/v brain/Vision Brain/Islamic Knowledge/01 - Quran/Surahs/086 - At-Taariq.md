# 86. At-Taariq — The Morning Star

###### 86:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ</div>

> By the heaven, and At-Tariq (the night-comer, i.e. the bright star)

###### 86:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ</div>

> And what will make you to know what At-Tariq (night-comer) is

###### 86:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلنَّجۡمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ</div>

> (It is) the star of piercing brightness

###### 86:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِن كُلُّ نَفۡسࣲ لَّمَّا عَلَيۡهَا حَافِظࣱ‏</div>

> There is no human being but has a protector over him (or her) (i.e. angels incharge of each human being guarding him, writing his good and bad deeds, etc)

###### 86:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ</div>

> So let man see from what he is created

###### 86:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءࣲ دَافِقࣲ‏</div>

> He is created from a water gushing forth

###### 86:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَخۡرُجُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ ٱلصُّلۡبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ</div>

> Proceeding from between the back-bone and the ribs

###### 86:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجۡعِهِۦ لَقَادِرࣱ‏</div>

> Verily, (Allah) is Able to bring him back (to life)

###### 86:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ تُبۡلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ</div>

> The Day when all the secrets (deeds, prayers, fasting, etc.) will be examined (as to their truth)

###### 86:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةࣲ وَلَا نَاصِرࣲ‏</div>

> Then will (man) have no power, nor any helper

###### 86:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجۡعِ</div>

> By the sky (having rain clouds) which gives rain, again and again

###### 86:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدۡعِ</div>

> And the earth which splits (with the growth of trees and plants)

###### 86:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلࣱ فَصۡلࣱ‏</div>

> Verily! This (the Quran) is the Word that separates (the truth from falsehood, and commands strict legal laws for mankind to cut the roots of evil)

###### 86:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلۡهَزۡلِ</div>

> And it is not a thing for amusement

###### 86:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهُمۡ يَكِيدُونَ كَيۡدࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, they are but plotting a plot (against you O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)

###### 86:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَكِيدُ كَيۡدࣰ ا</div>

> And I (too) am planning a plan

###### 86:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَهِّلِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَمۡهِلۡهُمۡ رُوَيۡدَۢا</div>

> So give a respite to the disbelievers. Deal you gently with them for a while
