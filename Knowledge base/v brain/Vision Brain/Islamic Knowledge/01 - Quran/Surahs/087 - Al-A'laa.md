# 87. Al-A'laa — The Most High

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 19 · **Juz':** 30

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.

###### 87:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَبِّحِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَى</div>

> Glorify the Name of your Lord, the Most High

###### 87:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ</div>

> Who has created (everything), and then proportioned it

###### 87:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ</div>

> And Who has measured (preordainments for each and everything even to be blessed or wretched); then guided (i.e. showed mankind the right as well as wrong paths, and guided the animals to pasture)

###### 87:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ ٱلۡمَرۡعَىٰ</div>

> And Who brings out the pasturage

###### 87:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَجَعَلَهُۥ غُثَآءً أَحۡوَىٰ</div>

> And then makes it dark stubble

###### 87:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَنُقۡرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰٓ</div>

> We shall make you to recite (the Quran), so you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) shall not forget (it)

###### 87:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ وَمَا يَخۡفَىٰ</div>

> Except what Allah, may will, He knows what is apparent and what is hidden

###### 87:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ</div>

> And We shall make easy for you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) the easy way (i.e. the doing of righteous deeds)

###### 87:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَكِّرۡ إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ</div>

> Therefore remind (men) in case the reminder profits (them)

###### 87:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَيَذَّكَّرُ مَن يَخۡشَىٰ</div>

> The reminder will be received by him who fears (Allah)

###### 87:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى</div>

> But it will be avoided by the wretched

###### 87:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي يَصۡلَى ٱلنَّارَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ</div>

> Who will enter the great Fire and made to taste its burning

###### 87:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحۡيَىٰ</div>

> Wherein he will neither die (to be in rest) nor live (a good living)

###### 87:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ</div>

> Indeed whosoever purifies himself (by avoiding polytheism and accepting Islamic Monotheism) shall achieve success

###### 87:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَذَكَرَ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ</div>

> And remembers (glorifies) the Name of his Lord (worships none but Allah), and prays (five compulsory prayers and Nawafil additional prayers)

###### 87:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بَلۡ تُؤۡثِرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا</div>

> Nay, you prefer the life of this world

###### 87:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ</div>

> Although the Hereafter is better and more lasting

###### 87:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِي ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> Verily! This is in the former Scriptures

###### 87:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">صُحُفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ</div>

> The Scriptures of Ibrahim (Abraham) and Musa (Moses)
