# 88. Al-Ghaashiya — The Overwhelming

###### 88:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡغَٰشِيَةِ</div>

> Has there come to you the narration of the overwhelming (i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 88:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ</div>

> Some faces, that Day, will be humiliated (in the Hell-fire, i.e. the faces of all disbelievers, Jews and Christians, etc)

###### 88:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَامِلَةࣱ نَّاصِبَةࣱ‏</div>

> Labouring (hard in the worldly life by worshipping others besides Allah), weary (in the Hereafter with humility and disgrace)

###### 88:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَصۡلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةࣰ‏</div>

> They will enter in the hot blazing Fire

###### 88:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تُسۡقَىٰ مِنۡ عَيۡنٍ ءَانِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> They will be given to drink from a boiling spring

###### 88:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّيۡسَ لَهُمۡ طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِن ضَرِيعࣲ‏</div>

> No food will there be for them but a poisonous thorny plant

###### 88:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا يُسۡمِنُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي مِن جُوعࣲ‏</div>

> Which will neither nourish nor avail against hunger

###### 88:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وُجُوهࣱ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ نَّاعِمَةࣱ‏</div>

> (Other) faces, that Day, will be joyful

###### 88:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِّسَعۡيِهَا رَاضِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> Glad with their endeavour (for their good deeds which they did in this world, along with the true Faith of Islamic Monotheism)

###### 88:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> In a lofty Paradise

###### 88:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّا تَسۡمَعُ فِيهَا لَٰغِيَةࣰ‏</div>

> Where they shall neither hear harmful speech nor falsehood

###### 88:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِيهَا عَيۡنࣱ جَارِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> Therein will be a running spring

###### 88:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِيهَا سُرُرࣱ مَّرۡفُوعَةࣱ‏</div>

> Therein will be thrones raised high

###### 88:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَكۡوَابࣱ مَّوۡضُوعَةࣱ‏</div>

> And cups set at hand

###### 88:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَمَارِقُ مَصۡفُوفَةࣱ‏</div>

> And cushions set in rows

###### 88:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَزَرَابِيُّ مَبۡثُوثَةٌ</div>

> And rich carpets (all) spread out

###### 88:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِبِلِ كَيۡفَ خُلِقَتۡ</div>

> Do they not look at the camels, how they are created

###### 88:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ رُفِعَتۡ</div>

> And at the heaven, how it is raised

###### 88:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِلَى ٱلۡجِبَالِ كَيۡفَ نُصِبَتۡ</div>

> And at the mountains, how they are rooted and fixed firm

###### 88:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَيۡفَ سُطِحَتۡ</div>

> And at the earth, how it is spread out

###### 88:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَكِّرۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُذَكِّرࣱ‏</div>

> So remind them (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)), you are only a one who reminds

###### 88:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَّسۡتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِمُصَيۡطِرٍ</div>

> You are not a dictator over them

###### 88:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا مَن تَوَلَّىٰ وَكَفَرَ</div>

> Save the one who turns away and disbelieves

###### 88:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَيُعَذِّبُهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرَ</div>

> Then Allah will punish him with the greatest punishment

###### 88:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ إِلَيۡنَآ إِيَابَهُمۡ</div>

> Verily, to Us will be their return

###### 88:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا حِسَابَهُم</div>

> Then verily, for Us will be their reckoning
