# 89. Al-Fajr — The Dawn

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.5em">سُوْرَةُ الْفَجْرِ</div>

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 30 · **Juz':** 30

**Arabic** — Uthmani Hafs, King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex. Text is verbatim and unaltered.
**Translation** — Hilali & Khan. *A translation is an interpretation of meaning, not the Qur'an itself.*

See [[Islamic Knowledge/99 - Source & Authenticity Rules|Source & Authenticity Rules]] · [[Islamic Knowledge/01 - Quran/00 - Quran Index|Qur'an Index]]

---

###### 89:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡفَجۡرِ</div>

> By the dawn

###### 89:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَيَالٍ عَشۡرࣲ‏</div>

> By the ten nights (i.e. the first ten days of the month of Dhul-Hijjah)

###### 89:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلشَّفۡعِ وَٱلۡوَتۡرِ</div>

> And by the even and the odd (of all the creations of Allah)

###### 89:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَسۡرِ</div>

> And by the night when it departs

###### 89:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">هَلۡ فِي ذَٰلِكَ قَسَمࣱ لِّذِي حِجۡرٍ</div>

> There is indeed in them (the above oaths) sufficient proofs for men of understanding (and that, they should avoid all kinds of sins and disbeliefs, etc)

###### 89:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِعَادٍ</div>

> Did you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) not see (thought) how your Lord dealt with 'Ad (people)

###### 89:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِرَمَ ذَاتِ ٱلۡعِمَادِ</div>

> Who were very tall like lofty pillars

###### 89:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّتِي لَمۡ يُخۡلَقۡ مِثۡلُهَا فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ</div>

> The like of which were not created in the land

###### 89:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَثَمُودَ ٱلَّذِينَ جَابُواْ ٱلصَّخۡرَ بِٱلۡوَادِ</div>

> And (with) Thamud (people), who cut (hewed) out rocks in the valley (to make dwellings)

###### 89:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ ذِي ٱلۡأَوۡتَادِ</div>

> And (with) Fir'aun (Pharaoh), who had pegs (who used to torture men by binding them to pegs)

###### 89:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ طَغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ</div>

> Who did transgress beyond bounds in the lands (in the disobedience of Allah)

###### 89:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَكۡثَرُواْ فِيهَا ٱلۡفَسَادَ</div>

> And made therein much mischief

###### 89:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَصَبَّ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّكَ سَوۡطَ عَذَابٍ</div>

> So your Lord poured on them different kinds of severe torment

###### 89:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِٱلۡمِرۡصَادِ</div>

> Verily, your Lord is Ever Watchful (over them)

###### 89:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكۡرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَكۡرَمَنِ</div>

> As for man, when his Lord tries him by giving him honour and gifts, then he says (puffed up): "My Lord has honoured me

###### 89:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّآ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيۡهِ رِزۡقَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَهَٰنَنِ</div>

> But when He tries him, by straitening his means of life, he says: "My Lord has humiliated me

###### 89:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّاۖ بَل لَّا تُكۡرِمُونَ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ</div>

> Nay! But you treat not the orphans with kindness and generosity (i.e. you neither treat them well, nor give them their exact right of inheritance)

###### 89:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا تَحَٰٓضُّونَ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ</div>

> And urge not on the feeding of AlMiskin (the poor)

###### 89:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلتُّرَاثَ أَكۡلࣰ ا لَّمࣰّ ا</div>

> And you devour inheritance all with greed

###### 89:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡمَالَ حُبࣰّ ا جَمࣰّ ا</div>

> And you love wealth with much love

###### 89:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآۖ إِذَا دُكَّتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ دَكࣰّ ا دَكࣰّ ا</div>

> Nay! When the earth is ground to powder

###### 89:22
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجَآءَ رَبُّكَ وَٱلۡمَلَكُ صَفࣰّ ا صَفࣰّ ا</div>

> And your Lord comes with the angels in rows

###### 89:23
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَجِاْيٓءَ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بِجَهَنَّمَۚ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ وَأَنَّىٰ لَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ</div>

> And Hell will be brought near that Day. On that Day will man remember, but how will that remembrance (then) avail him

###### 89:24
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي قَدَّمۡتُ لِحَيَاتِي</div>

> He will say: "Alas! Would that I had sent forth (good deeds) for (this) my life

###### 89:25
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَيَوۡمَئِذࣲ لَّا يُعَذِّبُ عَذَابَهُۥٓ أَحَدࣱ‏</div>

> So on that Day, none will punish as He will punish

###### 89:26
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يُوثِقُ وَثَاقَهُۥٓ أَحَدࣱ‏</div>

> And none will bind as He will bind

###### 89:27
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ</div>

> (It will be said to the pious): "O (you) the one in (complete) rest and satisfaction

###### 89:28
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةࣰ مَّرۡضِيَّةࣰ‏</div>

> Come back to your Lord, Well-pleased (yourself) and well-pleasing unto Him

###### 89:29
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي</div>

> Enter you, then, among My honoured slaves

###### 89:30
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي</div>

> And enter you My Paradise
