###### 90:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ</div>

> I swear by this city (Makkah)

###### 90:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَنتَ حِلُّۢ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ</div>

> And you are free (from sin, to punish the enemies of Islam on the Day of the conquest) in this city (Makkah)

###### 90:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوَالِدࣲ وَمَا وَلَدَ</div>

> And by the begetter (i.e. Adam) and that which he begot (i.e. his progeny)

###### 90:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِي كَبَدٍ</div>

> Verily, We have created man in toil

###### 90:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَحۡسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ أَحَدࣱ‏</div>

> Thinks he that none can overcome him

###### 90:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَقُولُ أَهۡلَكۡتُ مَالࣰ ا لُّبَدًا</div>

> He says (boastfully): "I have wasted wealth in abundance

###### 90:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَيَحۡسَبُ أَن لَّمۡ يَرَهُۥٓ أَحَدٌ</div>

> Thinks he that none sees him

###### 90:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عَيۡنَيۡنِ</div>

> Have We not made for him a pair of eyes

###### 90:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلِسَانࣰ ا وَشَفَتَيۡنِ</div>

> And a tongue and a pair of lips

###### 90:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلنَّجۡدَيۡنِ</div>

> And shown him the two ways (good and evil)

###### 90:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلَا ٱقۡتَحَمَ ٱلۡعَقَبَةَ</div>

> But he has made no effort to pass on the path that is steep

###### 90:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡعَقَبَةُ</div>

> And what will make you know the path that is steep

###### 90:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ</div>

> (It is) Freeing a neck (slave, etc)

###### 90:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوۡ إِطۡعَٰمࣱ فِي يَوۡمࣲ ذِي مَسۡغَبَةࣲ‏</div>

> Or giving food in a day of hunger (famine)

###### 90:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَتِيمࣰ ا ذَا مَقۡرَبَةٍ</div>

> To an orphan near of kin

###### 90:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوۡ مِسۡكِينࣰ ا ذَا مَتۡرَبَةࣲ‏</div>

> Or to a Miskin (poor) afflicted with misery

###### 90:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡمَرۡحَمَةِ</div>

> Then he became one of those who believed, and recommended one another to perseverance and patience, and (also) recommended one another to pity and compassion

###### 90:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ</div>

> They are those on the Right Hand (the dwellers of Paradise)

###### 90:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا هُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ</div>

> But those who disbelieved in Our Ayat (proofs, evidences, verses, lessons, signs, revelations, etc.), they are those on the Left Hand (the dwellers of Hell)

###### 90:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَارࣱ مُّؤۡصَدَةُۢ</div>

> The Fire will be shut over them (i.e. they will be enveloped by the Fire without any opening or window or outlet)
