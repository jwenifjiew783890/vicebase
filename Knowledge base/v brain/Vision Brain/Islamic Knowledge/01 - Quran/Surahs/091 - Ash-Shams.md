###### 91:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلشَّمۡسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا</div>

> And by the sun and its brightness

###### 91:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا</div>

> And by the moon as it follows it (the sun)

###### 91:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا</div>

> And by the day as it shows up (the sun's) brightness

###### 91:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰهَا</div>

> And by the night as it conceals it (the sun)

###### 91:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا</div>

> And by the heaven and Him Who built it

###### 91:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا</div>

> And by the earth and Him Who spread it

###### 91:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَنَفۡسࣲ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا</div>

> And by Nafs (Adam or a person or a soul, etc.), and Him Who perfected him in proportion

###### 91:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا</div>

> Then He showed him what is wrong for him and what is right for him

###### 91:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا</div>

> Indeed he succeeds who purifies his ownself (i.e. obeys and performs all that Allah ordered, by following the true Faith of Islamic Monotheism and by doing righteous good deeds)

###### 91:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا</div>

> And indeed he fails who corrupts his ownself (i.e. disobeys what Allah has ordered by rejecting the true Faith of Islamic Monotheism or by following polytheism, etc. or by doing every kind of evil wicked deeds)

###### 91:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِطَغۡوَىٰهَآ</div>

> Thamud (people) denied (their Prophet) through their transgression (by rejecting the true Faith of Islamic Monotheism, and by following polytheism, and by committing every kind of sin)

###### 91:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشۡقَىٰهَا</div>

> When the most wicked man among them went forth (to kill the she-camel)

###### 91:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَقَالَ لَهُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقۡيَٰهَا</div>

> But the Messenger of Allah [Salih (Saleh)] said to them: "Be cautious! Fear the evil end. That is the she-camel of Allah! (Do not harm it) and bar it not from having its drink

###### 91:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمۡدَمَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسَوَّىٰهَا</div>

> Then they denied him and they killed it. So their Lord destroyed them because of their sin, and made them equal in destruction (i.e. all grades of people, rich and poor, strong and weak, etc)

###### 91:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقۡبَٰهَا</div>

> And He (Allah) feared not the consequences thereof
