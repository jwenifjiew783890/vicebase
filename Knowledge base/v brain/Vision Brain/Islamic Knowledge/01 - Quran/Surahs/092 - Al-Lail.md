###### 92:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰ</div>

> By the night as it envelops

###### 92:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ</div>

> And by the day as it appears in brightness

###### 92:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ</div>

> And by Him Who created male and female

###### 92:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ سَعۡيَكُمۡ لَشَتَّىٰ</div>

> Certainly, your efforts and deeds are diverse (different in aims and purposes)

###### 92:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ</div>

> As for him who gives (in charity) and keeps his duty to Allah and fears Him

###### 92:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ</div>

> And believes in Al-Husna

###### 92:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ</div>

> We will make smooth for him the path of ease (goodness)

###### 92:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ</div>

> But he who is greedy miser and thinks himself self-sufficient

###### 92:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ</div>

> And gives the lie to Al-Husna (see Verse No: 6 footnote)

###### 92:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡعُسۡرَىٰ</div>

> We will make smooth for him the path for evil

###### 92:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥٓ إِذَا تَرَدَّىٰٓ</div>

> And what will his wealth benefit him when he goes down (in destruction)

###### 92:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا لَلۡهُدَىٰ</div>

> Truly! Ours it is (to give) guidance

###### 92:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> And truly, unto Us (belong) the last (Hereafter) and the first (this world)

###### 92:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ نَارࣰ ا تَلَظَّىٰ</div>

> Therefore I have warned you of a Fire blazing fiercely (Hell)

###### 92:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَا يَصۡلَىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى</div>

> None shall enter it save the most wretched

###### 92:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ</div>

> Who denies and turns away

###### 92:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَتۡقَى</div>

> And Al-Muttaqun (the pious and righteous - see V. 2:2) will be far removed from it (Hell)

###### 92:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي يُؤۡتِي مَالَهُۥ يَتَزَكَّىٰ</div>

> He who spends his wealth for increase in self-purification

###### 92:19
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَا لِأَحَدٍ عِندَهُۥ مِن نِّعۡمَةࣲ تُجۡزَىٰٓ</div>

> And have in his mind no favour from anyone for which a reward is expected in return

###### 92:20
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ</div>

> Except only the desire to seek the Countenance of his Lord, the Most High

###### 92:21
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَسَوۡفَ يَرۡضَىٰ</div>

> He surely will be pleased (when he will enter Paradise)
