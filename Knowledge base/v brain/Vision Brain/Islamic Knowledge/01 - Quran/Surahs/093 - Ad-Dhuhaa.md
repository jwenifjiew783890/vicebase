# 93. Ad-Dhuhaa — The Morning Hours

> **Revealed in:** Mecca · **Verses:** 11 · **Juz':** 30

###### 93:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلضُّحَىٰ</div>

> By the forenoon (after sun-rise)

###### 93:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ</div>

> And by the night when it is still (or darkens)

###### 93:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ</div>

> Your Lord (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) has neither forsaken you nor hated you

###### 93:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرࣱ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ</div>

> And indeed the Hereafter is better for you than the present (life of this world)

###### 93:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ</div>

> And verily, your Lord will give you (all i.e. good) so that you shall be well-pleased

###### 93:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ يَجِدۡكَ يَتِيمࣰ ا فَـَٔاوَىٰ</div>

> Did He not find you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) an orphan and gave you a refuge

###### 93:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلࣰّ ا فَهَدَىٰ</div>

> And He found you unaware (of the Quran, its legal laws, and Prophethood, etc.) and guided you

###### 93:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلࣰ ا فَأَغۡنَىٰ</div>

> And He found you poor, and made you rich (selfsufficient with selfcontentment, etc)

###### 93:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ</div>

> Therefore, treat not the orphan with oppression

###### 93:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ</div>

> And repulse not the beggar

###### 93:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ</div>

> And proclaim the Grace of your Lord (i.e. the Prophethood and all other Graces)
