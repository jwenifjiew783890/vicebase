###### 94:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ نَشۡرَحۡ لَكَ صَدۡرَكَ</div>

> Have We not opened your breast for you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)

###### 94:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَوَضَعۡنَا عَنكَ وِزۡرَكَ</div>

> And removed from you your burden

###### 94:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهۡرَكَ</div>

> Which weighed down your back

###### 94:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَرَفَعۡنَا لَكَ ذِكۡرَكَ</div>

> And raised high your fame

###### 94:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا</div>

> So verily, with the hardship, there is relief

###### 94:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرࣰ ا</div>

> Verily, with the hardship, there is relief (i.e. there is one hardship with two reliefs, so one hardship cannot overcome two reliefs)

###### 94:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَإِذَا فَرَغۡتَ فَٱنصَبۡ</div>

> So when you have finished (from your occupation), then stand up for Allah's worship (i.e. stand up for prayer)

###### 94:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرۡغَب</div>

> And to your Lord (Alone) turn (all your intentions and hopes and) your invocations
