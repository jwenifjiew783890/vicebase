# 96. Al-Alaq — The Clot

###### 96:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱقۡرَأۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ</div>

> Read! In the Name of your Lord, Who has created (all that exists)

###### 96:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنۡ عَلَقٍ</div>

> Has created man from a clot (a piece of thick coagulated blood)

###### 96:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱقۡرَأۡ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡأَكۡرَمُ</div>

> Read! And your Lord is the Most Generous

###### 96:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِٱلۡقَلَمِ</div>

> Who has taught (the writing) by the pen [the first person to write was Prophet Idrees (Enoch]

###### 96:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَلَّمَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ</div>

> Has taught man that which he knew not

###### 96:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطۡغَىٰٓ</div>

> Nay! Verily, man does transgress all bounds (in disbelief and evil deed, etc)

###### 96:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰٓ</div>

> Because he considers himself self-sufficient

###### 96:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجۡعَىٰٓ</div>

> Surely! Unto your Lord is the return

###### 96:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يَنۡهَىٰ</div>

> Have you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) seen him (i.e. Abu Jahl) who prevents

###### 96:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">عَبۡدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ</div>

> A slave (Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) when he prays

###### 96:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَرَءَيۡتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ</div>

> Tell me, if he (Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) is on the guidance (of Allah)

###### 96:12
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَوۡ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقۡوَىٰٓ</div>

> Or enjoins piety

###### 96:13
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَرَءَيۡتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ</div>

> Tell me if he (the disbeliever, Abu Jahl) denies (the truth, i.e. this Quran), and turns away

###### 96:14
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ يَعۡلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ</div>

> Knows he not that Allah does see (what he does)

###### 96:15
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا لَئِن لَّمۡ يَنتَهِ لَنَسۡفَعَۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ</div>

> Nay! If he (Abu Jahl) ceases not, We will catch him by the forelock

###### 96:16
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَاصِيَةࣲ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةࣲ‏</div>

> A lying, sinful forelock

###### 96:17
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلۡيَدۡعُ نَادِيَهُۥ</div>

> Then, let him call upon his council (of helpers)

###### 96:18
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَنَدۡعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ</div>

> We will call the guards of Hell (to deal with him)

###### 96:19 🕋
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا لَا تُطِعۡهُ وَٱسۡجُدۡۤ وَٱقۡتَرِب۩</div>

> Nay! (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him))! Do not obey him (Abu Jahl). Fall prostrate and draw near to Allah
