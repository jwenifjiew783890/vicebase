###### 97:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلرَّحِيمِإِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةِ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ</div>

> Verily! We have sent it (this Quran) down in the night of Al-Qadr (Decree)

###### 97:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ</div>

> And what will make you know what the night of Al-Qadr (Decree) is

###### 97:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ خَيۡرࣱ مِّنۡ أَلۡفِ شَهۡرࣲ‏</div>

> The night of Al-Qadr (Decree) is better than a thousand months (i.e. worshipping Allah in that night is better than worshipping Him a thousand months, (i.e. 83 years and 4 months)

###### 97:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَنَزَّلُ ٱلۡمَلَٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمۡرࣲ‏</div>

> Therein descend the angels and the Ruh [Jibrael (Gabriel)] by Allah's Permission with all Decrees

###### 97:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">سَلَٰمٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطۡلَعِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ</div>

> Peace! (All that night, there is Peace and Goodness from Allah to His believing slaves) until the appearance of dawn
