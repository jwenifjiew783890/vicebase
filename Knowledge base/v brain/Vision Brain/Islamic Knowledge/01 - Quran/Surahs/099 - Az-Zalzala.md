###### 99:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا</div>

> When the earth is shaken with its (final) earthquake

###### 99:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا</div>

> And when the earth throws out its burdens

###### 99:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا</div>

> And man will say: "What is the matter with it

###### 99:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَئِذࣲ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا</div>

> That Day it will declare its information (about all what happened over it of good or evil)

###### 99:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا</div>

> Because your Lord has inspired it

###### 99:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَئِذࣲ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتࣰ ا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ</div>

> That Day mankind will proceed in scattered groups that they may be shown their deeds

###### 99:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرࣰ ا يَرَهُۥ</div>

> So whosoever does good equal to the weight of an atom (or a small ant), shall see it

###### 99:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةࣲ شَرࣰّ ا يَرَهُۥ</div>

> And whosoever does evil equal to the weight of an atom (or a small ant), shall see it
