###### 100:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱلۡعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبۡحࣰ ا</div>

> By the (steeds) that run, with panting (breath)

###### 100:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡمُورِيَٰتِ قَدۡحࣰ ا</div>

> Striking sparks of fire (by their hooves)

###### 100:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَٱلۡمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبۡحࣰ ا</div>

> And scouring to the raid at dawn

###### 100:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَثَرۡنَ بِهِۦ نَقۡعࣰ ا</div>

> And raise the dust in clouds the while

###### 100:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَسَطۡنَ بِهِۦ جَمۡعًا</div>

> Penetrating forthwith as one into the midst (of the foe)

###### 100:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودࣱ‏</div>

> Verily! Man (disbeliever) is ungrateful to his Lord

###### 100:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدࣱ‏</div>

> And to that fact he bears witness (by his deeds)

###### 100:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ لَشَدِيدٌ</div>

> And verily, he is violent in the love of wealth

###### 100:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">۞أَفَلَا يَعۡلَمُ إِذَا بُعۡثِرَ مَا فِي ٱلۡقُبُورِ</div>

> Knows he not that when the contents of the graves are brought out and poured forth (all mankind is resurrected)

###### 100:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ</div>

> And that which is in the breasts (of men) shall be made known

###### 100:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمۡ يَوۡمَئِذࣲ لَّخَبِيرُۢ</div>

> Verily, that Day (i.e. the Day of Resurrection) their Lord will be Well-Acquainted with them (as to their deeds), (and will reward them for their deeds)
