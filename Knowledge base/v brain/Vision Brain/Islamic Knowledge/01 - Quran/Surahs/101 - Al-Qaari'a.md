###### 101:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ</div>

> Al-Qari'ah (the striking Hour i.e. the Day of Resurrection)

###### 101:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ</div>

> What is the striking (Hour)

###### 101:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ</div>

> And what will make you know what the striking (Hour) is

###### 101:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَوۡمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلۡفَرَاشِ ٱلۡمَبۡثُوثِ</div>

> It is a Day whereon mankind will be like moths scattered about

###### 101:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ ٱلۡمَنفُوشِ</div>

> And the mountains will be like carded wool

###### 101:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ</div>

> Then as for him whose balance (of good deeds) will be heavy

###### 101:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةࣲ رَّاضِيَةࣲ‏</div>

> He will live a pleasant life (in Paradise)

###### 101:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ</div>

> But as for him whose balance (of good deeds) will be light

###### 101:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةࣱ‏</div>

> He will have his home in Hawiyah (pit, i.e. Hell)

###### 101:10
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهۡ</div>

> And what will make you know what it is

###### 101:11
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَارٌ حَامِيَةُۢ</div>

> (It is) a hot blazing Fire
