###### 102:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ</div>

> The mutual rivalry for piling up of worldly things diverts you

###### 102:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">حَتَّىٰ زُرۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَقَابِرَ</div>

> Until you visit the graves (i.e. till you die)

###### 102:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Nay! You shall come to know

###### 102:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ</div>

> Again, Nay! You shall come to know

###### 102:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّا لَوۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ عِلۡمَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ</div>

> Nay! If you knew with a sure knowledge (the end result of piling up, you would not have occupied yourselves in worldly things)

###### 102:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ</div>

> Verily, You shall see the blazing Fire (Hell)

###### 102:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيۡنَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ</div>

> And again, you shall see it with certainty of sight

###### 102:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ثُمَّ لَتُسۡـَٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ</div>

> Then, on that Day, you shall be asked about the delight (you indulged in, in this world)
