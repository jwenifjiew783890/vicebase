###### 104:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيۡلࣱ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةࣲ لُّمَزَةٍ</div>

> Woe to every slanderer and backbiter

###### 104:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالࣰ ا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ</div>

> Who has gathered wealth and counted it

###### 104:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">يَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخۡلَدَهُۥ</div>

> He thinks that his wealth will make him last forever

###### 104:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">كَلَّاۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِي ٱلۡحُطَمَةِ</div>

> Nay! Verily, he will be thrown into the crushing Fire

###### 104:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحُطَمَةُ</div>

> And what will make you know what the crushing Fire is

###### 104:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُوقَدَةُ</div>

> The fire of Allah, kindled

###### 104:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةِ</div>

> Which leaps up over the hearts

###### 104:8
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِنَّهَا عَلَيۡهِم مُّؤۡصَدَةࣱ‏</div>

> Verily, it shall be closed in on them

###### 104:9
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي عَمَدࣲ مُّمَدَّدَةِۭ</div>

> In pillars stretched forth (i.e. they will be punished in the Fire with pillars, etc)
