###### 105:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡفِيلِ</div>

> Have you (O Muhammad (Peace be upon him)) not seen how your Lord dealt with the Owners of the Elephant? [The elephant army which came from Yemen under the command of Abrahah Al-Ashram intending to destroy the Ka'bah at Makkah]

###### 105:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡ كَيۡدَهُمۡ فِي تَضۡلِيلࣲ‏</div>

> Did He not make their plot go astray

###### 105:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَأَرۡسَلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ طَيۡرًا أَبَابِيلَ</div>

> And sent against them birds, in flocks

###### 105:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةࣲ مِّن سِجِّيلࣲ‏</div>

> Striking them with stones of Sijjil

###### 105:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَجَعَلَهُمۡ كَعَصۡفࣲ مَّأۡكُولِۭ</div>

> And made them like an empty field of stalks (of which the corn has been eaten up by cattle)
