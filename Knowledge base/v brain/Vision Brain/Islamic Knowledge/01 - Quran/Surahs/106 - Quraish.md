###### 106:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">لِإِيلَٰفِ قُرَيۡشٍ</div>

> (It is a great Grace and Protection from Allah), for the taming of the Quraish

###### 106:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِۦلَٰفِهِمۡ رِحۡلَةَ ٱلشِّتَآءِ وَٱلصَّيۡفِ</div>

> (And with all those Allah's Grace and Protections for their taming, We cause) the (Quraish) caravans to set forth safe in winter (to the south), and in summer (to the north without any fear)

###### 106:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَلۡيَعۡبُدُواْ رَبَّ هَٰذَا ٱلۡبَيۡتِ</div>

> So let them worship (Allah) the Lord of this House (the Ka'bah in Makkah)

###### 106:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِيٓ أَطۡعَمَهُم مِّن جُوعࣲ وَءَامَنَهُم مِّنۡ خَوۡفِۭ</div>

> (He) Who has fed them against hunger, and has made them safe from fear
