###### 107:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ</div>

> Have you seen him who denies the Recompense

###### 107:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يَدُعُّ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ</div>

> That is he who repulses the orphan (harshly)

###### 107:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ</div>

> And urges not the feeding of AlMiskin (the poor)

###### 107:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فَوَيۡلࣱ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ</div>

> So woe unto those performers of Salat (prayers) (hypocrites)

###### 107:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَن صَلَاتِهِمۡ سَاهُونَ</div>

> Who delay their Salat (prayer) from their stated fixed times

###### 107:6
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ يُرَآءُونَ</div>

> Those who do good deeds only to be seen (of men)

###### 107:7
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَيَمۡنَعُونَ ٱلۡمَاعُونَ</div>

> And refuse Al-Ma'un (small kindnesses e.g. salt, sugar, water, etc)
