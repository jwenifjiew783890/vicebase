<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَآ أَغۡنَىٰ عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ</div>

> He will be burnt in a Fire of blazing flames

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَٱمۡرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلۡحَطَبِ</div>

> And his wife too, who carries wood (thorns of Sadan which she used to put on the way of the Prophet (Peace be upon him), or use to slander him

###### 111:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">فِي جِيدِهَا حَبۡلࣱ مِّن مَّسَدِۭ</div>
