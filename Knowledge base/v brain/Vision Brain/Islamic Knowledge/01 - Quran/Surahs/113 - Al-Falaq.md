###### 113:1
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡفَلَقِ</div>

> Say: "I seek refuge with (Allah) the Lord of the daybreak

###### 113:2
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ</div>

> From the evil of what He has created

###### 113:3
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ</div>

> And from the evil of the darkening (night) as it comes with its darkness; (or the moon as it sets or goes away)

###### 113:4
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلۡعُقَدِ</div>

> And from the evil of the witchcrafts when they blow in the knots

###### 113:5
<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ</div>

> And from the evil of the envier when he envies
