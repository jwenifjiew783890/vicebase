###### 114:1

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ</div>

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size:1.35em; line-height:2.1">إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ</div>

> The Ilah (God) of mankind
