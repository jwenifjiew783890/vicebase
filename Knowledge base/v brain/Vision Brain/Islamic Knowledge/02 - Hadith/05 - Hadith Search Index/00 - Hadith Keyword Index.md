# Hadith Keyword Index

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] › [[Islamic Knowledge/00 - Index|Islamic Knowledge]] › [[Islamic Knowledge/02 - Hadith/00 - Hadith Index|Hadith]]

> [!danger] Why this exists — a real retrieval failure it prevents
> Vision once searched the Arabic for `سفر المرأة بغير محرم`, found nothing, and concluded
> **no hadith existed** — while Sahih al-Bukhari 1086 and Sahih Muslim both carry the
> governing narration. The corpus Arabic is **fully diacritised**
> (*لاَ تُسَافِرِ الْمَرْأَةُ ... إِلاَّ مَعَ ذِي مَحْرَمٍ*), so an undiacritised search string
> never matches. **Do not conclude a hadith is absent from a failed Arabic search.**
> Use this index, which matches on the English translation.

> Matches are literal and mechanical. A hit means the phrase occurs, not that the
> narration is primarily about the topic. Read it in its book note, and check the grading
> **with the grader named**.
