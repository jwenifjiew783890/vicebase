> [!info] The size difference between scholars is a licence artefact
> Ibn Baz's site grants reproduction with attribution; the others do not. The corpus is full where permission is explicit and thin where it is not — that is the law showing through, not uneven effort.

> [!danger] Never state what a scholar said from an unverified record
> The Ibn Uthaymeen register holds **locators only**. Summarising those fatwas from search snippets is forbidden — see [[Islamic Knowledge/99 - Source & Authenticity Rules|Source Rules §11, §21]].

## Evidence labelling

A fatwa is **C** (verified scholar statement). Applying it to a user's circumstances is **D** (inference). A source we can only point at is **F**.
