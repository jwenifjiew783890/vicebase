# Ibn Uthaymeen — Source Register

**Up:** [[Vision Brain]] › [[Islamic Knowledge/00 - Index|Islamic Knowledge]] ›

> [!danger] Nothing here is a verified quotation of the Shaykh
> This is a **locator register**, not a body of his statements. No text of his has been
> imported, and the titles below could **not** be confirmed against the pages themselves.
> Treat every row as **`NEEDS VERIFICATION`** and **evidence label F** until a human opens the
> URL and reads it.

## Two independent blockers

**1 — Licence.** The site footer reads *"جميع الحقوق محفوظة © لمؤسسة الشيخ محمد بن صالح
العثيمين الخيرية"* — **all rights reserved, with no grant of reproduction**. Its `robots.txt`
additionally carries Cloudflare content signals and an express reservation of rights under
**Article 4 of EU Directive 2019/790**. Full text may therefore **not** be stored, even if it
could be read.

*(Contrast [[Islamic Knowledge/08 - Fatawa/Ibn Baz/00 - Ibn Baz Fatawa Index|Ibn Baz]], whose
site explicitly permits transmission with attribution — which is why his fatwas **are** stored
in full.)*

**2 — The pages are JavaScript-rendered.** Verified 2026-09-03: an HTTP fetch of
`binothaimeen.net/content/<id>` returns only the site shell. The `<title>` element contains
just the site name; the fatwa title and body are loaded client-side. So the content **cannot be
verified programmatically** by this system at all.

## What Vision may and may not do with this

- **May**: tell the user these sources exist and give the URL so they can read them.
- **May not**: state what the Shaykh said, summarise these fatwas, or quote them. Doing so would
  be reconstructing his words from a search snippet — exactly what
  [[Islamic Knowledge/99 - Source & Authenticity Rules|Source Rules §11 and §21]] forbid.

## To promote a row to a real source record

Open the URL in a browser, read the actual title and text, then create a note under this folder
recording title, collection, date and topic — **metadata and permitted excerpts only**, never
the full text, because of blocker 1.
