# Al-Albani

Shaykh **Muhammad Nasir al-Din al-Albani** (d. 1420 AH / 1999 CE), the muhaddith whose
hadith gradings are the most widely referenced in contemporary Salafi study.

## What this library actually holds of his work

**19,081 hadith gradings** attributed to him by name in the
imported dataset, spread across:

| Collection | Gradings by Al-Albani |
| --- | ---: |
| Sunan an-Nasa'i | 5,727 |
| Sunan Abi Dawud | 5,252 |
| Sunan Ibn Majah | 4,308 |
| Jami' al-Tirmidhi | 3,794 |

> [!danger] Attribution limits
> A grading is shown as Al-Albani's **only** where the source dataset names him. This
> library never infers his opinion, never generalises one ruling to another narration,
> and never attaches his name to a collection he did not grade. Note that Sahih
> al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim carry **no** Al-Albani gradings here.

> **not** in this library. Their copyright status was not verified, so nothing was
