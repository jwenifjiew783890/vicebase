# 07 - Other Salafi Scholars

Candidates associated with the Ahl al-Hadith / Salafi corpus. **None has material imported.**
This note exists so that additions are deliberate rather than accumulated.

> [!danger] Inclusion is not automatic
> Before a scholar is added, four things must hold: attribution can be evidenced, primary
> material is actually available, the source is reliable, and the material is legally
> usable. A name being well known is not sufficient.
