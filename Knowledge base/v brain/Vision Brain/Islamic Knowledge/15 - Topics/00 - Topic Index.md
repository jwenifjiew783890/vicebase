# 15 - Topics

Cross-cutting entry points into the corpus. Each topic note lists narrations found by
**literal keyword search** over the imported English translations.

> [!warning] A finding aid, not a scholarly thematic index
> Keyword matching is mechanical. It over-matches (a narration mentioning prayer in passing
> appears under Salah) and under-matches (it misses anything phrased differently, and it
> does not search the Arabic at all). Use it to locate candidates, then read them in place.
